FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions About Placements & Careers?

Clear, straightforward answers about eligibility, interview opportunities, and our support scope.

❓ Who qualifies for placement assistance?

Students enrolled in our Tier A Career Programs (6–9 months) and Tier B Professional Programs (3–5 months) who satisfy all six published academic eligibility requirements qualify for active placement assistance and referral drives.

❓ When does placement support become active?

Placement support begins in the final phase of your course after completing core syllabus modules, submitting your capstone projects, and receiving mentor approval on your ATS resume and public portfolio.

❓ What projects must I complete before receiving referrals?

You must complete all mandatory production capstones assigned in your curriculum. For technical programs, projects must be pushed to a live GitHub repository; for design programs, case studies must be published on Behance or a personal portfolio.

❓ How are employer referrals handled?

When regional agencies, startups, or corporate recruiters notify our admissions and placement desk of active vacancies, we notify eligible students and share approved resumes with explicit student consent. Employers then shortlist candidates independently.

❓ Are interview calls guaranteed?

No. While Nova Skills forwards eligible candidate profiles directly to hiring connections, all interview shortlisting decisions are made exclusively by employers based on their technical assessments and vacancy criteria.

❓ Is a job offer or specific salary package guaranteed?

No. In accordance with Section 8 of our Terms & Conditions, Nova Skills does not guarantee employment, job offers, or specific salary amounts. Hiring and remuneration decisions rest solely with hiring companies.

❓ Do all 109 courses include placement support?

No. Short modular certification courses (3–8 weeks) focus on skill mastery and provide practical portfolio projects and completion certificates, but do not include dedicated corporate placement drives. Kids Tech courses strictly exclude placement marketing.

❓ What happens if I do not meet eligibility requirements on the first attempt?

Mentors provide detailed actionable feedback on your capstones, assessments, or mock interviews. You may revise your deliverables and resubmit them for mentor re-evaluation to unlock placement eligibility.

❓ How can I speak to a career counsellor about my options?

You can book a free 1-on-1 career counselling session using the button below or call our official admissions helpline at +91 9695904440.
