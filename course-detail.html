Refund & Cancellation Policy

Review our course cancellation, refund eligibility, cooling-off conditions, and non-refundable situations before proceeding with enrolment.

🕒

Standard Cancellation (≥ 7 Days)

Written cancellation received 7+ calendar days before batch start qualifies for 100% Tuition Fee refund (minus nominal administrative registration deduction, capped at ₹1,000 or 10%, whichever is lower).

🛡️

48-Hour Cooling-Off Window

For enrolments completed within 7 days of batch start, students receive a 48-hour cooling-off period (90% refund of fees paid), provided the first live class or classroom session has not yet commenced.

⚠️

Non-Refundable Situations

Under Section 4 of our policy, fees are strictly non-refundable once classes commence, LMS credentials / study materials are accessed, or for promotional / discounted / scholarship enrolments.

🏛️

Institute Cancellation Guarantee

If Nova Skills cancels or reschedules a batch by > 14 days without mutual agreement, students are entitled to a 100% full refund with zero deductions, or free priority batch transfer.

Formal refund requests must be emailed to novaskills.official@gmail.com. Processing timeline: 7–10 business days upon approval.
