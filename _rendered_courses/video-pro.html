STEP-BY-STEP MODULES

Detailed Curriculum & Labs

Module 01 Premiere Pro Fundamentals & The Rough Cut 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Master the Premiere Pro interface and the foundational techniques of non-linear editing.
  • Project setup and media management
  • Timeline mechanics (insert, overwrite, replace)
  • Trimming tools (ripple, roll, slip, slide)
  • Text-based editing
Key Concepts: ProxiesAssemblyRipple EditJ/L Cuts
1.1 Workspace & Ingest Strategy
Taught: Interface layout, standard folder structures, importing media, and creating proxies.
Learner Lab: Organizes provided raw footage and creates a proxy workflow for 4K media.
Why It Matters: A messy project leads to missing files and extremely slow editing.
Expected Deliverable: An optimized, organized Premiere Pro project.
1.2 The Assembly Edit
Taught: Source monitor usage, setting In/Out points, and laying down the A-roll spine.
Learner Lab: Assembles a 2-minute interview sequence from raw takes.
Why It Matters: The first step in crafting the narrative structure without worrying about polish.
Expected Deliverable: A cohesive, logical rough cut of an interview.
1.3 Advanced Trimming & Pacing
Taught: Using the ripple edit, roll edit, and slip tools to tighten the edit.
Learner Lab: Tightens the interview cut, removing dead space and awkward pauses.
Why It Matters: Pacing dictates the emotion of the scene; these tools make adjusting pace fast.
Expected Deliverable: A fast-paced, engaging sequence.
1.4 J & L Cuts for Seamless Flow
Taught: Detaching audio and video to create overlapping edits (J/L cuts).
Learner Lab: Applies J and L cuts to a two-person dialogue scene.
Why It Matters: Hides the cut and makes conversations feel natural rather than rigid.
Expected Deliverable: A smooth, cinematic dialogue edit.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Edit a narrative dialogue scene from provided short film footage.
📝 Practical Assignment: Deliverable: 1-minute narrative dialogue edit.
💼 Commercial Application: Cutting narrative films, commercials, and YouTube content.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Solid grasp of timeline mechanics and storytelling pacing.
Module 02 B-Roll, Transitions & Visual Effects 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Enhance the story using supplementary footage, dynamic transitions, and basic compositing.
  • B-roll integration
  • Speed ramping
  • Keyframing effects and motion
  • Dynamic Link with After Effects
Key Concepts: B-RollSpeed RampingMOGRTsUltra Key
2.1 Mastering B-Roll & Visual Storytelling
Taught: Layering video on V2/V3 to cover jump cuts and illustrate the narrative.
Learner Lab: Adds B-roll to the previously edited interview sequence.
Why It Matters: B-roll keeps the viewer engaged and visually explains the A-roll.
Expected Deliverable: A visually dynamic documentary-style sequence.
2.2 Speed Ramping & Time Remapping
Taught: Keyframing speed to create slow-motion to fast-motion transitions.
Learner Lab: Creates a stylized montage using 60fps/120fps footage.
Why It Matters: A highly popular technique in sports, automotive, and music video editing.
Expected Deliverable: A high-energy, speed-ramped promo sequence.
2.3 Motion Graphics & Essential Graphics Panel
Taught: Using MOGRTs (Motion Graphics Templates) for titles and lower thirds.
Learner Lab: Customizes and applies lower thirds and a title sequence.
Why It Matters: Adds professional branding and context without leaving Premiere.
Expected Deliverable: A branded video ready for broadcast or YouTube.
2.4 Basic Compositing & Green Screen
Taught: Using Ultra Key, masking, and tracking basic objects.
Learner Lab: Keys out a green screen subject and places them in a new environment.
Why It Matters: Essential for corporate shoots, gaming videos, and basic VFX.
Expected Deliverable: A clean, believable green screen composite.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Edit a high-energy promotional montage with speed ramps and titles.
📝 Practical Assignment: Deliverable: 30-second promotional montage video.
💼 Commercial Application: Product promos, music videos, and social media ads.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Ability to craft visually engaging sequences beyond simple cuts.
Module 03 Audio Mixing & Sound Design 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Achieve broadcast-quality sound by mastering dialogue, music, and sound effects.
  • Audio workspace and track mixer
  • Dialogue cleanup (EQ and Compression)
  • Sound design and Foley
  • Audition round-tripping
Key Concepts: Audio LevelsCompressionSound DesignNoise Reduction
3.1 The Audio Track Mixer & Essential Sound
Taught: Reading waveforms, setting levels, and using the Essential Sound panel.
Learner Lab: Balances the audio levels of dialogue, music, and SFX tracks.
Why It Matters: Bad audio ruins good video; proper levels are non-negotiable.
Expected Deliverable: A balanced mix peaking at appropriate broadcast levels.
3.2 Dialogue Processing: EQ & Compression
Taught: Removing rumble/hiss using EQ, and evening out volume using dynamics compression.
Learner Lab: Processes raw, noisy dialogue into a crisp, clean voiceover.
Why It Matters: Makes voices sound rich, clear, and professional, like a podcast or broadcast.
Expected Deliverable: Broadcast-ready dialogue audio.
3.3 Sound Design: Building the World
Taught: Layering ambient sound, Foley, whooshes, and hits.
Learner Lab: Builds a complete soundscape for a silent cinematic sequence.
Why It Matters: Sound design creates immersion and sells the visual edits.
Expected Deliverable: A rich, immersive audio experience.
3.4 Advanced Cleanup in Adobe Audition
Taught: Sending clips to Audition, using the spectral frequency display, and noise reduction.
Learner Lab: Removes a siren and background hum from an interview clip.
Why It Matters: Audition provides surgical tools for rescuing bad audio that Premiere cannot fix.
Expected Deliverable: Perfectly restored audio.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Perform a complete sound design and mix pass on a provided short film scene.
📝 Practical Assignment: Deliverable: Final mixed audio sequence.
💼 Commercial Application: Podcast editing, film mixing, and commercial audio finishing.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Proficiency in turning raw audio into a professional mix.
Module 04 Color Correction & Grading (Lumetri & Resolve) 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Transform the visual tone of your footage using industry-standard color workflows.
  • Color theory and scopes
  • Primary color correction
  • Secondary grading and LUTs
  • Intro to DaVinci Resolve nodes
Key Concepts: ScopesLUTsHSL QualifiersNode Workflow
4.1 Reading Scopes & Primary Correction
Taught: Using Waveforms and Vectorscopes to balance exposure and white balance.
Learner Lab: Neutralizes and matches multiple mismatched shots in a sequence.
Why It Matters: You cannot trust your eyes or monitor alone; scopes provide objective data.
Expected Deliverable: A perfectly color-corrected, matched sequence.
4.2 Color Grading & Cinematic Looks in Premiere
Taught: Using Lumetri Color curves, color wheels, and applying/modifying LUTs.
Learner Lab: Applies a 'teal and orange' blockbuster grade to a scene.
Why It Matters: Grading establishes the mood and cinematic feel of the video.
Expected Deliverable: A stylized, cinematic video sequence.
4.3 Secondary Grading: Skin Tones & Qualifiers
Taught: Isolating specific colors (like skin or skies) using HSL qualifiers and masks.
Learner Lab: Isolates and perfects skin tones while changing the background color.
Why It Matters: Ensures human subjects look natural even under heavy stylized grades.
Expected Deliverable: A professional grade with flawless skin tones.
4.4 Introduction to DaVinci Resolve
Taught: XML round-tripping from Premiere, node-based grading, and power windows.
Learner Lab: Exports an XML from Premiere, grades it using nodes in Resolve, and renders it back.
Why It Matters: Resolve is the industry standard for high-end color grading.
Expected Deliverable: A high-end color graded sequence utilizing Resolve's power.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Color correct and grade a music video sequence.
📝 Practical Assignment: Deliverable: Before/After color graded sequence.
💼 Commercial Application: Freelance color grading and finishing high-end commercial projects.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Ability to confidently correct and grade raw/log footage.
