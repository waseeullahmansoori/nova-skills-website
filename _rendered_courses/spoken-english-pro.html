STEP-BY-STEP MODULES

Detailed Curriculum & Labs

1 Pronunciation & Phonetics 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Master English sounds to ensure clear articulation.
  • Vowel and consonant sounds.
  • Word stress.
  • Syllable structure.
Key Concepts: PhonemesWord StressConnected SpeechIntonation
1 The English Sound System
Taught: Introduction to the IPA (International Phonetic Alphabet) and tricky sounds.
Learner Lab: Practice difficult sound pairs (e.g., v/w, p/b).
Why It Matters: Forms the basis of clear speech.
Expected Deliverable: Accurate production of individual sounds.
2 Word Stress Rules
Taught: Identifying which syllables to stress in multi-syllable words.
Learner Lab: Read lists of words applying correct stress patterns.
Why It Matters: Incorrect stress makes words incomprehensible.
Expected Deliverable: Natural-sounding word pronunciation.
3 Connected Speech
Taught: Linking words, elision, and assimilation.
Learner Lab: Practice reading sentences focusing on linking sounds.
Why It Matters: Helps you sound more like a native speaker and improves listening comprehension.
Expected Deliverable: Smoother transitions between words.
4 Intonation Patterns
Taught: Rising and falling pitch for questions, statements, and emotion.
Learner Lab: Record sentences using different intonation patterns.
Why It Matters: Conveys meaning and attitude beyond the words themselves.
Expected Deliverable: Expressive and meaningful speech delivery.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Reading texts into transcription software to test clarity.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit a 2-minute audio recording reading a provided text.
💼 Commercial Application: Being clearly understood on phone calls and virtual meetings.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Significantly improved pronunciation clarity.
2 Fluency Building 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Develop the ability to speak continuously without hesitation.
  • Thinking in English.
  • Using fillers naturally.
  • Expanding vocabulary for fluid speech.
Key Concepts: Direct AssociationFillersNarrative FlowParaphrasing
1 Stopping Mental Translation
Taught: Techniques to associate English words directly with concepts.
Learner Lab: Rapid-fire picture description exercises.
Why It Matters: Translating causes unnatural pauses and awkward phrasing.
Expected Deliverable: Faster response times when speaking.
2 Natural Fillers and Hesitation Devices
Taught: Using phrases like 'Well...', 'You see...', 'Let me think about that...'
Learner Lab: Practice answering complex questions using natural fillers.
Why It Matters: Buys time to think without awkward silence.
Expected Deliverable: Smoother handling of difficult questions.
3 Storytelling Basics
Taught: Structuring a short anecdote (beginning, middle, end).
Learner Lab: Tell a 2-minute personal story focusing on flow.
Why It Matters: Stories are powerful communication tools in any setting.
Expected Deliverable: Ability to recount events clearly.
4 Paraphrasing and Circumlocution
Taught: How to explain a word you don't know.
Learner Lab: Describe specific objects or concepts without using the target word.
Why It Matters: Prevents conversation breakdown when vocabulary fails.
Expected Deliverable: Resourcefulness in conversation.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Impromptu speaking drills.
📝 Practical Assignment: Record a 3-minute video telling a professional success story.
💼 Commercial Application: Engaging colleagues and clients with fluid conversation.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Reduced hesitation and improved speaking speed.
3 Conversational English & Small Talk 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Master everyday interactions and professional networking.
  • Starting and ending conversations.
  • Networking small talk.
  • Expressing opinions.
Key Concepts: Small Talk TopicsTransitionsDiplomatic LanguageBack-channeling
1 The Art of Small Talk
Taught: Safe topics, asking open-ended questions, and keeping a conversation going.
Learner Lab: Role-play networking at a corporate event.
Why It Matters: Builds rapport before getting to business.
Expected Deliverable: Confidence in casual professional settings.
2 Entering and Exiting Conversations
Taught: Polite phrases to join a group or leave a discussion.
Learner Lab: Practice transition phrases in group scenarios.
Why It Matters: Prevents awkwardness at networking events.
Expected Deliverable: Smooth navigation of social dynamics.
3 Expressing Opinions and Agreeing/Disagreeing
Taught: Language to share thoughts politely and constructively.
Learner Lab: Debate a light topic (e.g., remote work vs. office).
Why It Matters: Essential for collaboration and team discussions.
Expected Deliverable: Ability to voice thoughts clearly.
4 Active Listening Responses
Taught: Using back-channeling (e.g., 'Right', 'I see') to show engagement.
Learner Lab: Practice active listening in pairs while the partner speaks.
Why It Matters: Makes the speaker feel heard and encourages dialogue.
Expected Deliverable: Better interpersonal communication skills.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Live networking simulations.
📝 Practical Assignment: Participate in a recorded 10-minute unstructured group conversation.
💼 Commercial Application: Building strong relationships with clients and colleagues.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Comfortable and natural conversational skills.
4 Interview English 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Prepare specifically for job interviews in English.
  • Answering common questions.
  • The STAR method.
  • Asking questions to the interviewer.
Key Concepts: Elevator PitchSTAR MethodSelf-AwarenessClosing Techniques
1 The 'Tell Me About Yourself' Pitch
Taught: Structuring a professional summary.
Learner Lab: Draft and deliver a 90-second personal pitch.
Why It Matters: Sets the tone for the entire interview.
Expected Deliverable: A polished professional introduction.
2 Behavioral Questions & The STAR Method
Taught: Situation, Task, Action, Result framework for answering situational questions.
Learner Lab: Answer 'Tell me about a time when...' questions using STAR.
Why It Matters: Provides clear, concise, and evidence-based answers.
Expected Deliverable: Structured and impactful interview answers.
3 Discussing Strengths and Weaknesses
Taught: How to frame skills confidently and weaknesses positively.
Learner Lab: Role-play discussing areas for improvement.
Why It Matters: Demonstrates self-awareness and professionalism.
Expected Deliverable: Tactful handling of difficult questions.
4 Asking Questions and Closing
Taught: Smart questions to ask the employer and how to end the interview strongly.
Learner Lab: Formulate 3 specific questions for a mock company.
Why It Matters: Shows engagement and interest in the role.
Expected Deliverable: Professional interview conclusion skills.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Mock interview sessions with peer feedback.
📝 Practical Assignment: Complete a 15-minute recorded mock interview with an instructor.
💼 Commercial Application: Securing job offers in English-speaking environments.
🎯 Expected Outcome: High confidence and competence in job interviews.
