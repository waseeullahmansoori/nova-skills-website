This course is specifically engineered for D2C founders, Shopify store owners, performance marketers, digital marketing professionals, agency growth managers, and freelancers who want to specialize in high-growth online retail marketing.

Learners should have basic digital marketing literacy, familiarity with web browsing, spreadsheets, and commercial business terminology. Prior exposure to Google Ads, Meta Ads, or basic store operations is helpful but not required. No coding or web development experience is necessary.

The AI Digital Marketing Specialist (3 Months, 120h, ₹24,900) is a foundational execution program covering general SEO, social, and search ads. The AI Digital Marketing Strategist (6 Months, 240h, ₹44,900) is an executive career mastery in enterprise omnichannel strategy, technical SEO/GEO, and marketing automation. The E-Commerce & Growth Marketing Specialist (4 Months, 160h, ₹34,900) is an intensive, specialized vertical program focused 100% on online retail: Shopify store architecture, Google Merchant Center feed diagnostics, Google Shopping & PMax, Meta Advantage+ Catalog ads, CRO, Klaviyo retention, and e-commerce unit economics.

The program spans 4 Months (16 Weeks) comprising exactly 160 guided training hours led by industry instructors, plus an estimated 60-80 recommended hours for independent store building, feed diagnostics, and project development.
