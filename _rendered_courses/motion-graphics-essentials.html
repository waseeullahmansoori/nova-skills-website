STEP-BY-STEP MODULES

Detailed Curriculum & Labs

Module 01 Vector Prep & Basic Keyframing 3 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Bridge the gap between Illustrator and After Effects.
  • Illustrator layer prep
  • AE interface and composition settings
  • Transform properties and anchor points
  • Basic keyframing and easing
Key Concepts: Layer PrepAnchor PointsEasing
1.1 The AI to AE Workflow
Taught: Releasing layers in Illustrator and importing as a composition.
Learner Lab: Prepares a complex vector logo and imports it correctly.
Why It Matters: If you don't prep the file, you can't animate the individual parts.
Expected Deliverable: A ready-to-animate AE project.
1.2 Keyframes & The Graph Editor
Taught: Position, Scale, Rotation keyframes, and using the speed graph.
Learner Lab: Animates a bouncing ball and a swinging pendulum.
Why It Matters: Easing creates the illusion of weight and physics.
Expected Deliverable: Understanding of motion physics.
1.3 Animating an Icon Set
Taught: Applying basic principles (anticipation, overshoot) to UI icons.
Learner Lab: Animates a 'Like' heart and a 'Send' paper airplane.
Why It Matters: Micro-interactions are huge in web design.
Expected Deliverable: Two professional web animations.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Prep and animate a 3-piece icon set.
📝 Practical Assignment: Deliverable: Animated icon set (MP4).
💼 Commercial Application: Web design micro-interactions.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Basic mastery of 2D keyframing.
Module 02 Shape Layers & The Brand Package 3 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Master AE's native vector tools to build a complete animated brand identity.
  • Shape layer modifiers
  • Track mattes
  • Text animators
  • Exporting with Alpha channels
Key Concepts: Trim PathsAlpha MattesExport Formats
2.1 Trim Paths & Shape Layers
Taught: Converting vectors to shape layers and using Trim Paths.
Learner Lab: Animates a logo drawing itself on screen.
Why It Matters: The standard technique for drawing on lines and animating signatures.
Expected Deliverable: A sleek, modern logo reveal.
2.2 Track Mattes & Text
Taught: Using shapes to mask text (alpha mattes) and basic text animators.
Learner Lab: Builds an animated lower-third name graphic.
Why It Matters: Essential for clean, broadcast-style typography.
Expected Deliverable: A professional lower third.
2.3 Exporting for Delivery
Taught: Exporting MP4s, ProRes with Alpha (transparency), and intro to Bodymovin/Lottie.
Learner Lab: Compiles their logo and lower third into a final package and exports.
Why It Matters: You must know how to hand off files to video editors or web developers.
Expected Deliverable: A finalized, client-ready brand package.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Complete the final logo animation and lower third.
📝 Practical Assignment: Deliverable: Final Animated Brand Package.
💼 Commercial Application: Branding packages for corporate clients.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Ability to deliver professional motion graphics assets.
