CURRICULUM ARCHITECTURE

Multi-Stage Learning Journey

STEP-BY-STEP MODULES

Detailed Curriculum & Labs

1 Local Search Fundamentals & Strategy 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Understand local search intent, Google's local ecosystem, and evaluate businesses based on Google's core local ranking factors: Relevance, Distance, and Prominence.
Key Topics Covered (10):
  • Introduction to Local SEO & business value: how local search differs from traditional website SEO; consumer local search behavior
  • Local intent and commercial queries: 'near me' searches, geo-modified queries (e.g., 'dentist in Indiranagar'), and intent classification
  • Google Search local results & Google Maps ecosystem: understanding the Local Pack (3-Pack), Knowledge Panels, and Map search surfaces
  • Google's core local ranking considerations: Relevance (information match), Distance (geographic proximity to searcher), and Prominence (online authority and reviews)
  • Why rankings cannot be guaranteed: geographic variability, personalized results, and why paying Google cannot guarantee organic Map Pack rankings
  • Mobile local search behavior: click-to-call actions, directions requests, and the local customer decision journey
  • Business operating models: Storefront businesses (physical customer visits) versus Service-Area Businesses (SABs - travel to customers)
  • Local competitor benchmarking: auditing local pack competitors, category selections, review volume, and local visibility gaps
  • Local keyword research: identifying localized service terms, geographic modifiers, and search volume patterns
  • Local SEO KPI framework: setting measurable objectives for profile views, call clicks, direction requests, and website visits
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Conducting a local search audit across 3 competitors in a chosen city and niche, analyzing Local Pack results, identifying ranking variations by proximity, and compiling a competitor gap analysis.
📝 Practical Assignment: Local Search Opportunity Assessment (Google Sheets / PDF) evaluating a local business's market positioning, target audience, local keyword opportunities, competitor benchmarks, and initial KPI framework.
2 Google Business Profile Management & Optimization 5 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Master end-to-end Google Business Profile configuration, verification procedures, category strategy, attribute management, and compliance with Google's representation guidelines.
Key Topics Covered (11):
  • Google Business Profile fundamentals & eligibility guidelines: eligible vs. ineligible businesses; storefronts, service-area businesses (SABs), and hybrid models; restrictions on online-only businesses and virtual offices
  • Creating, claiming, and verifying profiles: verification methods (video verification, postcard, phone/SMS, email), verification availability, and ownership dispute procedures
  • Account access, governance, and permissions: Owner (Primary/Additional) vs. Manager permissions; safe agency access without sharing personal Google passwords
  • Business information accuracy & name guidelines: strict prohibition of keyword stuffing in business names; matching real-world signage and registration
  • Category selection strategy: primary category selection (the single highest-impact field) and choosing relevant secondary categories
  • Business description & services configuration: writing an engaging 750-character business description; setting up custom services and product catalogs where supported
  • Address & service-area management: physical address formatting; properly hiding addresses for service-area businesses to protect privacy and comply with guidelines; defining realistic service radiuses
  • Operating hours & special hours: regular hours, holiday hours, special open/closed statuses, and 'More hours' (delivery, takeout, happy hour)
  • Contact information & links: local landline vs. mobile phone numbers; primary website link and appointment URL integration
  • Visual assets & ongoing engagement: logo, cover photos, interior/exterior imagery, team photos, business updates/posts, and profile completeness audits
  • Profile edit approval, suspension risks, and appeal procedures: identifying triggers for suspensions (address edits, name spam), duplicate profile diagnosis, and Google Business Profile quality policies
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Executing a complete profile audit for an authorized business (or documented simulation), drafting an optimized business description, selecting primary/secondary categories, and configuring service lists.
📝 Practical Assignment: Comprehensive Google Business Profile Optimization Plan (Google Sheets / PDF) with eligibility audit, category selection rationale, services configuration, visual asset checklist, and 30-day maintenance calendar.
3 Local Website SEO & Location Strategy 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Optimize website landing pages for local search relevance, enforce NAP consistency, structure localized content, and implement LocalBusiness schema concepts.
Key Topics Covered (10):
  • Local website SEO fundamentals: how the website supports Google Business Profile rankings and organic local search visibility
  • NAP consistency (Name, Address, Phone): ensuring exact character-by-character parity between website footer, contact pages, and Google Business Profile
  • Local keyword research: identifying city-based keyword intent, neighborhood modifiers, and service-specific local searches
  • Location landing-page strategy: creating dedicated location pages for multi-branch businesses vs. service-area pages; search intent and page relevance
  • Avoiding low-quality doorway pages: why creating dozens of identical city pages harms site quality; ensuring unique local value on every landing page
  • Local landing-page content architecture: localized H1/H2 headings, localized service descriptions, local client testimonials, embedded Google Maps, directions, and prominent click-to-call CTAs
  • Internal linking architecture for local pages: linking branch pages from main navigation, breadcrumbs, and cross-linking adjacent service areas
  • Structured data fundamentals: LocalBusiness schema concepts; defining @type (e.g., Dentist, Restaurant, Plumber), name, address, telephone, openingHoursSpecification, and geo coordinates
  • Google Search Console for local landing pages: tracking local query impressions, click performance, and monitoring page indexing status
  • Mobile user experience for local visitors: fast page loads, sticky tap-to-call buttons, easy driving directions, and local accessibility
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Designing a comprehensive local landing-page brief for a multi-location or service-area business, structuring unique localized content sections, and drafting LocalBusiness schema JSON-LD.
📝 Practical Assignment: Location-Focused Website Content Strategy & Landing-Page Brief (Google Docs / PDF) detailing target keywords, localized copy hierarchy, NAP placement, CTA workflows, internal linking, and LocalBusiness schema markup.
4 Reviews, Reputation & Customer Trust 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Design ethical, policy-compliant customer review acquisition workflows, manage online reputation, write professional response templates, and handle complaints constructive.
Key Topics Covered (12):
  • Online reputation fundamentals: how customer reviews impact local consumer trust, click-through rates, and Google's Prominence ranking signal
  • Google review policies & strict prohibited practices: the severe legal and algorithmic penalties for buying reviews, fake reviews, or review gating
  • Review gating awareness: why filtering unhappy customers before they reach Google reviews violates Google policy and damages authenticity
  • Legitimate review collection workflows: creating short Google review links and custom QR codes; timing review requests post-service or at point of sale
  • Review invitation messaging: crafting neutral, unbiased customer request messages across email, WhatsApp, SMS, and printed receipts
  • Professional review response strategy: why responding to all reviews (positive, neutral, negative) signals active management to both Google and customers
  • Responding to positive & neutral feedback: personalized gratitude, reinforcing key services naturally, and avoiding repetitive boilerplate text
  • Negative review management & complaint resolution: de-escalating customer frustration, moving conversations offline, professional apologies, and customer service escalation paths
  • Privacy and confidentiality in responses: strict rules against disclosing customer personal data, medical details, or transaction disputes in public responses
  • Flagging and reporting policy-violating reviews: understanding grounds for removal (conflict of interest, harassment, spam, wrong business) and navigating Google's review management tool
  • Review sentiment analysis & business improvement: categorizing feedback to identify recurring operational, staff, or service delivery bottlenecks
  • AI-assisted response drafting: utilizing ChatGPT/Gemini for drafting polite response variations, followed by compulsory human review and brand tone editing
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Building a Google review response bank with 10 customized response templates (5 positive, 2 neutral, 3 severe negative complaints), and auditing a live profile's review sentiment.
📝 Practical Assignment: Customer Review & Reputation Management Playbook (Google Docs / Sheets) containing review request workflow steps, neutral messaging templates, QR code distribution strategy, negative complaint escalation protocol, and policy compliance checklist.
5 Local Authority, Measurement & Reporting 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Build ethical local directory authority, audit citations, track Google Business Profile performance telemetry, and communicate transparent monthly reports to stakeholders.
Key Topics Covered (10):
  • Local authority fundamentals: how external web signals and directory citations reinforce business prominence and verify physical location legitimacy
  • Local citations & directory listings: top national, regional, and industry-specific directories (Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, YellowPages, Yelp); citation consistency principles
  • Citation audit & cleanup: identifying inconsistent NAP listings, outdated phone numbers, old physical addresses, and duplicate directory entries
  • Ethical local link and partnership opportunities: local business associations, chambers of commerce, sponsored community events, and supplier co-marketing
  • Local rank tracking concepts & limitations: understanding geographic rank variability, grid tracking tools (e.g., BrightLocal/Local Falcon concepts), and why static ranking reports are misleading
  • Google Business Profile Performance reports deep-dive: analyzing Profile Views (Search vs. Maps), Search Queries (Direct vs. Discovery vs. Branded), Website Clicks, Call-Button Clicks, and Direction Requests
  • Metric accuracy & realistic reporting: understanding that call clicks do not guarantee completed conversations, and direction requests do not guarantee store foot traffic
  • Connecting GBP to Google Analytics 4: implementing UTM tracking parameters on GBP website and appointment links to measure real on-site conversions
  • Multi-location measurement fundamentals: comparing performance across branches, normalizing metrics by market size, and identifying underperforming locations
  • Building a monthly local SEO client dashboard: structuring Google Sheets reports, tracking monthly interaction trends, and providing prioritized 30-day optimization roadmaps
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Tagging GBP website links with custom UTM parameters, auditing 5 core directory citations for NAP consistency, and building an interactive monthly GBP Performance Dashboard in Google Sheets.
📝 Practical Assignment: Local Search Improvement Dashboard & Monthly Client Report (Google Sheets / PDF) compiling baseline GBP interaction metrics, search query trends, citation health, competitor benchmarks, and 30-day prioritized action plan.
