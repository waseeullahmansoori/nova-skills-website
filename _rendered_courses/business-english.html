  • ✕Students looking solely for academic test-cramming (IELTS, TOEFL, PTE) without practical business and workplace application
  • ✕Individuals with zero foundational English literacy who require remedial elementary phonics before workplace training
  • ✕Learners seeking passive lecture-watching without active participation in live speaking labs, paired role-plays, and recorded exercises
  • ✕Individuals looking for quick 'native accent erasure' or unrealistic overnight fluency guarantees

Required Prerequisites

Basic functional English literacy and reading comprehension recommended. Mandatory participation in the Week 1 six-factor diagnostic assessment to calibrate individual baseline proficiency. Approximately CEFR A2-level or higher is suitable for the core professional track; foundational bridge support is provided where necessary.

Recommended Background

Willingness to participate actively in live recorded speaking labs, paired role-plays, impromptu speech drills, video self-critiques, and collaborative cohort simulations.
