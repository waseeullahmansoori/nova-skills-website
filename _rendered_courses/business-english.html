STEP-BY-STEP MODULES

Detailed Curriculum & Labs

1 Foundations of Professional English 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Establish correct grammar and vocabulary usage for the workplace.
  • Essential business grammar.
  • Common corporate vocabulary and jargon.
  • Avoiding common translation errors.
Key Concepts: TensesBusiness IdiomsActive VoiceSoftening Language
1 Tenses in the Workplace
Taught: Using past, present, and future tenses accurately in reporting and planning.
Learner Lab: Rewrite informal sentences into formal, grammatically correct statements.
Why It Matters: Incorrect tenses cause confusion about project timelines.
Expected Deliverable: Accurate use of tenses in business contexts.
2 Corporate Vocabulary Essentials
Taught: Key terms, acronyms, and idioms used in modern business.
Learner Lab: Complete vocabulary matching exercises and contextual usage quizzes.
Why It Matters: Understanding jargon helps you integrate into professional environments.
Expected Deliverable: Ability to understand and use standard corporate terms.
3 Active vs. Passive Voice
Taught: When to use active voice for impact and passive voice for diplomacy.
Learner Lab: Transform sentences between active and passive voice based on scenarios.
Why It Matters: Controls the tone and directness of your communication.
Expected Deliverable: Strategic use of voice in writing and speaking.
4 Politeness & Diplomacy
Taught: Using modal verbs and softening language to make requests and give feedback.
Learner Lab: Role-play giving constructive feedback to a colleague.
Why It Matters: Maintains positive relationships while addressing sensitive issues.
Expected Deliverable: Ability to communicate difficult messages diplomatically.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Grammar and vocabulary quizzes, role-play scenarios.
📝 Practical Assignment: Create a glossary of 50 business terms with example sentences.
💼 Commercial Application: Clearer daily communication with colleagues.
🎯 Expected Outcome: A strong foundation in business-appropriate English.
2 Effective Written Communication 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Master email writing, report drafting, and instant messaging etiquette.
  • Structuring professional emails.
  • Writing clear reports.
  • Slack/Teams communication norms.
Key Concepts: Email StructureCTAsChat EtiquetteMeeting Minutes
1 The Art of the Professional Email
Taught: Subject lines, greetings, body structure, and sign-offs.
Learner Lab: Draft emails for different scenarios (requests, complaints, updates).
Why It Matters: Emails are the primary mode of formal business communication.
Expected Deliverable: Ability to write concise and effective emails.
2 Writing for Action and Clarity
Taught: Using bullet points, bold text, and clear calls to action (CTAs).
Learner Lab: Edit a long, confusing email into a clear, scannable format.
Why It Matters: Ensures recipients understand exactly what is required of them.
Expected Deliverable: Emails that get faster responses.
3 Instant Messaging Etiquette
Taught: Professionalism on platforms like Slack and MS Teams.
Learner Lab: Analyze and correct inappropriate IM interactions.
Why It Matters: Maintains boundaries in informal communication channels.
Expected Deliverable: Professional daily chat communication.
4 Drafting Meeting Minutes & Summaries
Taught: Capturing key points, decisions, and action items accurately.
Learner Lab: Listen to a mock meeting and draft official minutes.
Why It Matters: Creates a reliable record of meetings for accountability.
Expected Deliverable: Clear, organized meeting documentation.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Drafting and editing various business documents.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit a portfolio of 5 different business emails.
💼 Commercial Application: Efficient and professional written correspondence.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Mastery of written business English.
3 Meetings and Spoken English 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Develop confidence and skills for participating in and leading meetings.
  • Language for agreeing and disagreeing.
  • Interrupting politely.
  • Leading a meeting agenda.
Key Concepts: Meeting FacilitationDiplomatic DisagreementVirtual Etiquette
1 Participating in Discussions
Taught: Phrases for expressing opinions, agreeing, and tactfully disagreeing.
Learner Lab: Participate in a group debate on a business topic.
Why It Matters: Ensures your voice is heard without causing conflict.
Expected Deliverable: Confident contribution to meetings.
2 Leading a Meeting
Taught: Opening the meeting, keeping to the agenda, and closing with action items.
Learner Lab: Role-play chairing a 10-minute team meeting.
Why It Matters: Effective facilitation keeps meetings productive and concise.
Expected Deliverable: Ability to guide a meeting effectively.
3 Clarification and Interruptions
Taught: How to ask for clarification and interrupt politely.
Learner Lab: Practice using interruption phrases in a simulated fast-paced meeting.
Why It Matters: Prevents misunderstandings and ensures active participation.
Expected Deliverable: Smooth navigation of dynamic conversations.
4 Virtual Meeting Mastery
Taught: Specific language for tech issues, screen sharing, and virtual etiquette.
Learner Lab: Conduct a mock meeting handling simulated technical difficulties.
Why It Matters: Remote work requires specific communication skills.
Expected Deliverable: Professionalism in virtual environments.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Live role-plays and mock meetings.
📝 Practical Assignment: Lead a recorded 15-minute mock meeting with peers.
💼 Commercial Application: Productive and well-managed team meetings.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Strong spoken presence in professional settings.
4 Presentations and Negotiations 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Learn to deliver impactful presentations and negotiate effectively.
  • Structuring a presentation.
  • Engaging an audience.
  • Negotiation strategies and language.
Key Concepts: SignpostingPersuasionNegotiation TacticsQ&A Management
1 Structuring Your Pitch
Taught: The beginning, middle, and end of a persuasive presentation.
Learner Lab: Outline a 5-minute presentation on a chosen topic.
Why It Matters: A clear structure keeps the audience engaged and aids memory.
Expected Deliverable: A well-organized presentation outline.
2 Signposting and Transitions
Taught: Language to guide the audience through different parts of the talk.
Learner Lab: Deliver a segment of a presentation using strong transitions.
Why It Matters: Makes complex information easy to follow.
Expected Deliverable: Smooth delivery of presentation content.
3 Language of Negotiation
Taught: Making offers, compromising, and rejecting proposals politely.
Learner Lab: Engage in a role-play negotiation over a vendor contract.
Why It Matters: Crucial for reaching agreements in sales, procurement, and management.
Expected Deliverable: Ability to negotiate outcomes using professional English.
4 Handling Q&A Sessions
Taught: Techniques for buying time, rephrasing questions, and answering confidently.
Learner Lab: Field unexpected questions from peers after a short pitch.
Why It Matters: Maintains authority when put on the spot.
Expected Deliverable: Confident handling of audience questions.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Delivering mini-presentations and negotiation role-plays.
📝 Practical Assignment: Record a final 10-minute presentation covering a business proposal.
💼 Commercial Application: Winning pitches and successful contract negotiations.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Advanced persuasive speaking skills.
5 Personality Development & Cross-Cultural Communication 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Enhance executive presence and navigate global communication nuances.
  • Building professional confidence.
  • Body language and vocal tonality.
  • Adapting to different cultural communication styles.
Key Concepts: Executive PresenceNon-verbal CommunicationCultural ContextActive Listening
1 Executive Presence & Confidence
Taught: Techniques to project authority, competence, and approachability.
Learner Lab: Practice posture and speaking drills on camera.
Why It Matters: Influences how colleagues and clients perceive your capabilities.
Expected Deliverable: Improved physical and vocal confidence.
2 Mastering Body Language
Taught: Eye contact, hand gestures, and facial expressions in both virtual and in-person settings.
Learner Lab: Analyze video recordings of their own speaking style.
Why It Matters: Non-verbal cues often communicate more than words.
Expected Deliverable: Intentional use of body language.
3 Cross-Cultural Communication
Taught: High-context vs. low-context cultures and direct vs. indirect communication.
Learner Lab: Analyze case studies of cross-cultural miscommunication.
Why It Matters: Prevents accidental offense in international business.
Expected Deliverable: Adaptability in global communication.
4 Active Listening & Empathy
Taught: Techniques for truly hearing and acknowledging others' viewpoints.
Learner Lab: Practice mirroring and reflective listening in pairs.
Why It Matters: Builds trust and stronger professional relationships.
Expected Deliverable: Stronger interpersonal communication skills.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Self-analysis of video recordings and listening exercises.
📝 Practical Assignment: Write a reflection paper on your personal communication style and growth plan.
💼 Commercial Application: Effective leadership and global collaboration.
🎯 Expected Outcome: A polished, confident professional persona.
