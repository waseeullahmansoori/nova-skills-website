STEP-BY-STEP MODULES

Detailed Curriculum & Labs

Module 01 AE Foundations & The Graph Editor 3 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Understand the AE interface and master the art of smooth animation.
  • Compositions and layers
  • Transform properties (Anchor Point, Position, Scale, Rotation, Opacity)
  • Keyframe interpolation
  • The Speed Graph Editor
Key Concepts: Graph EditorTrack MattesPre-Comps
1.1 Interface & Transform Properties
Taught: Navigating the UI and animating basic properties.
Learner Lab: Animates a basic bouncing ball.
Why It Matters: The absolute foundation of all motion graphics.
Expected Deliverable: Understanding of the timeline and keyframes.
1.2 Mastering the Graph Editor
Taught: Using the speed graph to create ease-in, ease-out, and custom velocity curves.
Learner Lab: Refines the bouncing ball and animates UI elements with custom easing.
Why It Matters: Separates amateur, robotic motion from professional, organic motion.
Expected Deliverable: Smooth, professional animations.
1.3 Masks, Mattes, and Pre-Comps
Taught: Using alpha track mattes for text reveals and pre-composing layers.
Learner Lab: Creates a professional lower-third graphic.
Why It Matters: Essential for clean transitions and organizing complex timelines.
Expected Deliverable: A broadcast-ready text reveal.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Animate a multi-layered logo reveal.
📝 Practical Assignment: Deliverable: 5-second logo animation.
💼 Commercial Application: Brand intro videos.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Solid grasp of 2D animation fundamentals.
Module 02 Shape Layers & Kinetic Typography 3 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Build dynamic, vector-based animations for explainer videos and ads.
  • Shape layer modifiers (Trim Paths)
  • Text Animators
  • Parenting and Null Objects
Key Concepts: Trim PathsText AnimatorsParenting
2.1 Shape Layer Modifiers
Taught: Using Trim Paths, Repeater, and Wiggle Paths.
Learner Lab: Animates bursting lines and abstract shapes.
Why It Matters: Allows for complex vector animations without manual keyframing.
Expected Deliverable: A dynamic vector background.
2.2 Kinetic Typography
Taught: Using text animators and range selectors to automate text motion.
Learner Lab: Animates a 10-second kinetic typography quote.
Why It Matters: Crucial for social media ads and lyric videos.
Expected Deliverable: A punchy text animation.
2.3 Parenting and Basic Expressions
Taught: Using null objects to control multiple layers and the wiggle expression.
Learner Lab: Rigs a simple mechanical arm or camera move.
Why It Matters: Saves hours of animation time.
Expected Deliverable: An automated, controllable animation setup.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Build a kinetic typography scene synced to an audio clip.
📝 Practical Assignment: Deliverable: 10-second kinetic typography quote.
💼 Commercial Application: Social media video ads.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Ability to create engaging text and vector motion.
Module 03 VFX, Tracking & Capstone 3 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Integrate graphics into live-action footage and complete the final project.
  • 2D Motion Tracking
  • Screen Replacements
  • Roto Brush 2
  • Project rendering
Key Concepts: Motion TrackingMocha AERoto Brush
3.1 Motion Tracking and Mocha AE
Taught: Point tracking and planar tracking with Mocha for screen replacements.
Learner Lab: Replaces the screen of a moving smartphone with custom UI.
Why It Matters: Allows graphics to lock seamlessly into moving footage.
Expected Deliverable: A flawless screen replacement.
3.2 Rotoscoping with AI Tools
Taught: Using Roto Brush to isolate subjects from their backgrounds.
Learner Lab: Places large kinetic text behind a walking subject.
Why It Matters: Allows placing text or graphics *behind* moving people in video.
Expected Deliverable: A dynamic VFX shot.
3.3 Capstone Explainer Video
Taught: Bringing together shape layers, text, and tracking into a final project.
Learner Lab: Builds, refines, and exports a 30-second explainer video.
Why It Matters: Proves end-to-end competency in After Effects.
Expected Deliverable: A portfolio-ready motion graphics piece.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Complete a screen replacement and rotoscope shot, then finalize the capstone.
📝 Practical Assignment: Deliverable: 30-second explainer video.
💼 Commercial Application: Commercial VFX and explainer production.
🎯 Expected Outcome: A fully finished, industry-ready project.
