STEP-BY-STEP MODULES

Detailed Curriculum & Labs

Module 01 Agency Positioning & Productized Retainer Architecture 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Transform customized one-off freelance services into profitable, scalable productized retainers with clear deliverables.
  • Narrow agency positioning to a high-demand vertical industry or specific technical outcome.
  • Design 3-tier productized service offers that clients can buy on a monthly recurring basis.
  • Calculate gross margins, contractor cost ceilings, and cashflow reserve targets.
Key Concepts: Vertical niche positioningProductized servicesRetainer architectureGross margin optimization
1.1 Niche Dominance & The Anti-Generalist Agency
Taught: Why full-service generalist agencies fail and how vertical specialization attracts premium enterprise pricing.
Learner Lab: Audits 5 successful boutique agencies and selects a defined vertical niche for their own agency.
Why It Matters: Niche agencies close sales in half the time because case studies directly match the prospect's exact business model.
Expected Deliverable: Agency Positioning Matrix and UVP (Unique Value Proposition) document.
1.2 Packaging Productized Services & Retainers
Taught: Converting hours-for-dollars billing into predictable recurring retainers ($2k–$8k/month).
Learner Lab: Builds a 3-tier productized offer matrix defining exact deliverables, turnaround times, and revision limits.
Why It Matters: Retainers eliminate income volatility and establish valuation for your business.
Expected Deliverable: Productized Agency Service Menu ready for client presentation.
1.3 Agency Unit Economics & Pricing Calculator
Taught: Pricing models ensuring minimum 50% gross margins after contractor payouts and software overhead.
Learner Lab: Calculates profit margins, contractor caps, and breakeven thresholds using financial spreadsheet models.
Why It Matters: Underpricing kills agencies as soon as delegation starts; proper unit economics ensures sustained profitability.
Expected Deliverable: Agency Financial Unit Economics Model.
1.4 High-Ticket Pitch Decks & Discovery Frameworks
Taught: Conducting diagnostic agency discovery calls and presenting pitch decks that anchor high fees.
Learner Lab: Customizes a 12-slide high-converting agency sales deck in Canva/Figma.
Why It Matters: Agency sales are consultative consultations, not aggressive pitches; diagnosing value commands respect.
Expected Deliverable: Production-ready Agency Pitch Deck.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Create the complete productized offer deck for a boutique agency with 3 distinct pricing tiers.
📝 Practical Assignment: Deliver the Agency Business Model Blueprint detailing niche, packaging, and financial unit economics.
💼 Commercial Application: Positions the agency as an indispensable domain partner rather than an interchangeable commodity vendor.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Complete Agency Offer Architecture approved by mentor.
Module 02 SOP Engine & ClickUp Operations Hub 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Build the internal engine that allows your agency to deliver consistent results without your constant intervention.
  • Map core delivery workflows into clear, step-by-step Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
  • Set up ClickUp spaces, folders, sprint lists, and automations for multi-client execution.
  • Establish automated client onboarding sequences and communication boundaries.
Key Concepts: 3-layer SOP structureClickUp automation enginesZero-friction client onboardingAsynchronous communication boundaries
2.1 Writing Foolproof Video & Written SOPs
Taught: The 3-layer SOP framework: Trigger, Action Checklist, Verification Rubric.
Learner Lab: Records 3 Loom video SOPs and documents matching step-by-step checklists in Notion.
Why It Matters: A contractor can only execute as well as your instructions; clear SOPs eliminate rework and quality drops.
Expected Deliverable: 3 production-ready Core Delivery SOPs with embedded video guides.
2.2 Architecting the ClickUp Agency Workspace
Taught: Configuring client spaces, task templates, custom fields, and automated status transitions.
Learner Lab: Constructs a complete agency workspace in ClickUp featuring intake, in-progress, QA, and delivered views.
Why It Matters: Centralized project management keeps contractors synchronized and prevents missed deadlines.
Expected Deliverable: Live ClickUp workspace configured with multi-client dashboard views.
2.3 Automating Client Onboarding with Make.com & Stripe
Taught: Connecting invoice payment to contract delivery, client folder creation, and welcome emails.
Learner Lab: Builds an automated onboarding pipeline connecting payment triggers to Notion portal invites.
Why It Matters: First impressions set the tone for retention; seamless onboarding builds instant client confidence.
Expected Deliverable: Automated Onboarding Workflow diagram and live test execution.
2.4 Client Communication Guidelines & Slack Connect Hygiene
Taught: Establishing strict async communication boundaries, response SLAs, and emergency protocols.
Learner Lab: Drafts the Agency Client Welcome Packet and Rules of Engagement document.
Why It Matters: Prevents client scope creep and burnout from endless late-night messaging.
Expected Deliverable: Published Client Welcome Packet ready for client sign-off.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Build a ClickUp project board and test an automated task assignment workflow.
📝 Practical Assignment: Deliver an Agency Operations Manual including 5 core SOPs and a configured ClickUp workspace.
💼 Commercial Application: Enables seamless multi-project delivery that scales without requiring your 24/7 presence.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Fully functional digital agency delivery platform.
Module 03 Contractor Recruitment, Vetting & Quality Control 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Find, hire, test, and manage top-tier remote contractors while enforcing rigorous quality gates.
  • Source specialist contractors across international talent hubs (Upwork, OnlineJobs.ph, Twitter/X).
  • Design paid mini-test projects that screen candidates on communication and technical skill.
  • Implement peer-review QA gates before client delivery.
Key Concepts: Global contractor sourcingPaid test project evaluationQA verification gatesSubcontractor IP assignment
3.1 Sourcing Specialist Talent & Writing Compelling Job Posts
Taught: How to attract skilled remote contractors without paying enterprise recruiter fees.
Learner Lab: Drafts 2 specialized job postings featuring knockout screening questions.
Why It Matters: High-performing agencies are powered by extraordinary contractor talent.
Expected Deliverable: 2 optimized Contractor Job Post listings.
3.2 The Paid Test Project & Vetting Rubric
Taught: Designing a 2-hour paid evaluation project that reveals punctuality, skill, and attention to SOPs.
Learner Lab: Develops a standard paid test task, grading rubric, and contractor agreement contract.
Why It Matters: Never hire based on resumes alone; paid test projects reveal real-world execution ability.
Expected Deliverable: Contractor Evaluation Kit with scoring rubric.
3.3 Quality Assurance Gates & Milestone Checklists
Taught: Designing mandatory QA checklists that contractors must complete before flagging work for review.
Learner Lab: Creates domain-specific QA rubrics for design, code, or marketing deliverables.
Why It Matters: Prevents embarrassing junior mistakes from ever reaching client inboxes.
Expected Deliverable: Standard Agency QA Checklist.
3.4 Contractor Agreements, IP Assignment & Payment Cadence
Taught: Legal protection: independent contractor agreements, non-disclosure, and copyright assignment.
Learner Lab: Reviews and prepares standard subcontractor legal templates.
Why It Matters: Protects your intellectual property and guarantees all work delivered belongs cleanly to your client.
Expected Deliverable: Subcontractor Legal Agreement Dossier.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Simulate a contractor evaluation on 3 anonymized candidate work samples.
📝 Practical Assignment: Assemble the Contractor Hiring Kit (Job Post + Test Brief + QA Rubric + Subcontractor Agreement).
💼 Commercial Application: Builds an on-demand, variable-cost team ready to fulfill client contracts without high fixed payroll.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Vetted contractor roster ready for live project assignments.
Module 04 Agency Client Acquisition, Retainers & Capstone Launch 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Deploy predictable outbound and inbound sales systems, close high-ticket retainers, and defend your agency model.
  • Build cold outbound email and LinkedIn pipelines that generate qualified discovery calls weekly.
  • Run Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs) that increase client retention beyond 12 months.
  • Defend your completed Agency Launchpad capstone before mentors.
Key Concepts: Targeted B2B pipeline developmentMulti-touch cold outreachQuarterly Business Reviews (QBRs)Long-term client retention
4.1 B2B Outbound Lead Gen & LinkedIn Social Selling
Taught: Building targeted prospect lists with Apollo and executing hyper-personalized multi-touch outreach.
Learner Lab: Builds a targeted list of 50 dream clients and scripts a 4-step value-first email sequence.
Why It Matters: Consistent outbound outreach protects you from market downturns and platform reliance.
Expected Deliverable: 50-contact B2B Outreach Campaign ready to send.
4.2 Negotiating High-Ticket Retainers & Closing the Deal
Taught: Navigating client procurement, handling price objections, and structuring multi-month commitments.
Learner Lab: Roleplays an agency contract negotiation handling scope pushback and price objections.
Why It Matters: Closing retainers shifts agency revenue from lumpy project fees to predictable enterprise cashflow.
Expected Deliverable: Objection Handling Script & Retainer Contract Agreement.
4.3 Client Retention, QBRs & Upsell Strategies
Taught: Structuring 90-day Quarterly Business Reviews to showcase ROI and expand contract value.
Learner Lab: Designs a QBR presentation deck showcasing performance telemetry and pitching expanded scope.
Why It Matters: Retaining an existing client costs 5x less than acquiring a new one and generates 80% of agency profits.
Expected Deliverable: Quarterly Business Review Presentation Template.
4.4 Capstone: The Agency Launchpad Defense
Taught: Presenting your complete agency architecture: niche, productized offers, SOPs, ClickUp workspace, and hiring funnel.
Learner Lab: Delivers an end-to-end 20-minute oral defense of their Agency Operating System.
Why It Matters: Proves total operational readiness to operate a profitable, resilient digital agency.
Expected Deliverable: Defended and mentor-approved Agency Launchpad.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Execute an outbound campaign simulation to 25 verified company decision-makers.
📝 Practical Assignment: Deliver the Capstone Agency Launchpad (Brand, Offer Deck, ClickUp Workspace, SOP Library, Legal Contracts).
💼 Commercial Application: Establishes a sustainable commercial agency capable of generating $10k–$30k/month in recurring fees.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Certified and fully operational boutique digital agency.
