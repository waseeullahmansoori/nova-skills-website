Module 01 Audience Research & Content Strategy 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Establish the strategic foundations of content marketing by connecting business objectives with in-depth audience research, buyer personas, customer journey mapping, content pillars, and KPI scorecards.
  • How to conduct audience research and uncover high-intent customer problems.
  • Mapping educational and conversion-focused content across the 3-stage buyer journey.
  • Developing content pillars, brand voice guides, and strategic KPI scorecards.
Key Concepts: Audience-Centric StrategyBuyer PersonasFunnel Content MappingContent PillarsBrand Voice Architecture
Lesson 1.1 Content Marketing Fundamentals & Commercial Alignment
Taught: The strategic role of content in modern business growth, distinguishing content marketing from advertising, customer attention economics, and demand creation versus capture.
  • Content marketing as an asset that compounds in value over time
  • Connecting content initiatives directly to business revenue and customer retention
  • Analyzing commercial case studies of content-led business growth
Learner Lab: Evaluates two commercial brands to dissect how their content builds audience trust and supports sales funnels.
Why It Matters: Without clear commercial alignment, content production quickly degenerates into purposeless blogging with zero business impact.
Expected Deliverable: A Content Marketing Commercial Alignment Matrix.
Lesson 1.2 Audience Persona Research & Pain-Point Discovery
Taught: Methods for conducting qualitative audience research, extracting authentic customer phrasing from online communities, and constructing actionable buyer personas.
  • Mining Reddit, Quora, and customer reviews for real customer objections and questions
  • Building buyer personas: demographics, job challenges, decision barriers, and content formats
  • B2B vs. B2C audience research nuances
Learner Lab: Conducts online research on a chosen industry and builds two detailed buyer personas documenting pain points and content needs.
Why It Matters: Content tailored to specific customer pain points achieves significantly higher engagement and conversion rates.
Expected Deliverable: Two complete Buyer Persona profiles.
Lesson 1.3 Customer Journey Mapping: TOFU, MOFU & BOFU Content
Taught: The 3-stage buyer journey, designing content formats tailored to buyer awareness levels, and maintaining the balance between educational and promotional material.
  • Top-of-Funnel (TOFU): Educational guides, industry trends, problem awareness
  • Middle-of-Funnel (MOFU): Comparison frameworks, checklists, how-to guides
  • Bottom-of-Funnel (BOFU): Case studies, implementation blueprints, ROI calculators
Learner Lab: Maps 6 content topics across the 3 funnel stages for a designated product or service.
Why It Matters: Pumping sales pitches to top-of-funnel prospects alienates buyers; journey mapping ensures the right message at the right time.
Expected Deliverable: A Customer Journey Content Funnel Map.
Lesson 1.4 Content Pillars, Brand Voice & KPI Scorecards
Taught: Defining 3-5 core brand pillars, establishing competitive differentiation, authoring brand voice rules, and setting up a comprehensive KPI scorecard.
  • Selecting core content pillars that intersect brand authority and audience interest
  • Brand voice dimensions: tone attributes, vocabulary choices, and formatting guidelines
  • Setting SMART KPIs across reach, engagement, lead capture, and assisted sales
Learner Lab: Drafts a Brand Voice Guide with tone examples and establishes a multi-tier KPI scorecard.
Why It Matters: A clear brand voice ensures consistent customer experience across all touchpoints, while KPIs keep teams accountable to business outcomes.
Expected Deliverable: A Brand Voice Guide and Content KPI Framework.

Module Focus: Master professional content research methodologies, analyze search engine results pages (SERPs) for audience intent, extract subject matter expert (SME) insights, verify facts, and prepare research-backed editorial briefs.

  • Fact-checking protocol: verifying claims across at least two independent credible sources
Learner Lab: Compiles a verified evidence repository of 8 credible statistics and expert quotes for an upcoming article.
Why It Matters: Inaccurate data or unverified claims destroy brand credibility and can expose companies to legal or reputational damage.

Lesson 2.3 Outline Construction & Information Hierarchy

  • Heading hierarchy best practices: descriptive H2s and scannable subtopics
  • Structuring narrative flow: introduction hook, problem explanation, practical steps, and CTA
  • Formatting for modern digital reading: bulleted lists, callouts, and summary tables
Learner Lab: Creates a detailed 6-section content outline complete with key talking points and scannable elements.
Why It Matters: A solid outline saves hours of rewriting by resolving structural flaws before drafting begins.
Expected Deliverable: A Detailed Content Outline worksheet.
Lesson 2.4 Authoring Professional Editorial Briefs
Taught: Synthesizing research, audience intent, evidence, outlines, and brand voice into a complete editorial brief that writers can execute with precision.
  • Anatomy of an editorial brief: Target Audience, Central Proposition, Outline, Sources, CTA
  • Establishing editorial quality criteria and word count parameters
  • Adapting briefs for different formats: articles, landing pages, and case studies
Learner Lab: Writes three distinct editorial briefs (one educational article, one service page, one case study) with complete research citations.
Why It Matters: Editorial briefs eliminate ambiguity between strategists, editors, and writers, ensuring first-draft quality.

Taught: The mandatory 11-step workflow, isolating raw AI drafts, applying structural editing, rewriting passive sentences, improving cadence, and enhancing readability.
  • The 11-step pipeline: Research -> Evidence -> Brief -> Prompt -> Draft -> Edit -> Verify -> Voice -> QA -> Approval -> Publish
  • Line editing: removing wordiness, varying sentence lengths, and improving clarity
  • Reading grade optimization: using Hemingway editor standards to ensure accessibility
Learner Lab: Takes a raw AI-generated section and performs comprehensive line and developmental editing in Google Docs.

Taught: Detecting AI hallucinations, identifying invented citations, verifying numbers against primary studies, and inserting clean source hyperlinks.

  • Manual verification checklist: searching for the exact source study, methodology, and sample size
  • Clean attribution formatting: hyperlinking anchor text to primary sources rather than general homepages
Learner Lab: Audits a sample AI draft containing intentional factual errors, logs every hallucination, and inserts verified source citations.
Why It Matters: Publishing hallucinated data destroys brand credibility and breaches professional editorial standards.
Expected Deliverable: A Fact-Checking Audit Log with verified citations.

Taught: Purging repetitive AI words ('delve', 'testament', 'beacon'), infusing original viewpoints, and adhering to Google's official guidance on generative AI content.

  • Infusing proprietary frameworks, client examples, and contrarian opinions
  • Google's Helpful Content and spam policies: prioritizing people-first utility over automated ranking schemes
Learner Lab: Cleanses an article draft of AI cliches, injects a proprietary framework, and completes a pre-publication QA checklist.
