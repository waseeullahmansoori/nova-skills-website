STEP-BY-STEP MODULES

Detailed Curriculum & Labs

1 Design & Photoshop Foundations 7 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Build a strong foundation in Photoshop's workspace, raster artwork, document preparation, image resolution and essential design principles.
Key Topics Covered (15):
  • Photoshop interface and workspace customization: Tools panel, Options bar, Panels docking, and custom studio workspaces
  • Document creation, canvas dimensions, pixel aspect ratios, and resolution standards for web, screen, and print
  • Raster versus vector graphics: mathematical vectors vs pixel matrices, scalability boundaries, and interpolation artifacts
  • Common raster formats: PSD master working files, JPEG compression algorithms, PNG transparency, and WebP efficiency
  • Image resolution versus physical print dimensions: Pixels Per Inch (PPI) fundamentals and viewing distance calculation
  • Color modes: RGB additive color vs CMYK subtractive color, bit depth (8-bit vs 16-bit), and gamut limitations
  • Color profiles: sRGB for web outputs, Adobe RGB (1998) for wide-gamut workflows, and CMYK prepress target profiles
  • Core commercial design principles: visual hierarchy, contrast, balance, alignment, proximity, repetition, and purposeful whitespace
  • Advertising typography fundamentals: font pairing, tracking, leading, kerning, type hierarchy, and legibility over complex backgrounds
  • Layer fundamentals: background layer unlocking, layer stacking order, opacity vs fill, layer locking modes, and color coding
  • Transformation and alignment: Free Transform controls, proportional scaling, reference points, Skew, Distort, and Perspective
  • Rulers, guides, grids, and Smart Guides: precision coordinate positioning and mathematical margin setup
  • Artboards in Photoshop: multi-canvas setup for social media formats and responsive banner suites
  • Cropping and composition: Rule of Thirds, Golden Ratio, Straighten tool, and non-destructive crop modes
  • Studio asset sourcing, moodboard development, file naming conventions, and interpreting commercial design briefs
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Configure custom commercial workspaces; construct grid-aligned advertising canvases with designated margins and safe zones; balance typographic headlines over product photography with purposeful contrast.

2 Precision Editing 8 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Develop precise, controlled image-editing skills using selections, masks, paths, adjustment layers and Smart Objects.
Key Topics Covered (17):
  • Selection fundamentals: Geometric Marquee tools, Lasso, Polygonal Lasso, and Magnetic Lasso mechanics
  • AI-assisted selection tools: Quick Selection, Object Selection Tool, Selection Brush, Magic Wand, and Select Subject engine
  • Select and Mask dedicated workspace: Global Refinements (Smooth, Feather, Contrast, Shift Edge) and View Modes
  • Refine Edge Brush: soft-edge isolation, fur and flyaway hair extraction, and edge contamination decontaminate colors
  • Non-destructive masking: Layer Masks vs permanent pixel deletion, revealing (white), concealing (black), and gray opacity gradients
  • Clipping masks: bounding adjustment layers and textures strictly to underlying shapes and text layers
  • Vector masks and Pen Tool mastery: Bézier curves, anchor points, smooth vs corner points, and Path Operations (Add, Subtract, Intersect)
  • Precision product clipping paths: creating ultra-sharp commercial vector silhouetting for e-commerce catalog isolation
  • Non-destructive Adjustment Layers: Levels histograms, input/output sliders, black/white/gray eyedroppers, and tonal clipping
  • Curves deep dive: S-curves for contrast, individual RGB channel curve color grading, and precise point sampling
  • Color adjustments: Hue/Saturation colorized modes, Color Balance shadow/midtone/highlight grading, Selective Color, and Vibrance
  • Adobe Camera Raw as a filter: white balance temperature/tint, exposure, highlight recovery, shadow boosting, clarity, and dehaze
  • Smart Objects architecture: Embedded vs Linked Smart Objects, preserving raw pixel data against destructive degradation
  • Smart Filters: applying reversible blurs, sharpening, and distortions with independent filter masks
  • Photoshop Blend Modes: Multiply (darken), Screen (lighten), Overlay/Soft Light (contrast), and Color/Luminosity separation
  • Layer Effects (Layer Styles): Drop Shadow, Inner Shadow, Stroke, Color Overlay, and Bevel & Emboss with non-destructive control
  • Quality assurance at 100% and 200% zoom: checking for fringing, halos, jagged edges, and mask bleed
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Hand-draw vector clipping paths around intricate product contours; isolate models with complex flyaway hair; perform non-destructive color balancing and tone curve corrections without modifying background pixels.
📝 Practical Assignment: [object Object]
3 Retouching & Image Enhancement 8 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Learn to enhance portraits and products while preserving realistic texture, lighting, geometry and visual authenticity.
Key Topics Covered (17):
  • Professional retouching philosophy: enhancement vs alteration, visual authenticity, and client brief adherence
  • Before-and-after image evaluation: identifying sensor dust, scratches, stray hairs, blemishes, and tonal inconsistencies
  • Healing toolset: Spot Healing Brush (Content-Aware, Proximity Match), Healing Brush (sampled texture), and Patch Tool
  • The Remove Tool (AI-powered generative cleanup): clearing complex background distractions while reconstructing underlying textures
  • Clone Stamp Tool: sample alignment, brush hardness, opacity/flow settings, and avoiding repeating stamp patterns
  • Frequency separation fundamentals: separating high-frequency texture from low-frequency color and tone, and understanding over-smoothing risks
  • Dodge and Burn techniques: non-destructive 50% gray overlay layers, Curves-based dodging and burning, and sculpting facial/product volume
  • Portrait retouching ethics: preserving natural skin pores, correcting temporary blemishes without plasticized smoothing
  • Commercial product retouching: removing manufacturing seam lines, packaging scratches, label misalignments, and dust specks
  • Challenging surface materials: glass transparency preservation, chrome/metallic reflection cleanup, and matte surface smoothing
  • Product label preservation: maintaining brand logos, regulatory text, and barcode clarity without blur or distortion
  • Realistic shadow anatomy: contact shadows (darkest, tight), cast shadows (directional, softening), and ambient occlusion
  • Shadow creation and rendering: custom brush softness, motion blurs, perspective skews, and opacity gradients matching original light sources
  • Realistic reflections: duplicating subjects, vertical inversion, perspective warping, layer masking gradients, and surface texture blending
  • Perspective correction: Perspective Warp tool, Lens Correction filter for optical barrel/pincushion distortion, and vertical line straightening
  • Camera Raw finishing: detail sharpening, luminance noise reduction, chromatic aberration removal, and output sharpening
  • Image quality inspection: 100% zoom check for retouching artifacts, banding, edge halos, and color blotches
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Clean up cosmetic packaging imperfections; reconstruct broken reflections on chrome and glass surfaces; build multi-layer contact and cast shadows matching a studio strobe key light; execute ethical portrait skin cleanup.
📝 Practical Assignment: [object Object]
4 Commercial Compositing & AI 8 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Build photorealistic commercial composites using traditional Photoshop craftsmanship and AI-assisted tools where appropriate.
Key Topics Covered (17):
  • Commercial compositing principles: visual storytelling, concept ideation, and moodboard construction
  • Source asset compatibility: camera focal length, sensor resolution, perspective angles, horizon line, and eye-level alignment
  • Lighting consistency: identifying key light, fill light, rim light, ambient bounce, and color temperature (Kelvin) across sources
  • Scale, proportion, and spatial placement: establishing realistic physical relationships between products and environments
  • Atmospheric depth: aerial perspective, contrast falloff with distance, depth-of-field simulation, and Gaussian/Lens Blur gradients
  • Edge blending: hair and transparent object integration, light wrap simulation via inner shadows and brush overlays
  • Color grading for compositing: Curves matching highlights, midtones, and shadows to the environment's ambient color palette
  • Generative Fill in commercial workflows: adding contextual background environments, extending sets, and replacing unwanted props
  • Generative Expand: expanding composition canvases to accommodate new aspect ratios and white space requirements
  • Harmonize tool mechanics: matching lighting, color balance, and tonal contrast between cutout foregrounds and background plates
  • Supported Harmonize workflows: layer rasterization requirements, Smart Object conversion constraints, and manual refinement
  • Generate Background: creating contextual concept environments with Adobe Firefly generative models
  • Prompt engineering for commercial image editing: descriptive keywords, lighting terminology, lens specifications, and avoiding prompt drift
  • AI artifact identification: spotting anatomical errors, perspective mismatches, distorted text/logos, unnatural shadows, and edge melting
  • Manual correction of AI outputs: stamping over AI artifacts, manual edge masking, repainting textures, and replacing distorted logos
  • Adobe licensing and commercial terms: generative credits consumption, commercial-use terms, beta limitations, and client copyright
  • Stock asset licensing, model releases, and property permissions for commercial advertising deployment
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Place an e-commerce product photograph into an AI-generated luxury lifestyle environment; adjust lighting angle and color temperature with Curves; cast contact and directional shadows; paint light wrap along product boundaries; fix melted AI background details with manual cloning.

5 Advertising Production & Portfolio 8 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Convert Photoshop editing skills into commercial advertisements, multi-format campaigns, export-ready artwork and professional client handovers.
Key Topics Covered (16):
  • Commercial advertising campaign fundamentals: target audience, product positioning, single-minded proposition, and visual hooks
  • Product hero advertising design: placing the product as the hero, establishing hierarchy between visual, headline, copy, and CTA
  • Advertising typography rules: tracking and leading for headlines, contrast against varied backgrounds, and button CTA styling
  • Social media campaign formats: feed posts, vertical stories, carousel frames, and e-commerce hero banners
  • Mandatory campaign dimensions: 4:5 Portrait Feed (1080 × 1350 px), 1:1 Square Feed (1080 × 1080 px), and 9:16 Vertical Story/Reel (1080 × 1920 px)
  • Responsive composition principles: adapting layouts across ratios without stretching, distorting, or awkward cropping
  • Repositioning and scale: adjusting product focal point, headline sizing, and whitespace distribution per format
  • Safe-area management: keeping vital text, logos, and CTAs clear of platform UI overlays (profile headers, comment bars, swipe indicators)
  • Export for digital platforms: Export As workspace, JPEG quality settings, PNG-24 transparency, WebP optimization, and sRGB profile embedding
  • Print production fundamentals: physical dimensions (mm/inches), effective resolution versus PPI, and viewing distance standards
  • CMYK color separation: process color limitations, rich black (C:60 M:40 Y:40 K:100) vs pure black, and soft proofing with target ICC profiles
  • Prepress technicalities: bleed (3mm/0.125in), trim lines, safety margins, and high-resolution PDF/X export standards
  • Commercial mockup creation: placing finished advertising creatives onto realistic 3D billboards, subway posters, or device screens via Smart Objects
  • Professional PSD handover standards: the 10 standard layer groups, eliminating hidden duplicate layers, and relinking missing assets
  • Client delivery package architecture: organizing Source_PSD, Final_Exports, Original_Assets, Preview, and Documentation folders
  • Final portfolio presentation, client revision workflows, feedback implementation, and final assessment review
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Design a luxury product hero advertisement; convert the design into three responsive aspect ratios (4:5, 1:1, 9:16) using Artboards; soft-proof CMYK color gamuts; package a complete client handover folder with structured PSDs.
