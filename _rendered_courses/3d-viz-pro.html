STEP-BY-STEP MODULES

Detailed Curriculum & Labs

Module 01 3D Modeling Fundamentals in Blender 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Master polygon and subdivision surface modeling.
  • Subdivision surface modeling
  • Topology flow
  • Hard surface detailing
Key Concepts: SubdivisionTopology
1.1 Blender Interface & Navigation
Taught: Viewport controls and basic hotkeys.
Learner Lab: Navigates and sets up workspace.
Why It Matters: Foundation of all Blender workflows.
Expected Deliverable: Customized UI.
1.2 Polygon Modeling Tools
Taught: Extrude, bevel, inset, and loop cuts.
Learner Lab: Models a basic electronic device.
Why It Matters: Core modeling operations.
Expected Deliverable: Blockout model.
1.3 Subdivision Surface Workflow
Taught: Controlling edge loops and smoothing.
Learner Lab: Refines the blockout with subdivision.
Why It Matters: Creates smooth, high-quality curved surfaces.
Expected Deliverable: Smooth, high-poly model.
1.4 Hard Surface Detailing
Taught: Booleans and intricate detailing.
Learner Lab: Adds buttons, ports, and panel lines.
Why It Matters: Adds realism to product models.
Expected Deliverable: Finalized high-poly product model.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Model a high-end headphone set.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit Blender file of the headphone model.
💼 Commercial Application: Product prototyping.
🎯 Expected Outcome: High-quality 3D asset.
Module 02 Advanced Texturing in Substance Painter 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Create realistic materials and weathering.
  • UV Unwrapping
  • Texture Baking
  • Smart Materials
Key Concepts: UV LayoutBakingPBR
2.1 UV Unwrapping in Blender
Taught: Marking seams and unwrapping geometry.
Learner Lab: Unwraps the headphone model.
Why It Matters: Required for painting 2D textures onto 3D objects.
Expected Deliverable: Optimized UV layout.
2.2 Baking Mesh Maps
Taught: Baking normals, AO, and curvature in Substance.
Learner Lab: Bakes maps from high to low poly.
Why It Matters: Drives smart materials and mask generators.
Expected Deliverable: Baked texture set.
2.3 PBR Materials & Masking
Taught: Creating base materials and using layer masks.
Learner Lab: Applies plastic, leather, and metal materials.
Why It Matters: Accurately represents physical surfaces like plastic and metal.
Expected Deliverable: Textured product.
2.4 Weathering & Micro-details
Taught: Adding dust, scratches, and fingerprints.
Learner Lab: Adds subtle wear and tear.
Why It Matters: Breaks up perfection for photorealism.
Expected Deliverable: Final exported PBR textures.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Texture the headphones with realistic materials.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit exported texture maps.
💼 Commercial Application: Creating marketing assets.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Production-ready textures.
Module 03 V-Ray Lighting & LookDev 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Illuminate products for commercial rendering.
  • Studio Lighting Setup
  • Look Development
  • Camera Settings
Key Concepts: LookDevStudio LightingDOF
3.1 LookDev Environment Setup
Taught: Importing models and connecting PBR textures in V-Ray.
Learner Lab: Sets up the lookdev scene and assigns materials.
Why It Matters: Ensures materials look correct under neutral lighting.
Expected Deliverable: Lookdev ready scene.
3.2 Studio Lighting Techniques
Taught: Three-point lighting and softbox setups.
Learner Lab: Lights the headphones using V-Ray rectangular lights.
Why It Matters: Highlights product features and forms.
Expected Deliverable: Professionally lit product.
3.3 Advanced Material Tuning
Taught: Tweaking roughness, transmission, and SSS in context.
Learner Lab: Adjusts material properties under final lighting.
Why It Matters: Refines the physical accuracy of the surfaces.
Expected Deliverable: Photorealistic material response.
3.4 Depth of Field & Camera FX
Taught: Setting up physical camera properties like F-Stop for DOF.
Learner Lab: Configures shallow depth of field for close-up shots.
Why It Matters: Adds photographic realism and focus.
Expected Deliverable: Cinematic camera framing.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Create three studio lighting variations.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit three studio renders.
💼 Commercial Application: E-commerce product visuals.
🎯 Expected Outcome: High-end product renders.
Module 04 Final Rendering & Compositing 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Polish renders in post-production.
  • Render Optimization
  • Render Elements
  • Photoshop Compositing
Key Concepts: Render ElementsCompositingColor Grading
4.1 Render Quality vs Speed
Taught: Optimizing V-Ray image sampler and GI settings.
Learner Lab: Tunes settings for final 4K output.
Why It Matters: Crucial for meeting production deadlines.
Expected Deliverable: Optimized render configuration.
4.2 Rendering Passes (Elements)
Taught: Setting up Cryptomatte, reflections, and raw lighting passes.
Learner Lab: Renders out multi-pass EXRs.
Why It Matters: Allows total control in post-production.
Expected Deliverable: Complete render pass set.
4.3 Compositing in Photoshop
Taught: Blending passes using screen/multiply modes.
Learner Lab: Rebuilds the render using passes in Photoshop.
Why It Matters: Enhances reflections and shadows beyond the raw render.
Expected Deliverable: Composited image.
4.4 Final Polish & Grading
Taught: Adding grain, vignette, and color grading.
Learner Lab: Applies final grading adjustments.
Why It Matters: Unifies the image and adds a cinematic feel.
Expected Deliverable: Final commercial imagery.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Composite and grade the final headphone renders.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit PSD file and final JPEG renders.
💼 Commercial Application: Advertising campaigns.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Commercial-ready visuals.
