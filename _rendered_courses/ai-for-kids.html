STEP-BY-STEP MODULES

Detailed Curriculum & Labs

1 Discovering AI 3 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Demystify AI and show how it recognizes patterns.
  • What AI is and where it is used
  • How computers learn (Machine Learning basics)
  • Playing with AI pattern recognition
Key Concepts: Machine LearningPattern RecognitionAI Bias
1 What is AI?
Taught: Definition of AI, real-world examples (Netflix, Siri).
Learner Lab: Identifies AI in their daily lives.
Why It Matters: Builds foundational understanding.
Expected Deliverable: Ability to define AI simply.
2 How Computers See: QuickDraw
Taught: Pattern recognition and neural networks.
Learner Lab: Plays QuickDraw and analyzes how the AI guesses their drawings.
Why It Matters: Shows that AI learns from lots of examples.
Expected Deliverable: Understanding of data-driven learning.
3 AI Mistakes (Bias and Errors)
Taught: Why AI sometimes gets things wrong.
Learner Lab: Reviews funny or interesting AI mistakes and discusses why they happened.
Why It Matters: Teaches critical thinking regarding technology.
Expected Deliverable: Critical awareness of AI limitations.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Play AI-based web games and record how they react.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit a short reflection on a time the AI guessed wrong in QuickDraw.
💼 Commercial Application: Understanding AI capabilities.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Solid foundational knowledge of what AI is.
2 Training Your Own AI 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Hands-on experience creating a machine learning model.
  • Data collection
  • Training a model
  • Testing and tweaking a model
Key Concepts: Data CollectionTrainingClassificationTesting
1 Meet Teachable Machine
Taught: The interface of Google Teachable Machine.
Learner Lab: Sets up their first project.
Why It Matters: It's the most accessible tool for creating real ML models.
Expected Deliverable: Familiarity with the tool.
2 The Sorting Hat: Image Classification
Taught: Collecting webcam data for different classes.
Learner Lab: Trains a model to distinguish between them holding an apple or a banana.
Why It Matters: Teaches how data inputs create outcomes.
Expected Deliverable: A working image classification model.
3 Improving Your Model
Taught: The importance of varied data (lighting, angles).
Learner Lab: Retrains their model with more diverse images to make it smarter.
Why It Matters: Teaches how to fix bias and improve accuracy.
Expected Deliverable: An improved, highly accurate model.
4 Sound and Poses
Taught: Training audio and pose models.
Learner Lab: Builds a model that recognizes clapping vs. snapping.
Why It Matters: Expands understanding of what data can be.
Expected Deliverable: A multi-modal understanding of ML.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Build a Rock-Paper-Scissors hand-pose recognizer.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit the link to a trained Teachable Machine model.
💼 Commercial Application: Model training pipelines.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Ability to train and test custom ML models.
3 Creating with AI (Generative AI) 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Explore how AI can generate text and art, focusing on prompt engineering.
  • What Generative AI is
  • Writing effective text prompts
  • Creating AI art
  • Ethics of AI creation
Key Concepts: Generative AIPrompt EngineeringAI EthicsDigital Citizenship
1 Talking to Bots: Prompt Engineering
Taught: How to ask an AI for exactly what you want.
Learner Lab: Practices getting a chatbot to write a story in the style of a pirate.
Why It Matters: A crucial skill for using future tools.
Expected Deliverable: Understanding of prompt modifiers.
2 AI Art Gallery
Taught: Using text-to-image generators.
Learner Lab: Generates images of imaginative creatures (e.g., 'a cat riding a skateboard on Mars').
Why It Matters: Shows the intersection of tech and art.
Expected Deliverable: A collection of AI-generated art.
3 Being Responsible with AI
Taught: Plagiarism, deepfakes, and responsible use.
Learner Lab: Discusses when it's okay to use AI for homework and when it isn't.
Why It Matters: Teaches digital citizenship.
Expected Deliverable: Strong ethical foundation.
4 Final AI Showcase
Taught: Presentation skills.
Learner Lab: Presents their favorite trained model or generated project to the class.
Why It Matters: Sharing knowledge cements learning.
Expected Deliverable: A final presentation.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Use AI to co-write a comic book script and generate the character art.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit the AI-assisted comic project.
💼 Commercial Application: Content generation and prompt engineering.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Ability to use Generative AI creatively and ethically.
