Brand Identity Design & Visual Systems is an intensive 8-week, 64-hour applied specialization focused on developing complete, research-informed visual identity systems for commercial businesses. Students learn client discovery, creative direction, logo families, typography, color calibration, supporting graphic devices, multi-touchpoint brand applications, and comprehensive brand guidelines, graduating with two complete assessed brand case studies.

This is an Applied Specialization requiring foundational vector-design knowledge. It is not an introductory software tool course. Learners should already understand basic Illustrator tools (shapes, Pen Tool, basic typography) so instruction can focus on strategic thinking, creative direction, systems design, and client-level brand delivery.

The course duration is 8 Weeks (64 guided learning hours across 6 modules), and the tuition fee is ₹10,900. Nova Skills maintains a transparent pricing policy with 0% fake discounts, no artificial comparison pricing, and no countdown timers.

Yes, basic vector design knowledge in Adobe Illustrator is required. Students should be comfortable creating documents, manipulating vector paths, using the Pathfinder tool, and handling type and color palettes before enrolling.

A logo is simply a primary identifying mark or signature. A visual brand identity is an entire sensory ecosystem comprising logo configurations, typography hierarchies, color proportions, graphic devices, imagery art direction, and compositional rules that ensure a brand is instantly recognizable even without its logo present.

Yes. Module 1 is dedicated to brand discovery, client questionnaires, stakeholder interviews, audience profiling, and translating business objectives into concrete visual design criteria.

Yes. Module 2 teaches you how to bridge strategy and visual aesthetics, establishing distinct conceptual visual territories, curating targeted moodboards, and formulating written creative rationales.

A visual territory is an explored conceptual direction that embodies a specific brand personality through a unified combination of typography, color palette, photographic mood, and graphic language before finalizing logo designs.

Yes. You will design cohesive logo families including primary lockups, secondary stacked or horizontal layouts, compact symbols, monochrome versions, and reversed treatments calibrated for diverse backgrounds and scales.

Yes. You will establish rigorous typography hierarchies (display, heading, body, caption) with leading and tracking rules, alongside calibrated color systems specifying exact HEX, RGB, CMYK, and spot color swatches.

Yes. Every brand identity system requires at least two custom supporting graphic devices, such as bespoke brand patterns, proprietary vector framing shapes, or custom icon styles that enrich brand touchpoints.

Yes. You will develop photography art direction guidelines specifying lighting style, subject composition, color grading, and treatment rules to ensure all future brand imagery aligns with the visual identity.

Yes. You will apply the identity system across at least five purposeful touchpoints, including stationery (business cards, letterhead, email signatures), digital marketing assets (social templates, web hero banners), and environmental collateral.

A brand-guidelines document (often 12–20 pages) is an authoritative manual that documents logo usage, exclusion zones, minimum sizes, approved color breakdowns, typography scales, graphic rules, application examples, and prohibited misuses.

Yes. You will learn professional asset packaging: organizing layers in Illustrator, creating global swatches, outlining type for production, exporting web-ready SVGs and PNGs, and structuring an 8-folder client handover archive.
