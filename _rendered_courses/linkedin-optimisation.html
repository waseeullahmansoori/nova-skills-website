STEP-BY-STEP MODULES

Detailed Curriculum & Labs

1 Profile SEO & Visual Branding 3 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Ensure you are easily found by recruiters and clients.
  • LinkedIn search algorithms.
  • Writing a high-converting headline.
  • Designing a profile banner.
  • Writing an engaging summary.
Key Concepts: Profile SEOFirst-person NarrativeVisual Branding
1 The Algorithm & Keywords
Taught: How recruiters search for candidates on LinkedIn.
Learner Lab: Identifies 5-10 core keywords for their target role.
Why It Matters: If you don't have the right keywords, you won't appear in searches.
Expected Deliverable: A keyword strategy.
2 Headlines & Visuals
Taught: Crafting the headline and setting up the banner/photo.
Learner Lab: Writes a new headline and designs a banner in Canva.
Why It Matters: The first impression viewers get before clicking your profile.
Expected Deliverable: Updated top-of-profile assets.
3 The 'About' Section Narrative
Taught: Writing a compelling summary in the first person.
Learner Lab: Drafts their 'About' section.
Why It Matters: Tells your professional story, not just a list of facts.
Expected Deliverable: A completed profile summary.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Update the top half of the LinkedIn profile.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit the new headline and About section for review.
💼 Commercial Application: Increasing search visibility and professional credibility.
🎯 Expected Outcome: A search-optimized profile foundation.
2 Experience & Network Building 3 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Flesh out your history and connect with decision-makers.
  • Formatting the Experience section.
  • Requesting recommendations.
  • Strategic connection requests.
Key Concepts: Social ProofConnection StrategyPersonalized Outreach
1 Optimizing Experience & Skills
Taught: Translating resume bullets into LinkedIn format and managing the Skills section.
Learner Lab: Updates their work history.
Why It Matters: Provides depth and validation to your headline.
Expected Deliverable: Completed Experience section.
2 Social Proof & Recommendations
Taught: How to ask for and give strong recommendations.
Learner Lab: Sends 3 recommendation requests.
Why It Matters: Builds trust with profile visitors.
Expected Deliverable: Active recommendation requests.
3 Strategic Connection Outreach
Taught: Finding target connections and writing personalized notes.
Learner Lab: Sends 10 personalized connection requests.
Why It Matters: Grows your network with relevant, valuable contacts.
Expected Deliverable: An expanding professional network.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Fully complete the profile to 'All-Star' status.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit a screenshot of an 'All-Star' profile rating.
💼 Commercial Application: Building a targeted audience for future opportunities.
🎯 Expected Outcome: A fully complete profile and an active networking strategy.
3 Engagement & Content Strategy 3 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Drive traffic to your profile through active participation.
  • Commenting strategies.
  • Creating text and visual posts.
  • Building a simple content calendar.
Key Concepts: Engagement StrategyContent CreationConsistency
1 The Power of Commenting
Taught: How to leave insightful comments on industry leaders' posts.
Learner Lab: Leaves 5 strategic comments.
Why It Matters: The easiest way to get visibility without creating original content.
Expected Deliverable: Increased profile views.
2 Creating Original Content
Taught: Structuring text posts and sharing professional updates.
Learner Lab: Drafts their first professional post.
Why It Matters: Establishes you as an active voice in your field.
Expected Deliverable: A published LinkedIn post.
3 Maintaining Consistency
Taught: Setting up a manageable routine for LinkedIn activity.
Learner Lab: Creates a 2-week engagement calendar.
Why It Matters: Prevents burnout while maintaining visibility.
Expected Deliverable: A sustainable LinkedIn routine.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Execute the engagement strategy for one week.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit a link to a published post and metrics on profile views.
💼 Commercial Application: Establishing thought leadership and inbound lead generation.
🎯 Expected Outcome: A habit of active professional engagement.
