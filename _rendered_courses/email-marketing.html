  • Why email remains the highest-ROI digital channel ($36-$42 return per $1 spent)
  • The permission marketing contract: providing value in exchange for inbox access
  • The truth about purchased lists: instant spam traps, domain blacklisting, and zero conversions

Module 02 Email Deliverability & Sender Authentication 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Master email deliverability mechanics, configure sender authentication protocols (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) in accordance with Google's sender guidelines, monitor domain reputation, and ensure RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe compliance.
  • The technical mechanics of SPF, DKIM, and DMARC domain authentication and alignment.
  • Current Google and Yahoo sender requirements: mandatory rules for all senders vs. bulk senders (5,000+/day).
  • How to monitor spam complaint rates, interpret Postmaster Tools data, and execute sunset list hygiene policies.
Key Concepts: Delivery vs. Inbox PlacementSPF, DKIM & DMARC AlignmentGoogle Sender Guidelines (0.1% Spam SLA)RFC 8058 One-Click UnsubscribeSunset List Hygiene
Lesson 2.1 How Email Delivery Works: Delivery vs. Inbox Placement
Taught: The journey of an email from sending server to recipient mailbox, understanding SMTP handshakes, spam filters, reputation algorithms, and why 'delivered' does not mean 'read'.
  • The email delivery pathway: MTA, DNS lookup, spam filter evaluation, inbox routing
  • Why emails land in Spam or Promotions tabs instead of Primary
  • Sender reputation as a credit score: how mailbox providers evaluate sender history
Learner Lab: Maps the technical path of an email and identifies 5 common points of delivery failure.
Why It Matters: Marketers who confuse delivery with inbox placement waste budget on emails that subscribers never see.
Expected Deliverable: An Email Delivery Pathway Diagram and Diagnostic Flowchart.
Lesson 2.2 Sender Authentication: SPF, DKIM & DMARC in Practice
Taught: Deep dive into the 3 core authentication protocols: authoring SPF TXT records, configuring DKIM keys in Mailchimp/Brevo, setting up DMARC records, and verifying domain alignment.
  • SPF syntax breakdown: 'v=spf1 include:servers.mcsv.net ~all'
  • DKIM CNAME record setup and public key verification
  • DMARC policy rollout: starting with 'p=none' to monitor and moving to 'p=quarantine'
Learner Lab: Inspects sample DNS records using MXToolbox and verifies SPF, DKIM, and DMARC syntax.
Why It Matters: Without SPF and DKIM, major mailbox providers reject or spam-flag emails immediately under 2024+ security rules.
Expected Deliverable: A DNS Authentication Verification Log.
Lesson 2.3 Google Sender Guidelines & Spam Rate Compliance
Taught: Auditing compliance against official Google Email Sender Guidelines, the 0.1% spam complaint ceiling, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe header rules, and Gmail Postmaster Tools.
  • The mandatory rules for all senders sending to personal Gmail accounts
  • Bulk sender rules (5,000+ daily messages): mandatory DMARC, one-click unsubscribe, and spam monitoring
  • Why keeping spam complaints below 0.1% (never exceeding 0.3%) is vital for survival
Learner Lab: Audits an email header for RFC 8058 List-Unsubscribe syntax and calculates spam complaint ratios from sample data.
Why It Matters: Exceeding a 0.3% spam complaint rate will cause Gmail to block or spam-route all domain emails.
Expected Deliverable: A Google Sender Guidelines Compliance Audit Checklist.
Lesson 2.4 Bounce Management, List Hygiene & Deliverability Auditing
Taught: Differentiating hard and soft bounces, configuring automated suppression, executing sunset policies for inactive subscribers, and compiling deliverability audit reports.
  • Hard bounce protocol: immediate suppression of invalid, deleted, or fake addresses
  • Sunset policies: sending re-engagement campaigns and purging users inactive for 90+ days
  • Pre-flight deliverability checklist before scheduling major marketing campaigns
Learner Lab: Prepares a comprehensive Deliverability & Sender Authentication Audit for a case-study business.
Why It Matters: Regular list hygiene prevents low engagement from degrading overall sender reputation.
Expected Deliverable: An Email Deliverability Audit & Remediation Plan.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Audit a sending domain using DNS diagnostic tools, verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC records, inspect unsubscribe headers, and produce a Deliverability Remediation Plan.

Lesson 5.1 Metric Taxonomy: Beyond Open Rates to Real Engagement

  • Benchmarking healthy rates: 15-25% CTOR and 2-5% CTR across standard commercial industries
