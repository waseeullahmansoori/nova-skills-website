Identify eligible Microsoft 365 subscription plans, prerequisite licenses, and administrative feature enablement criteria.

ACHIEVE

Understand work-data grounding principles and how Copilot utilizes authorized enterprise context versus web-grounded search.

ACHIEVE

Explain Microsoft Graph and Work IQ permission boundaries, tenant policies, and the risks associated with organizational data oversharing.

ACHIEVE

Write structured, professional workplace prompts incorporating task, context, source material, intended audience, constraints, and format.

ACHIEVE

Specify precise business context, objective parameters, and required deliverables in conversational AI interactions.

ACHIEVE

Reference authorized source documents, tables, and notes accurately within Copilot prompts across desktop and web applications.

ACHIEVE

Set explicit negative constraints, confidentiality limits, and formatting boundaries in Copilot instructions to minimize hallucinations.

ACHIEVE

Identify weak, vague, or unsupported AI outputs and systematically refine prompts through iterative engineering.

ACHIEVE

Draft structured business reports, proposals, memos, and executive summaries with Microsoft Word Copilot assistance.

ACHIEVE

Rewrite, rephrase, and edit draft business documentation for clarity, professional conciseness, and audience suitability in Word.

ACHIEVE

Summarize multi-page business reports, extract key operational findings, and identify unresolved strategic questions using Copilot in Word.

ACHIEVE

Validate AI-extracted information against original source notes, identifying omissions, exaggerations, and unsupported claims.

ACHIEVE

Correct factual errors, misattributed dates, and unsubstantiated conclusions in AI-assisted business documentation.

ACHIEVE

Use Copilot in Excel for formula assistance, dataset preparation, and structured table analysis across everyday business scenarios.

ACHIEVE

Interpret and explain AI-suggested Excel formulas including SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, IF, and XLOOKUP logic before applying them to workbooks.

ACHIEVE

Independently recalculate totals, sample rows, and boundary conditions to verify that Copilot-generated spreadsheet formulas are accurate.

ACHIEVE

Verify AI-generated spreadsheet insights, trends, and summary metrics against underlying raw records before reporting.

ACHIEVE

Create and refine structured presentation outlines in PowerPoint from meeting notes, project briefs, and Word source documents.

ACHIEVE

Review AI-generated slides for visual hierarchy, readability, data consistency, and alignment with source reference materials.

ACHIEVE

Check and maintain cross-application consistency in dates, metrics, and business recommendations across Word, Excel, and PowerPoint deliverables.

ACHIEVE

Draft professional, context-appropriate email replies and client communications using Copilot in Outlook.

ACHIEVE

Summarize lengthy email threads in Outlook to extract key stakeholder points, decisions, and required next steps.

ACHIEVE

Review and validate Microsoft Teams meeting recaps, automated notes, and AI-generated discussion summaries.

ACHIEVE

Verify action-item ownership, explicit participant commitments, and delivery deadlines extracted from meeting records.

ACHIEVE

Identify and eliminate hallucinated meeting decisions, false agreements, and unassigned task allocations in follow-up communications.

ACHIEVE

Explain Copilot licensing limitations, app client variations, and features requiring transcription versus no-transcription modes in Teams.

ACHIEVE

Protect confidential business data, employee privacy, and intellectual property by adhering to responsible AI and least-privilege sharing rules.

ACHIEVE

ACHIEVE
