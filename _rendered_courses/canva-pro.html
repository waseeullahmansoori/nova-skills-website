Canva Professional — Brand & Marketing Design is an intensive 4-week, 32-hour practical certification programme focused on visual hierarchy, typography, Brand Kits, original social media layouts, multi-slide carousels, 10-slide business presentations, reusable template systems, and responsible Canva AI workflows. Students learn to create marketing collateral starting from blank canvases rather than merely swapping text in premade templates, graduating with a commercial marketing toolkit backed by fully editable Canva source links.

Yes, this course is specifically structured for beginners including entrepreneurs, small business owners, educators, content creators, marketing assistants, and aspiring freelance designers. Training begins with foundational design principles—visual hierarchy, typography pairing, margins, spacing, alignment, and color harmony—ensuring learners develop solid graphic design fundamentals before creating complex marketing collateral.

The course also teaches equivalent manual style workflows for learners utilizing free Canva accounts.

Yes, typography and layout are central pillars of the curriculum. You will master font pairing, scale hierarchy, leading, tracking, optical alignment, grid-based composition, and intentional whitespace to ensure every marketing design communicates clearly and looks professionally art-directed.

Yes. Module 4 is dedicated to business presentations. You will learn slide architecture, executive narrative pacing, clean typographic hierarchy, custom data visualizations, process workflows, and timeline diagrams in 16:9 widescreen format.
