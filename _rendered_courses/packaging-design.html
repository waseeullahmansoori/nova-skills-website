Packaging Artwork & Print-Ready Design

Learn how to design professional retail and direct-to-consumer packaging that combines strong visual communication with reliable production artwork. This practical Packaging Artwork & Print-Ready Design course develops hands-on skills in product research, packaging graphics, dieline interpretation, multi-panel artwork, label typography, print preparation and realistic packaging visualization. Work with supplier-approved dielines to understand cut lines, crease and fold lines, bleed areas, safe margins, glue zones, panel orientation and physical dimensions. Learn how to organize product information, create clear hierarchy, position barcodes, develop consistent brand graphics and prepare label layouts with appropriate regulatory-content review checkpoints. Develop technical skills in CMYK and spot-color fundamentals, overprint awareness, transparency, image resolution, PDF output, color separation previews and Acrobat Pro preflight. Explore the visual effects of paperboard, flexible packaging, labels, coatings, matte and gloss finishes, foil effects and other common packaging treatments while understanding their production limitations. Create realistic packaging mockups and learn how soft proofs, physical samples, supplier feedback and artwork revisions work together in a commercial production workflow. Build a professional portfolio containing an FMCG food label with an illustrative compliance checklist, a cosmetic folding-carton artwork project using an approved dieline, and a premium D2C packaging project with organized print artwork, realistic mockups and a technical preflight report. The programme emphasizes precise artwork, commercially relevant design decisions, responsible labeling practices and professional supplier handover. It does not qualify students to certify regulatory compliance, engineer packaging structures or approve industrial manufacturing specifications.
