STEP-BY-STEP MODULES

Detailed Curriculum & Labs

Module 01 E-Commerce Strategy & Platform Setup 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Understand the digital retail landscape and configure your first staging environments.
  • Evaluate business models (Dropshipping, D2C, B2B, Digital).
  • Set up Shopify Partner and WordPress local environments.
  • Configure foundational settings: currencies, timezones, and legal pages.
Key Concepts: SaaS vs Open SourceMerchant complianceDNS configuration
1.1 The Anatomy of a Modern Online Store
Taught: Core components of e-commerce: frontend, backend, gateways, and logistics.
Learner Lab: Maps out a store architecture diagram for their planned project.
Why It Matters: Provides the structural blueprint needed before clicking any buttons.
Expected Deliverable: A documented technical brief for the storefront.
1.2 Initializing Shopify & WooCommerce
Taught: Account creation, dashboard navigation, and basic configuration.
Learner Lab: Creates a Shopify developer store and installs WordPress + WooCommerce locally.
Why It Matters: Familiarity with the primary control centers of both platforms.
Expected Deliverable: Two blank, accessible store environments.
1.3 Legal, Trust, and Policy Pages
Taught: Generating Terms of Service, Privacy Policies, and Return Policies.
Learner Lab: Drafts and links footer policy pages using built-in generators.
Why It Matters: Payment gateways require these pages before approving merchant accounts.
Expected Deliverable: Fully compliant informational pages published.
1.4 Domain Mapping and SSL setup
Taught: Connecting custom domains and ensuring HTTPS encryption.
Learner Lab: Configures DNS records to point to their storefronts.
Why It Matters: Crucial for brand legitimacy and securing customer data.
Expected Deliverable: Store accessible via a custom secure URL.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Setup a Shopify partner account and link a custom domain.
📝 Practical Assignment: Deliver a configuration audit report of the newly created store.
💼 Commercial Application: First step in onboarding any new retail client.
🎯 Expected Outcome: A foundational store ready for product catalog integration.
Module 02 Product Catalogs, Variants & Inventory Logic 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Master the complex data structures required to manage physical and digital goods.
  • Create simple, variable, and grouped products.
  • Manage SKUs, barcodes, and inventory tracking.
  • Organize products using collections, categories, and tags.
Key Concepts: SKU architectureVariant logicTaxonomy
2.1 Building the Product Database
Taught: Data entry for titles, descriptions, pricing, and SEO metadata.
Learner Lab: Manually adds 10 diverse products with rich text descriptions.
Why It Matters: Clean product data is the foundation of site search and conversion.
Expected Deliverable: A populated product grid.
2.2 Mastering Variants & Options
Taught: Setting up size, color, and material variations with conditional pricing.
Learner Lab: Configures a complex apparel product with 12 distinct variants and specific images.
Why It Matters: Prevents database bloat while giving customers accurate choices.
Expected Deliverable: A functional product page with dynamic variant switching.
2.3 Collections and Taxonomy
Taught: Automated vs Manual collections in Shopify; Categories in WooCommerce.
Learner Lab: Builds automated collections based on pricing rules and tags.
Why It Matters: Dictates how users navigate and discover items in the store.
Expected Deliverable: Structured navigation menus linked to smart categories.
2.4 Bulk Importing & CSV Management
Taught: Structuring CSV files to upload hundreds of products instantly.
Learner Lab: Formats and imports a dummy CSV with 50 products.
Why It Matters: Essential for migrating clients from legacy platforms.
Expected Deliverable: Mass product population without manual data entry.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Importing a supplier catalog and structuring the variant options.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit a store with a properly nested category tree and 50+ imported products.
💼 Commercial Application: Managing inventory databases for mid-sized retailers.
🎯 Expected Outcome: A robust, logically structured product catalog.
Module 03 Storefront Design & Conversion UX 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Design visually appealing interfaces optimized for buying behavior.
  • Customize Shopify OS 2.0 Themes.
  • Build custom WooCommerce layouts with Elementor.
  • Implement conversion-boosting UI elements (scarcity, reviews, trust badges).
Key Concepts: Above-the-fold optimizationResponsive designSocial proof integration
3.1 Shopify Theme Architecture
Taught: Using the drag-and-drop customizer, sections, and blocks.
Learner Lab: Customizes the homepage, typography, and color schemes of a premium theme.
Why It Matters: Allows rapid visual branding without touching Liquid code.
Expected Deliverable: A branded, visually cohesive storefront.
3.2 High-Converting Product Pages
Taught: Layout principles for the 'Buy Box', image galleries, and social proof.
Learner Lab: Restructures the product template to prioritize the Add to Cart button and reviews.
Why It Matters: The product page is where the actual purchasing decision occurs.
Expected Deliverable: An optimized PDP (Product Detail Page).
3.3 WooCommerce & Elementor Integration
Taught: Overriding default WordPress shop pages using Elementor Theme Builder.
Learner Lab: Designs a custom single product template from scratch in Elementor.
Why It Matters: Breaks free from rigid WordPress theme limitations.
Expected Deliverable: A completely bespoke WooCommerce product layout.
3.4 Mobile First Commerce
Taught: Auditing and adjusting touch targets, sticky headers, and mobile navigation.
Learner Lab: Performs a mobile UI audit and adjusts responsive breakpoints.
Why It Matters: Majority of consumer transactions happen on mobile devices.
Expected Deliverable: A seamless smartphone shopping experience.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Design a homepage and product page mockup, then implement it.
📝 Practical Assignment: Deliver a fully branded store matching a specific client mood board.
💼 Commercial Application: Providing UI/UX redesigns for underperforming e-commerce brands.
🎯 Expected Outcome: A professional, consumer-ready storefront design.
Module 04 Checkout, Payments & Shipping Logistics 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Configure the complex mechanics of getting paid and delivering goods.
  • Integrate Stripe, PayPal, and regional gateways (e.g., Razorpay).
  • Configure weight-based, price-based, and carrier-calculated shipping rates.
  • Set up tax jurisdictions and automated calculation.
Key Concepts: Payment tokenizationFulfillment workflowsTax liability
4.1 Payment Gateway Integration
Taught: Connecting payment processors, handling API keys, and test modes.
Learner Lab: Connects Stripe and PayPal, and processes 5 test transactions.
Why It Matters: Without gateways, a store cannot capture revenue.
Expected Deliverable: A store capable of simulating credit card captures.
4.2 Advanced Shipping Matrix Setup
Taught: Creating shipping profiles, local delivery zones, and free shipping thresholds.
Learner Lab: Configures domestic flat rates, international weight-based tiers, and local pickup.
Why It Matters: Incorrect shipping rates destroy profit margins or deter buyers.
Expected Deliverable: Accurate shipping costs calculated at checkout.
4.3 Tax Compliance and Automation
Taught: Setting up tax rules based on regional laws (VAT, GST, State Sales Tax).
Learner Lab: Configures automated tax overrides for specific states/countries.
Why It Matters: Prevents massive legal and financial liabilities for merchants.
Expected Deliverable: Dynamic tax calculation displayed at checkout.
4.4 Post-Purchase Operations
Taught: Customizing order confirmation emails, packing slips, and fulfillment flows.
Learner Lab: Edits transactional email templates and fulfills a test order.
Why It Matters: Professional post-purchase communication reduces buyer's remorse.
Expected Deliverable: An automated customer notification pipeline.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Execute an end-to-end test order from add-to-cart to shipping fulfillment.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit video evidence of a successful checkout flow capturing simulated funds and taxes.
💼 Commercial Application: Configuring the operational backbone of commercial retail businesses.
🎯 Expected Outcome: A technically sound transactional engine.
Module 05 Marketing Tech, SEO & Analytics 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Install the tracking tools and growth mechanisms needed to scale the store.
  • Implement Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel for e-commerce.
  • Optimize product pages for organic search (SEO).
  • Set up abandoned cart recovery and email marketing.
Key Concepts: Conversion trackingAverage Order Value (AOV)Organic Acquisition
5.1 E-Commerce SEO Fundamentals
Taught: Keyword mapping, meta descriptions, image alt tags, and URL structures.
Learner Lab: Optimizes 10 products with targeted keywords and meta data.
Why It Matters: Drives free organic traffic to product pages over the long term.
Expected Deliverable: Search-engine ready product pages.
5.2 Analytics and Conversion Tracking
Taught: Installing GA4 and configuring purchase event tracking.
Learner Lab: Installs tracking pixels and verifies purchase events in Google Analytics.
Why It Matters: You cannot scale an e-commerce business without knowing your acquisition costs.
Expected Deliverable: Live telemetry monitoring user behavior.
5.3 Abandoned Cart & Retention
Taught: Connecting Klaviyo/Mailchimp to trigger automated recovery emails.
Learner Lab: Designs and activates a 3-step abandoned cart email sequence.
Why It Matters: Recovers up to 15% of lost revenue automatically.
Expected Deliverable: An automated revenue recovery pipeline.
5.4 App Ecosystems and Extending Functionality
Taught: Selecting and installing apps for upsells, subscriptions, and reviews.
Learner Lab: Installs a product review app and an in-cart upsell widget.
Why It Matters: Enhances Average Order Value (AOV) and customer lifetime value.
Expected Deliverable: An optimized storefront engineered for higher order values.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Set up an email marketing sequence and link it to store checkout data.
📝 Practical Assignment: Deliver a fully integrated store with active tracking pixels and a documented SEO strategy.
💼 Commercial Application: Acting as a growth partner for retail clients, not just a web designer.
🎯 Expected Outcome: A growth-ready, fully tracked e-commerce platform ready for live traffic.
