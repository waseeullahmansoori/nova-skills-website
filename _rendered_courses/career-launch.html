STEP-BY-STEP MODULES

Detailed Curriculum & Labs

1 Professional Branding & Resume Building 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Create the core assets needed to apply for roles.
  • Understanding ATS systems.
  • Writing impactful resume bullet points.
  • Structuring cover letters.
  • Building a portfolio.
Key Concepts: ATS OptimizationQuantifiable MetricsProfessional Narrative
1 Beating the ATS
Taught: How Applicant Tracking Systems parse resumes.
Learner Lab: Formats a base resume document.
Why It Matters: Ensures your resume is actually read by a human.
Expected Deliverable: An ATS-compliant document structure.
2 Quantifying Achievements
Taught: Shifting from duty-based to achievement-based writing.
Learner Lab: Rewrites experience bullets using the XYZ formula.
Why It Matters: Proves your value to employers.
Expected Deliverable: Impactful resume content.
3 Cover Letters that Get Read
Taught: Writing concise, compelling cover letters.
Learner Lab: Drafts a master cover letter template.
Why It Matters: Provides context for career transitions or special circumstances.
Expected Deliverable: A flexible cover letter framework.
4 Portfolio Development
Taught: Showcasing work samples visually.
Learner Lab: Assembles a digital portfolio in Canva.
Why It Matters: Provides tangible proof of skills.
Expected Deliverable: A professional portfolio link.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Complete the master resume and run it through an ATS scanner.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit the finalized resume and cover letter for feedback.
💼 Commercial Application: Presenting a highly professional candidate profile.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Application-ready core documents.
2 LinkedIn Optimization & Networking 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Build digital presence and tap into the hidden job market.
  • Optimizing LinkedIn for recruiter search.
  • Building a professional network.
  • Conducting informational interviews.
Key Concepts: Profile SEOHidden Job MarketPipeline Management
1 LinkedIn Profile Optimization
Taught: Structuring headlines, summaries, and experience for SEO.
Learner Lab: Updates their entire LinkedIn profile.
Why It Matters: Makes you discoverable to headhunters.
Expected Deliverable: An All-Star LinkedIn profile.
2 Strategic Networking
Taught: Identifying and connecting with key industry players.
Learner Lab: Maps out a list of target connections.
Why It Matters: Many jobs are filled through referrals before being posted.
Expected Deliverable: A targeted networking strategy.
3 The Informational Interview
Taught: Requesting and conducting informational chats.
Learner Lab: Drafts outreach messages for informational interviews.
Why It Matters: Builds relationships that lead to internal referrals.
Expected Deliverable: Outreach templates.
4 Job Search Organization
Taught: Managing the application pipeline.
Learner Lab: Sets up a job tracker in Notion.
Why It Matters: Maintains momentum and prevents missed follow-ups.
Expected Deliverable: A functional application CRM.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Execute 10 networking outreach messages.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit LinkedIn profile URL and job tracker setup.
💼 Commercial Application: Proactively sourcing career opportunities.
🎯 Expected Outcome: An active networking strategy and organized search process.
3 Interview Mastery 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Excel in verbal communication and technical assessments.
  • Mastering the STAR method.
  • Handling behavioral questions.
  • Preparing for technical rounds.
  • Asking the right questions.
Key Concepts: STAR MethodBehavioral InterviewingElevator Pitch
1 The STAR Method
Taught: Structuring behavioral answers (Situation, Task, Action, Result).
Learner Lab: Drafts 5 core stories using STAR.
Why It Matters: Provides clear, impactful, and concise answers.
Expected Deliverable: A library of behavioral stories.
2 Navigating the Screener Call
Taught: Handling the initial HR phone screen.
Learner Lab: Practices the 'Tell me about yourself' pitch.
Why It Matters: The critical first gate to pass.
Expected Deliverable: A polished elevator pitch.
3 Handling Difficult Questions
Taught: Addressing gaps, weaknesses, or transitions.
Learner Lab: Scripts responses to challenging questions.
Why It Matters: Turns potential red flags into neutral or positive points.
Expected Deliverable: Prepared answers for difficult topics.
4 Questions for the Interviewer
Taught: Formulating insightful questions to ask at the end.
Learner Lab: Compiles a list of high-level questions.
Why It Matters: Demonstrates engagement and strategic thinking.
Expected Deliverable: A custom question bank.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Record video responses to common interview questions.
📝 Practical Assignment: Complete a mock interview and submit the recording.
💼 Commercial Application: Effectively communicating value under pressure.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Confidence and competence in interview settings.
4 Offer Evaluation & Negotiation 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Secure the best possible compensation and terms.
  • Evaluating total compensation.
  • Researching market rates.
  • Negotiation strategies.
  • Handling multiple offers.
Key Concepts: Total CompensationMarket BenchmarkingNegotiation Scripts
1 Understanding Total Compensation
Taught: Analyzing salary, equity, bonuses, and benefits.
Learner Lab: Breaks down a sample offer letter.
Why It Matters: Base salary is only one part of the package.
Expected Deliverable: Ability to evaluate full offer value.
2 Market Rate Research
Taught: Finding accurate compensation data.
Learner Lab: Researches salary bands for target roles.
Why It Matters: Provides leverage for negotiation.
Expected Deliverable: A targeted salary range.
3 The Art of Negotiation
Taught: Scripting and executing a salary counter-offer.
Learner Lab: Drafts a negotiation email/script.
Why It Matters: Can significantly increase starting compensation.
Expected Deliverable: A negotiation strategy.
4 Onboarding & First 30 Days
Taught: Setting yourself up for success in the new role.
Learner Lab: Creates a 30-day success plan.
Why It Matters: Early impressions dictate long-term trajectory.
Expected Deliverable: An integration roadmap.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Roleplay a negotiation scenario.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit a market research report and negotiation script.
💼 Commercial Application: Maximizing financial outcomes and professional start.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Preparedness to accept and thrive in a new role.
