Who Is This Program Built For?

✓ Ideal For (Best For)

  • ✓Children aged 8–12 curious about video games, cartoons, and storytelling
  • ✓Young beginners with no prior coding or programming experience
  • ✓Kids who want to move from playing games to understanding and creating their own
  • ✓Parents looking for a creative, child-safe, logic-building screen activity
  • ✓Students preparing for future school computer science and robotics curricula

✕ Who Is This NOT For

  • ✕Older students or teenagers seeking text-based Python, JavaScript, or C++ courses
  • ✕Learners expecting professional 3D commercial game engine development (Unity / Unreal)
  • ✕Children unable to use a computer mouse or basic keyboard navigation
  • ✕Those looking for quick commercial monetization or app-store game publishing

Required Prerequisites

Basic computer literacy and internet access.

Recommended Background

Curiosity and enthusiasm for hands-on project creation.
