STEP-BY-STEP MODULES

Detailed Curriculum & Labs

1 Conquering Stage Fright 3 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Understand and manage the physical and mental symptoms of anxiety.
  • The psychology of fear.
  • Breathing and grounding techniques.
  • Reframing nervousness as excitement.
Key Concepts: Adrenaline ManagementDiaphragmatic BreathingReframing
1 Understanding Speaking Anxiety
Taught: Why we fear speaking and how adrenaline affects the body.
Learner Lab: Journaling exercise on personal speaking fears.
Why It Matters: Understanding the biology helps demystify the fear.
Expected Deliverable: Self-awareness of anxiety triggers.
2 Physical Grounding Techniques
Taught: Diaphragmatic breathing, posture, and muscle relaxation.
Learner Lab: Practice breathing exercises before a short recording.
Why It Matters: Calms the nervous system before and during a speech.
Expected Deliverable: Tools to reduce physical symptoms of stress.
3 Mental Reframing
Taught: Shifting focus from 'performing' to 'sharing value'.
Learner Lab: Deliver a 1-minute low-stakes speech on a passionate topic.
Why It Matters: Takes the pressure off being perfect.
Expected Deliverable: Increased comfort speaking out loud.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Short, low-pressure video recordings.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit a 2-minute video on 'Why I want to improve my speaking', focusing on relaxed breathing.
💼 Commercial Application: Staying calm during high-stakes presentations.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Reduced anxiety and better emotional control.
2 Vocal and Physical Command 3 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Use your voice and body to command attention.
  • Eliminating filler words.
  • Vocal projection and pausing.
  • Stage movement and gestures.
Key Concepts: PausingVocal VarietyAnchoring
1 The Power of the Pause
Taught: Replacing 'um' and 'uh' with silence.
Learner Lab: Record a speech, analyze with Otter.ai for fillers, and re-record.
Why It Matters: Silence projects confidence; filler words project uncertainty.
Expected Deliverable: Cleaner speech delivery.
2 Vocal Variety
Taught: Adjusting volume, pitch, and speed to maintain interest.
Learner Lab: Read a text using extreme variations in voice, then find a natural balance.
Why It Matters: Monotone delivery is the fastest way to lose an audience.
Expected Deliverable: Dynamic vocal range.
3 Gestures and Movement
Taught: Purposeful hand gestures and 'anchoring' on stage (or in frame).
Learner Lab: Deliver a speech focusing solely on coordinated hand gestures.
Why It Matters: Random movement distracts; purposeful movement emphasizes.
Expected Deliverable: Controlled and effective body language.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Vocal drills and transcript analysis.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit a 3-minute video demonstrating vocal variety and purposeful pausing.
💼 Commercial Application: Holding attention in long meetings or conferences.
🎯 Expected Outcome: A strong, engaging speaking presence.
3 Impromptu Speaking & Q&A 3 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Learn to think on your feet and handle unexpected situations.
  • Frameworks for impromptu answers.
  • Handling difficult questions.
  • Buying time elegantly.
Key Concepts: PREP FrameworkBridgingRecovery Techniques
1 Impromptu Frameworks
Taught: PREP (Point, Reason, Example, Point) for quick structuring.
Learner Lab: Answer random prompt questions using PREP.
Why It Matters: Prevents rambling when put on the spot.
Expected Deliverable: Structured spontaneous speaking.
2 Handling the Q&A
Taught: Repeating questions, bridging, and dealing with hostile questions.
Learner Lab: Live Q&A simulation with peers.
Why It Matters: Q&A can make or break a speaker's credibility.
Expected Deliverable: Grace under pressure.
3 Buying Time & Recovering
Taught: What to do when you lose your train of thought.
Learner Lab: Practice recovery phrases ('Let me take a step back...').
Why It Matters: Mistakes happen; recovery is what matters.
Expected Deliverable: Resilience during speaking.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Live impromptu drills.
📝 Practical Assignment: Participate in a live impromptu speaking session graded by the instructor.
💼 Commercial Application: Excelling in media interviews, panels, and unscripted meetings.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Confidence in unscripted environments.
4 The Keynote Delivery 3 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Prepare and deliver a polished, extended presentation.
  • Speech preparation techniques.
  • Connecting with the audience.
  • Final delivery.
Key Concepts: Extemporaneous SpeakingAudience Connection
1 Preparation Without Memorization
Taught: Using mind maps and bullet points instead of full scripts.
Learner Lab: Create a speech outline using only 10 keywords.
Why It Matters: Creates a conversational, authentic delivery.
Expected Deliverable: Ability to speak from concepts, not scripts.
2 Audience Interaction
Taught: Asking rhetorical questions, eye contact scanning, and reading the room.
Learner Lab: Incorporate 3 interaction techniques into their final speech draft.
Why It Matters: Makes the audience feel involved.
Expected Deliverable: Engaging delivery style.
3 The Final Performance
Taught: Bringing together mindset, vocal variety, body language, and structure.
Learner Lab: Deliver the final 10-minute keynote.
Why It Matters: The culmination of the specialization.
Expected Deliverable: A complete, professional speech delivery.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Rehearsing and refining the final speech.
📝 Practical Assignment: Deliver a 10-minute keynote speech (live on Zoom or recorded via Loom).
💼 Commercial Application: Delivering major presentations, town halls, or conference talks.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Mastery of public speaking.
