Module 02 Boolean Job Feed Curation & The Connects Economy 3 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Filter thousands of daily job posts down to the top 1% of high-probability, high-paying client contracts.
  • Use Boolean search operators to create targeted, noise-free saved job searches.
  • Audit client hiring history to avoid low-paying or demanding clients.
  • Manage and optimize Connects spending for maximum return on investment.
Key Concepts: Boolean search syntaxClient hire rate evaluationConnects optimizationRisk screening rubrics
2.1 Boolean Search Mastery & Noise Elimination
Taught: Constructing search strings using AND, OR, NOT, title filters, and category exclusions.
Learner Lab: Builds and saves 3 targeted search feeds filtering for budget, client spend, and verified payment.
Why It Matters: Saves 2 hours of daily manual browsing and surfaces high-value jobs instantly.
Expected Deliverable: 3 automated, saved Upwork job search feeds.
2.2 The Client Vetting Rubric: Spotting Red & Green Flags
Taught: Analyzing client feedback history, average hourly rate paid, hire rate percentage, and review patterns.
Learner Lab: Audits 10 active job postings against the 6-point client risk rubric.
Why It Matters: Protects your Job Success Score by screening out unreasonable clients before applying.
Expected Deliverable: Scored Client Audit Worksheet separating ideal clients from red flag posts.
2.3 Connects Budgeting & The Boosting Strategy
Taught: When to boost proposals, how many Connects to invest, and when to avoid boosted bidding.
Learner Lab: Creates a monthly Connects allocation budget and bidding criteria checklist.
Why It Matters: Prevents wasting expensive Connects on crowded jobs with low conversion probability.
Expected Deliverable: Connects ROI Strategy & Bidding Decision Tree.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Filter the live job feed to find 5 prime candidate jobs posted within the last 2 hours.
📝 Practical Assignment: Deliver the Saved Search Feeds & Client Screening Checklist.
💼 Commercial Application: Focuses bidding efforts exclusively on verified, high-paying clients with proven hiring habits.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Automated workflow feeding high-probability leads daily.
