Learn to create and manage WooCommerce stores on WordPress. Learn how an online store works from product listing and checkout to payment handling, shipping configuration, inventory updates, refunds and daily administration.

⚡ Why Learn This Now? 2026/27 Industry Shift

Modern e-commerce requires full ownership over customer data, transaction workflows, and order architectures. With High-Performance Order Storage (HPOS) and block-based checkout, WooCommerce delivers scalable commerce on independent infrastructure.

AI tools accelerate product catalogue drafting and customer service template creation, but human operational rigor is essential for inventory reconciliation, tax accuracy, and payment security.
