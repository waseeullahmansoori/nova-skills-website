STEP-BY-STEP MODULES

Detailed Curriculum & Labs

1 Generative AI & LLM Fundamentals 3 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Deconstruct how large language models generate text, understand the implications of tokens, context windows, and stochastic behavior, and establish practical safety, privacy, and responsible AI guardrails.
Key Topics Covered (13):
  • Introduction to artificial intelligence & practical generative AI ecosystem overview
  • How large language models work: next-token prediction, tokenization mechanics, and probabilistic text generation
  • Model training phases (pre-training, fine-tuning, RLHF) versus real-time inference
  • Context windows and token constraints: understanding input limits, output limits, and context decay
  • Instruction hierarchy fundamentals: system instructions versus user-supplied queries versus external data
  • Frontier model landscape in 2027: core capabilities, design philosophies, and architectural differences between ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini
  • Model capabilities versus realistic constraints: why LLMs are reasoning engines, not deterministic databases
  • Hallucinations, factual inaccuracies, and the critical distinction between model confidence and objective correctness
  • Output variability and non-deterministic behavior: understanding temperature, top-p, and stochastic generation
  • Selecting the right model for specific business tasks: speed, context size, cost, reasoning depth, and multimodal needs
  • Tool-augmented models (browsing, code execution) versus parametric model memory
  • Responsible AI practices: data privacy, avoiding confidential enterprise disclosures, intellectual property, and platform acceptable use policies
  • Recognizing bias, harmful outputs, and establishing the necessity of human oversight for high-stakes decisions
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Conducting token-budget calculations, observing output variations across multiple runs of the same prompt, and testing model knowledge cutoffs versus tool-assisted live retrieval.
📝 Practical Assignment: AI Model Capability & Limitation Analysis: A comparative diagnostic report evaluating ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini across three representative business tasks (factual research, creative synthesis, and document summarization), documenting output differences, accuracy observations, and tool selection rationales.
2 Professional Prompt Design 3 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Master the 7-step Professional Prompt Framework to design unambiguous, role-calibrated, reusable prompts utilizing zero-shot, one-shot, and few-shot in-context learning.
Key Topics Covered (14):
  • Prompt engineering as a software-like discipline: why vague questions produce mediocre results
  • Core prompt components: clear task directives, persona/role definition, audience specification, and explicit constraints
  • The 7-Step Professional Prompt Framework: Objective → Context → Inputs → Constraints → Output Specification → Success Criteria → Evaluation
  • Separating instructions from input data using clear structural delimiters (markdown tags, triple quotes, XML-style blocks)
  • Zero-shot prompting: when direct instructions work and where they break down
  • One-shot and few-shot in-context learning: selecting diverse, high-quality positive examples to calibrate tone, structure, and depth
  • Positive guidance versus negative constraints: why telling the model what to do outperforms long lists of prohibitions
  • Designing reusable prompt templates with variable placeholders (e.g., [CLIENT_NAME], [INDUSTRY], [SOURCE_TEXT])
  • Iterative prompt refinement: testing baseline instructions, analyzing shortcomings, and systematically adding missing constraints
  • Handling ambiguous user requests: crafting prompts that ask clarifying questions before generating final answers
  • Deconstructing complex multifaceted business requests into logical sequential instructions
  • Managing instruction conflicts and edge cases: ensuring constraints take precedence over creative elaboration
  • Documenting prompts for team reuse: recording intent, required context, and expected output parameters
  • Cross-model portability principles: engineering prompts that function predictably across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini without heavy model-specific rewriting
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Refining a poorly worded 1-line business request through 4 progressive iterations using the 7-step framework, then converting it into a standardized few-shot template with variable placeholders.
📝 Practical Assignment: Reusable Business Prompt Library Starter: Crafting, testing, and documenting eight standardized business prompts (customer email drafting, executive summaries, marketing campaign briefs, product descriptions, meeting action items, educational explanations, report outlines, and FAQ generators) with complete input-output logs.
3 Advanced Prompt Workflows, Context Engineering & Structured Outputs 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Engineer precise context to anchor model responses in verified source data, design schema-constrained JSON outputs, handle multimodal inputs, and implement prompt-injection defenses.
Key Topics Covered (16):
  • Context engineering fundamentals: the critical role of relevant context in determining output quality
  • Curating, filtering, and organizing context: minimizing noise, preventing 'lost in the middle' phenomena, and respecting token budgets
  • Document-grounded prompting (Retrieval-Augmented Prompting): constraining the model strictly to supplied reference material
  • Source attribution and citation techniques: instructing models to cite specific paragraph numbers or direct quotes
  • Preventing hallucinated extrapolation: crafting strict fallback instructions when answers are not present in the source text
  • Structured output fundamentals: understanding key-value pairs, arrays, nested objects, and data typing in JSON
  • JSON Schema principles: defining required versus optional fields, string enums, number boundaries, and object structures
  • The Critical Triad: Distinguishing Valid JSON Syntax vs. Schema-Conforming JSON vs. Factually Correct JSON Values
  • Data extraction workflows: transforming messy unstructured emails, PDFs, and meeting transcripts into clean JSON entities
  • Classification prompts: multi-class categorization, sentiment scoring, and urgency tagging with strict schema enums
  • Multimodal prompt architecture: analyzing screenshots, invoices, data charts, and diagrams using vision-capable LLMs
  • Multi-step prompt workflows and task decomposition: breaking complex analysis into sequential staging prompts
  • Intermediate verification: validating step-1 outputs before feeding them into step-2 prompt stages
  • Prompt injection and untrusted input defense: understanding indirect prompt injection inside user documents, emails, and web pages
  • Defensive prompting strategies: treating external text strictly as passive data and shielding system instructions from override attacks
  • Enterprise data confidentiality: redacting PII, masking sensitive client numbers, and ethical source data governance
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Extracting invoice line-items from a synthetic receipt image into validated JSON, and testing indirect prompt injection vulnerabilities by embedding malicious override commands inside source text.
📝 Practical Assignment: Structured AI Output Portfolio: Five complete, validated structured-output deliverables (customer enquiry classification JSON, synthetic invoice information extraction JSON, meeting notes action-item JSON, product catalog attribute JSON, and educational reporting JSON) with validation logs and factual accuracy checks.
4 Prompt Evaluation & Output Optimization 3 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Implement scientific, empirical evaluation methodologies using 5-dimension scoring rubrics, test case suites, failure categorization, and cross-model comparison workbooks.
Key Topics Covered (17):
  • Why evaluation is the cornerstone of professional prompt engineering: moving beyond subjective 'looks good to me' impressions
  • The challenge of non-deterministic model behavior: evaluating outputs across repeated runs and edge-case inputs
  • Dissecting prompt quality versus response quality: isolating prompt ambiguity from underlying model limitations
  • Constructing representative test case suites: standard inputs, complex multi-intent requests, and deliberate edge cases
  • Defining ground-truth references and acceptable output variance in open-ended language tasks
  • Developing standardized scoring rubrics: the Nova Skills 5-Dimension Evaluation Matrix (0–5 per dimension, 25 points maximum)
  • Dimension 1 — Accuracy: factual correctness, source alignment, and absence of hallucinations
  • Dimension 2 — Instruction Adherence: strict compliance with all specified constraints, negative rules, and persona guidelines
  • Dimension 3 — Completeness: addressing all requested points without omitting critical sub-tasks
  • Dimension 4 — Format Compliance: adherence to JSON schemas, markdown headers, character counts, or bullet layouts
  • Dimension 5 — Practical Usefulness: commercial relevance, clarity, tone appropriateness, and client readiness
  • Categorizing AI failure modes: hallucination, instruction drift, over-refusal, truncation, formatting collapse, and tone mismatch
  • Empirical model comparison protocols: establishing fair, identical testing conditions across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini
  • Tracking test metadata: recording model version, test date, system settings (temperature/tool access), and prompt revisions
  • Before-and-after prompt iteration testing: measuring quantitative score improvements following targeted prompt adjustments
  • Prompt regression awareness: ensuring revisions that fix edge case A do not break baseline capability B
  • Prompt version control and repository management: organizing enterprise prompt libraries with changelogs in Google Sheets
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Evaluating 15 model outputs against a synthetic business customer complaint dataset using the 5-dimension scoring rubric, identifying failure patterns, and calculating mean quality scores.
📝 Practical Assignment: AI Model Comparison & Evaluation Workbook: A comprehensive Google Sheets evaluation matrix testing at least five diverse business tasks across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini (or documented benchmark outputs), featuring quantitative 25-point rubric scoring, failure analysis, and model selection recommendations.
5 Business Applications, Prompt Libraries & Capstone 3 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Apply advanced prompting and evaluation techniques to real-world business productivity scenarios, organize an enterprise-ready prompt library, and build an evaluated business workflow capstone.
Key Topics Covered (13):
  • Mapping business productivity opportunities: where prompt engineering delivers immediate operational ROI
  • Customer operations: multi-turn enquiry classification, empathetic complaint resolution, and personalized follow-up drafting
  • Marketing & commercial copy: creative campaign briefs, audience avatar persona generation, and multi-channel content variations
  • Executive research & business intelligence: synthesizing lengthy market reports, competitor teardowns, and spreadsheet-ready tabular data
  • Training & educational operations: automated lesson planning, Bloom's taxonomy assessment generation, and explanatory study guides
  • Meeting productivity: transforming disorganized audio/transcript notes into structured executive summaries, action items, and task assignments
  • Designing multi-step business prompting workflows: connecting intake prompts, extraction prompts, drafting prompts, and QA prompts
  • Building human-in-the-loop verification gates: identifying exactly where human approval is legally, ethically, and commercially required
  • Constructing an enterprise-ready Prompt Library in Google Sheets: folder hierarchies, variable documentation, sample outputs, and version histories
  • Handling edge cases and failure fallbacks: designing prompts that gracefully flag uncertainty or request managerial review
  • Data security, confidentiality, and organizational AI policies: establishing safe operating procedures for corporate teams
  • Capstone Project Workshop: assembling, testing, and presenting an end-to-end Evaluated Business Prompting Workflow
  • Career navigation and portfolio presentation: packaging prompt libraries, evaluation workbooks, and workflow diagrams for employers and freelance clients
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Designing a complete multi-step customer support intake workflow that classifies incoming customer requests, extracts account numbers into JSON, drafts a personalized empathetic reply, and prepares an internal summary for human review.
📝 Practical Assignment: Final Capstone Project — Evaluated Business AI Workflow: Designing, testing, and documenting a complete business prompting workflow (selected from customer support, marketing brief generation, executive document synthesis, or educational operations) complete with problem statement, prompt architecture, test cases, evaluation scoring, failure handling, and human approval protocols.
