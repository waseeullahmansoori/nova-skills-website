STEP-BY-STEP MODULES

Detailed Curriculum & Labs

Module 01 Architecture Modeling in 3ds Max 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Translate 2D CAD plans into precise 3D architectural geometry.
  • Import and scale DWG files in 3ds Max.
  • Use spline modeling to create walls and foundations.
  • Model architectural details like windows, doors, and stairs.
Key Concepts: Unit SetupSpline ModelingPoly Extrusion
1.1 Interface and CAD Import Workflow
Taught: 3ds Max UI navigation and proper DWG import settings.
Learner Lab: Imports a residential floor plan and sets up project units.
Why It Matters: Working to scale is critical for lighting and material accuracy later.
Expected Deliverable: Properly scaled 2D reference ready for 3D extrusion.
1.2 Spline to Poly Modeling
Taught: Extruding splines and poly modeling techniques for walls.
Learner Lab: Models the exterior walls and roof of a modern house.
Why It Matters: Forms the structural basis of any ArchViz project.
Expected Deliverable: Completed primary exterior structure.
1.3 Detailing Windows and Doors
Taught: Boolean operations and specific architectural modeling tools.
Learner Lab: Models and places windows, doors, and mullions.
Why It Matters: Adds necessary realism and scale cues to the building.
Expected Deliverable: Fully detailed exterior envelope.
1.4 Interior Hard Surface Modeling
Taught: Modeling interior elements like stairs, skirting boards, and cabinetry.
Learner Lab: Models a custom staircase and kitchen island.
Why It Matters: Prepares the scene for interior rendering views.
Expected Deliverable: Complete structural model ready for texturing.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Model a complete modern villa from provided CAD drawings.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit the completed 3ds Max project file of the villa model.
💼 Commercial Application: Rapid prototyping of architectural designs for client approval.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Accurate 3D whitebox model of the residential project.
Module 02 Material Creation & PBR Texturing 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Apply realistic surfaces to architectural models using V-Ray materials.
  • Understand the Principles of Physically Based Rendering (PBR).
  • Create complex V-Ray materials like glass, metal, and concrete.
  • Use UVW mapping for correct texture alignment.
Key Concepts: PBR TexturingV-Ray MtlUVW Mapping
2.1 Introduction to V-Ray Material Editor
Taught: Navigating the Slate Material Editor and basic V-Ray Mtl settings.
Learner Lab: Creates basic diffuse, reflective, and refractive materials.
Why It Matters: The foundation for creating any surface type in V-Ray.
Expected Deliverable: Library of basic architectural materials.
2.2 PBR Workflow and Texture Maps
Taught: Using Albedo, Normal, Roughness, and Displacement maps.
Learner Lab: Builds a realistic concrete and wood floor material using PBR maps.
Why It Matters: Essential for photorealism and accurate light interaction.
Expected Deliverable: High-fidelity PBR materials applied to scene elements.
2.3 Advanced Glass and Metals
Taught: Configuring IOR (Index of Refraction), anisotropy, and thin-film effects.
Learner Lab: Creates architectural glazing and brushed aluminum materials.
Why It Matters: Glass and metal are prominent in modern architecture and must look perfect.
Expected Deliverable: Realistic windows and metallic fixtures in the scene.
2.4 UVW Mapping and Unwrapping
Taught: Aligning textures correctly on complex geometry using UVW Map modifier.
Learner Lab: Maps brick and wood textures correctly onto the villa exterior.
Why It Matters: Prevents stretched or misaligned textures which ruin realism.
Expected Deliverable: Fully textured exterior architectural model.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Texture the entire exterior and interior of the modern villa model.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit rendered material previews of 5 key architectural surfaces.
💼 Commercial Application: Providing clients with accurate material representations before construction.
🎯 Expected Outcome: A fully textured 3D scene ready for lighting.
Module 03 V-Ray Lighting & HDRI Setup 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Illuminate architectural scenes realistically using V-Ray lighting tools.
  • Setup physical cameras and exposure controls.
  • Use V-Ray Sun and Sky for natural daylight.
  • Implement Image-Based Lighting (IBL) with HDRI maps.
Key Concepts: Physical CameraHDRI LightingIES Profiles
3.1 V-Ray Physical Camera
Taught: F-stop, shutter speed, and ISO settings for controlling exposure.
Learner Lab: Sets up multiple camera angles and adjusts exposure values.
Why It Matters: Mimics real-world photography principles for realistic render output.
Expected Deliverable: Properly framed and exposed camera views.
3.2 Daylight Systems: Sun and Sky
Taught: Configuring the V-Ray Sun and Sky environment for exterior shots.
Learner Lab: Lights the villa exterior for a midday shot.
Why It Matters: The standard method for quick, accurate exterior daylight.
Expected Deliverable: Well-lit exterior daylight render.
3.3 HDRI Lighting Techniques
Taught: Using V-Ray Dome Light with HDRI maps for complex, realistic lighting.
Learner Lab: Sets up a sunset/twilight lighting scenario using an HDRI map.
Why It Matters: Provides superior reflection details and atmospheric lighting.
Expected Deliverable: Atmospheric twilight exterior render.
3.4 Interior Artificial Lighting
Taught: Using V-Ray Lights, IES profiles, and light portals for interiors.
Learner Lab: Lights the interior living room using recessed IES lights and lamps.
Why It Matters: Essential for evening interior shots and highlighting design features.
Expected Deliverable: Warm, realistically lit interior evening scene.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Create three distinct lighting setups (Day, Sunset, Night) for the villa.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit test renders of the three different lighting scenarios.
💼 Commercial Application: Showcasing properties in various atmospheric conditions to evoke client emotion.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Mastery of V-Ray illumination techniques.
Module 04 Render Optimization & Post-Production 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Generate high-quality final renders and enhance them in Photoshop.
  • Optimize V-Ray render settings for quality and speed.
  • Output Render Elements (passes) for compositing.
  • Use Photoshop for color grading, adding entourage, and final touches.
Key Concepts: Render ElementsDenoisingPost-Production
4.1 V-Ray Render Settings and Denoising
Taught: Balancing AA samples, GI settings, and using the V-Ray Denoiser.
Learner Lab: Configures optimal settings and renders the final 4K images.
Why It Matters: Eliminates render noise while minimizing render times for tight deadlines.
Expected Deliverable: Clean, high-resolution raw renders.
4.2 Render Elements and Masking
Taught: Setting up MultiMatte, Reflection, Refraction, and Z-Depth passes.
Learner Lab: Outputs required passes and composites them in Photoshop.
Why It Matters: Allows non-destructive editing of specific materials in post-production.
Expected Deliverable: Layered Photoshop document ready for editing.
4.3 Color Grading and Atmosphere
Taught: Using Camera Raw Filter, levels, and curves to enhance mood.
Learner Lab: Applies color grading and atmospheric fog effects in Photoshop.
Why It Matters: Takes a raw render from flat to professional and punchy.
Expected Deliverable: Dramatically improved final image quality.
4.4 Adding Entourage (People and Landscaping)
Taught: Integrating 2D cutouts of people, skies, and trees into the render.
Learner Lab: Adds a new sky, landscaping, and human figures using masks.
Why It Matters: Adds scale, life, and context to architectural scenes.
Expected Deliverable: Client-ready, photorealistic architectural visualization.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Render and post-process the final exterior and interior shots of the villa.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit the final high-resolution post-processed renders.
💼 Commercial Application: Delivering the final polished marketing assets to real estate clients.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Portfolio-ready architectural images.
Module 05 Real-Time Walkthroughs in Twinmotion 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Create interactive, real-time architectural presentations.
  • Sync 3ds Max models to Twinmotion via Datasmith.
  • Populate scenes with animated assets and vegetation.
  • Export high-quality real-time videos and panoramas.
Key Concepts: DatasmithReal-Time RenderingAnimation Paths
5.1 Data Sync and Environment Setup
Taught: Using Datasmith to transfer geometry and setting up geographic context.
Learner Lab: Imports the villa model into Twinmotion and sets the location/time of day.
Why It Matters: Bridges the gap between traditional modeling and real-time engines.
Expected Deliverable: Imported and lit real-time scene.
5.2 Twinmotion Materials and Lighting
Taught: Replacing standard materials with Twinmotion PBR materials and adjusting lights.
Learner Lab: Applies real-time glass, water, and emissive materials.
Why It Matters: Optimizes visual quality specifically for real-time rendering.
Expected Deliverable: Fully textured real-time environment.
5.3 Vegetation, Weather, and Entourage
Taught: Painting foliage, adding animated characters, and changing weather systems.
Learner Lab: Paints trees, adds walking characters, and simulates rain.
Why It Matters: Brings the scene to life instantly, a key advantage of real-time tools.
Expected Deliverable: Dynamic, living architectural environment.
5.4 Media Export and Animation
Taught: Creating camera paths, rendering video clips, and exporting panoramas.
Learner Lab: Sets up a 30-second camera flythrough and exports the video.
Why It Matters: The final deliverable format for client presentations.
Expected Deliverable: Completed MP4 architectural walkthrough video.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Create a full real-time presentation of the villa project.
📝 Practical Assignment: Submit the final exported 30-second walkthrough video.
💼 Commercial Application: Providing interactive presentations for client design reviews.
🎯 Expected Outcome: A polished real-time architectural animation.
