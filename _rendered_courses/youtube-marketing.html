STEP-BY-STEP MODULES

Detailed Curriculum & Labs

1 YouTube Channel Strategy & Positioning 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Define channel positioning, analyze audience demand, structure core content pillars, and design an optimized channel homepage.
Key Topics Covered (10):
  • YouTube marketing fundamentals: the dual nature of YouTube as the world's 2nd largest search engine and leading recommendation platform
  • YouTube for business vs. independent creators: brand awareness, lead generation, customer trust, and organic conversion funnels
  • Audience research & niche validation: evaluating topic demand, audience pain points, search volume, and market saturation
  • Viewer persona development: identifying viewer demographics, viewing habits, preferred formats, and core motivations
  • Search-driven vs. recommendation-driven discovery: understanding browse features, suggested video triggers, and organic search traffic
  • Channel brand positioning & value proposition: formulating a clear, differentiated channel promise ('Who it is for and why they should subscribe')
  • Competitor channel research & content gap analysis: evaluating competitor top videos, posting cadences, and unanswered viewer questions
  • Channel naming, branding & visual identity: banner design principles (safe zones for mobile/TV), profile icons, and brand consistency
  • Channel description & homepage architecture: writing a searchable 'About' section, setting up channel trailers, and organizing featured sections
  • Content pillar framework: establishing core topic buckets, episodic series concepts, and resource planning for sustainable publishing
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Conducting a competitive channel audit on 3 top competitors in a chosen niche, documenting content gaps, and creating an optimized channel homepage architecture blueprint.
📝 Practical Assignment: YouTube Channel Strategy Blueprint (Google Docs / PDF) detailing target niche rationale, viewer personas, positioning statement, content pillars, branding guidelines, and 30-day channel launch roadmap.
2 YouTube Search, Discovery & Video SEO 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Master YouTube search intent, video keyword research, title engineering, metadata structuring, and playlist navigation.
Key Topics Covered (11):
  • YouTube Search vs. Recommendations ecosystem: understanding how the algorithm matches video content to viewer intent and past watch history
  • Video keyword research methodology: leveraging YouTube search suggest, autocomplete, Google Trends for YouTube, and question discovery
  • Evaluating topic demand and competition: identifying high-search volume, low-competition video topics for initial channel traction
  • Title engineering & optimization: crafting titles that balance exact search keywords with emotional curiosity and viewer click-through intent
  • Video description architecture: writing the critical first 3 lines (above the fold), secondary description copy, outbound links, and source citations
  • Video metadata realities: the modern, minor role of video tags; avoiding keyword stuffing and outdated 'tag-cloud' manipulation tactics
  • Hashtags in video discovery: placement rules, category tagging, and avoiding hashtag spam policies
  • Playlists as SEO assets: creating topic-based playlists with keyword-rich titles and descriptions to boost session watch time
  • Video chapters & timestamps: structuring timestamped navigation to improve user experience, key moments, and Google Search rich snippet visibility
  • Captions, subtitles & transcriptions: the SEO and accessibility benefits of accurate manual subtitles vs. automated speech recognition
  • Video SEO audit checklist: pre-publishing metadata review to ensure zero misleading claims, clickbait traps, or policy violations
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Researching 10 high-opportunity video topics using Google Trends and YouTube Search suggest, engineering title variations, drafting descriptions with chapters, and compiling an SEO checklist.
📝 Practical Assignment: YouTube Search & Video SEO Optimization Workbook (Google Sheets / Docs) containing topic research, search intent mapping, 6 optimized video title variations, full descriptions with chapters, and playlist structure.
3 Creative Strategy, Scripts & Audience Retention 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Craft retention-focused video scripts, design compelling thumbnail concepts, and implement visual storytelling frameworks.
Key Topics Covered (11):
  • Audience attention dynamics: viewer psychology in the first 30 seconds; why retention is the #1 signal for recommendation algorithms
  • The Hook–Value–CTA scripting framework: crafting opening hooks that confirm the title's promise without drawn-out intro animations
  • Storytelling structures for business & educational videos: Problem–Agitate–Solution, 'How-To' tutorials, listicles, and case study breakdowns
  • Information pacing & viewer retention: maintaining momentum, eliminating fluff, and using pattern interrupts (visual changes, sound cues, B-roll)
  • Scriptwriting techniques: conversational voice, writing for spoken delivery, formatting audio/visual two-column scripts, and teleprompter tips
  • Audience retention graph analysis: identifying common drop-off spikes (intros, slow transitions, premature sign-offs) and retention peaks
  • Visual storytelling & B-roll briefing: planning screen recordings, product close-ups, text overlays, and graphic callouts to support comprehension
  • Thumbnail psychology & visual hierarchy: the 3-element rule (face/subject, background, 3-4 word text punch); mobile readability and contrast
  • Title–Thumbnail synergy: ensuring thumbnail and title complement each other without repeating identical wording; avoiding clickbait disappointment
  • Canva thumbnail design workflows: color psychology, bold typography, background removal, border accents, and creating reusable thumbnail templates
  • AI-assisted scriptwriting workflows: using ChatGPT/Gemini for research, outline generation, and hook variations while enforcing human review and voice
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Writing a complete 6-minute educational script using the Hook–Value–CTA framework, drafting visual B-roll cues, and designing 2 complementary thumbnail concepts in Canva.
📝 Practical Assignment: Six Production-Ready Video Packages Portfolio (3 Long-Form + 3 Shorts) complete with titles, hooks, full scripts, scene/B-roll briefs, CTAs, and mobile-optimized thumbnail concepts.
4 YouTube Shorts & Long-Form Publishing Systems 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Build sustainable multi-format publishing operations, master vertical Shorts storytelling, and execute content repurposing workflows.
Key Topics Covered (10):
  • The multi-format YouTube ecosystem: how Shorts, standard long-form videos, podcasts, and community posts work together to grow a channel
  • YouTube Shorts fundamentals: 9:16 vertical video specifications, 60-second limit, the Shorts Feed algorithm, and viewer swipe dynamics
  • Shorts scripting & storytelling: the 3-second hook, fast-paced visual delivery, continuous loops, and seamless audio integration
  • Long-form video editorial planning: developing episodic content series, scheduling sustainable production cadences, and managing burnout
  • Content repurposing systems: extracting 3-5 high-impact Shorts from a cornerstone long-form video; crafting dedicated vertical hooks for excerpts
  • Converting Shorts viewers into long-form subscribers: using the 'Related Video' link feature in Shorts to direct traffic to deep-dive content
  • YouTube Studio publishing operations: upload workflows, selecting video categories, language settings, licensing options, and visibility scheduling
  • Publishing Quality Assurance (QA) checklist: verifying end screens, info cards, chapters, monetization settings, and ad suitability ratings
  • Creating an editorial content calendar: planning weekly production schedules, asset management in Google Drive/Notion, and batch recording
  • Post-publish distribution: sharing videos via email newsletters, embedded blog posts, and organic social channels without triggering spam flags
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Taking an existing long-form video script, extracting 2 standalone 45-second Shorts scripts with vertical visual directions, and drafting a 4-week publishing calendar.
📝 Practical Assignment: Four Publishing Workflow Demonstrations: detailed operational guides for (1) Search-Focused Long-Form, (2) Educational Long-Form, (3) Shorts Discovery, and (4) Long-Form-to-Shorts Repurposing.
5 YouTube Analytics & Audience Intelligence 4 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Navigate YouTube Studio Analytics, master the August 2026 view-counting update, interpret retention curves, and extract actionable content insights.
Key Topics Covered (11):
  • YouTube Studio Analytics navigation: Overview, Content, Audience, and Research tabs; understanding channel-level vs. video-level telemetry
  • The August 24, 2026 YouTube View-Counting Overhaul: understanding how views are counted from the first second across Shorts and long-form
  • Engaged Views vs. Public Views: defining engaged views (viewers watching past initial threshold) and why raw views do not equal true attention
  • Thumbnail impressions & Impressions Click-Through Rate (CTR): calculating CTR by traffic source (Browse vs. Search vs. Suggested)
  • Watch time & Average View Duration (AVD): measuring total watch hours, Average Percentage Viewed (APV), and their impact on algorithmic promotion
  • Audience retention curve deep-dive: analyzing the 4 retention patterns (Intro drop-offs, gradual decline, flat lines, and re-watch spikes)
  • Shorts analytics metrics: 'Shown in Feed', 'Stayed to Watch' percentage vs. 'Swiped Away', and interpreting short-form engaged views
  • Audience intelligence & loyalty: tracking New Viewers vs. Returning Viewers, subscriber conversion rates, and viewer demographic profiles
  • Traffic source diagnostics: dissecting YouTube Search queries, Browse feature impressions, Suggested video placements, and external embeds
  • Advanced Analytics Mode: custom date comparisons, normalized video performance curves, multi-metric scatter plots, and export to Google Sheets
  • Translating data into creative improvements: identifying underperforming thumbnails for A/B testing and fixing recurring script drop-offs
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Analyzing the retention graph and traffic source breakdown of an authorized channel (or provided comprehensive dataset), identifying drop-off causes, and drafting thumbnail/title optimization recommendations.
📝 Practical Assignment: YouTube Analytics & Channel Growth Performance Report (Google Sheets / PDF) evaluating public vs. engaged views, CTR trends, retention curve diagnostics, Shorts performance, and data-backed content optimization priorities.
6 Channel Growth, Policies & Business Outcomes 3 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Build community loyalty, execute creator collaborations, understand YPP monetization eligibility, and navigate copyright and AI content disclosure policies.
Key Topics Covered (10):
  • Community engagement & audience loyalty: managing YouTube comments, pinned comments, hearting responses, and using the Community Tab (polls, images, updates)
  • Creator collaborations & networking: identifying complementary non-competing creators, guest appearances, and collaborative video formats
  • Business conversion journeys: designing frictionless viewer journeys from YouTube videos to email newsletter signups, lead magnets, and product pages
  • Video Call-To-Action (CTA) strategy: verbal CTAs, pinned comment links, end-screen cards, description links, and basic UTM tracking parameters
  • YouTube Partner Program (YPP) eligibility overview: understanding current criteria (subscribers, qualified watch hours, qualified Shorts views); why YPP is not guaranteed
  • Copyright fundamentals & Content ID: understanding Creative Commons, public domain, royalty-free licensing, copyright claims (ad sharing) vs. copyright strikes (penalties)
  • YouTube reused-content policies: why minimal compilation or reaction to third-party footage risks demonetization; establishing significant original commentary
  • AI-generated content compliance: mandatory YouTube disclosure rules for realistic altered or synthetic video/audio; avoiding deceptive synthetic media
  • Community Guidelines & spam policies: strict prohibition of artificial subscriber buying, automated view manipulation, misleading thumbnails, and scam links
  • Final Capstone assembly: compiling the channel blueprint, 6 video packages, 4 publishing workflows, and analytics audit into an employer-ready portfolio
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Auditing a proposed video concept against YouTube's copyright and AI disclosure policies, designing a lead magnet conversion CTA workflow with UTM parameters, and structuring a community poll.
📝 Practical Assignment: YouTube Channel Growth & Business Outcome Plan (Google Docs / PDF) detailing community engagement workflows, lead conversion funnel, copyright compliance checklist, AI disclosure rules, and 60-day strategic roadmap.
