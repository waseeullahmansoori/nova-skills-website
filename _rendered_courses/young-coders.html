FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions?

The Young Coders Program is designed for children and teenagers aged 9 to 16. To ensure age-appropriate pedagogy, learners are organized into two distinct developmental pathways: Junior Coders (ages 9–12) and Teen Coders (ages 13–16), each with tailored project complexity and pacing.

No prior coding experience is needed. The course begins with foundational computational thinking—how computers process instructions, algorithms, flowcharts, and block-based visual coding in MIT Scratch—before progressing to typed Python programming.

The initial readiness check is a friendly, low-stress 15-minute diagnostic session with an instructor before classes begin.
