Eliminating jump cuts and refining conversational rhythm with J-cuts and L-cuts (Alt/Option click audio unlinking).
  • Ripple Edit (B): trimming clip duration while automatically closing or expanding downstream timeline gaps
  • Rolling Edit (N): adjusting the edit point between two adjacent clips without changing the overall sequence length
  • Slip Edit (Y): shifting the in/out range of footage inside a clip without changing its position or duration on the timeline
  • Slide Edit (U): moving a clip earlier or later in time while automatically adjusting the durations of adjacent clips
