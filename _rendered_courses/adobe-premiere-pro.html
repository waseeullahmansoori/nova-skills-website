1.1 Fundamentals of Digital Video
Taught: Resolution, frame size (HD, 4K), frame rate (24fps, 30fps, 60fps), progressive vs interlaced scanning, aspect ratios (16:9, 9:16, 1:1), containers vs codecs (MP4, MOV, ProRes, H.264), compression basics, bitrate concepts, and format compatibility.
  • Understanding digital video resolution: 1080p Full HD vs 4K UHD
  • Frame rates and temporal motion: cinematic 24fps vs broadcast 30fps vs slow-mo 60fps
  • Progressive video frames vs legacy interlaced scanning
  • Aspect ratios: landscape 16:9, vertical 9:16, and square 1:1
  • Containers vs codecs: MP4/MOV wrappers vs H.264/H.265 compression
  • Bitrate fundamentals: constant bitrate (CBR) vs variable bitrate (VBR)
Learner Lab: Audits technical properties of mixed video clips using media inspection tools.
Why It Matters: Technical video literacy prevents sequence mismatch errors, distorted pixels, and playback stutter.
Expected Deliverable: Accurate comprehension of frame sizes, frame rates, codecs, and container wrappers.

  • The three-point editing mathematical model: defining duration via 3 points
  • Executing Insert edits to push downstream clips forward without gaps
  • Executing Overwrite edits to replace timeline segments cleanly

Learner Lab: Assembles a multi-clip narrative sequence using keyboard-driven three-point edits.

2.3 Professional Trimming
Taught: Advanced non-linear trimming tools: Selection tool (V), Ripple Edit tool (B), Rolling Edit tool (N), Slip tool (Y), Slide tool (U), Razor tool (C), Trim Mode (T), and dynamic keyboard trimming.
  • Ripple Edit: trimming head/tail of clips while automatically closing empty gaps
  • Rolling Edit: adjusting the cut point between adjacent clips without changing total duration
  • Slip Edit: shifting internal media frames while maintaining clip position and duration
  • Slide Edit: moving a clip on the timeline while trimming neighboring clips to fit
  • Trim Edit mode (T) and J-K-L dynamic live trimming in the Program Monitor
Learner Lab: Refines cut points using Ripple, Rolling, Slip, and Slide tools to perfect timing.
Why It Matters: Trimming precision is what gives an edit rhythm, flow, and commercial polish.
Expected Deliverable: Tight, rhythmic sequence trimmed to exact frame accuracy using advanced trim tools.

  • Eyeline matching: aligning gaze direction between conversational shots
  • Screen direction consistency: maintaining left-to-right subject movement
  • Cutaways and inserts: covering continuity jumps and speech edits
