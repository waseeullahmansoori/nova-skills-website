STEP-BY-STEP MODULES

Detailed Curriculum & Labs

Module 01 Interface, Ingest, and the Rough Cut 3 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Get comfortable with the Premiere Pro workspace and build your first edit.
  • Workspace panels and customization
  • Importing and organizing media
  • Source monitor and timeline basics
  • Basic trimming and transitions
Key Concepts: WorkspaceIn/Out PointsRipple Edit
1.1 Navigating the Workspace
Taught: Project panel, source/program monitors, and the timeline.
Learner Lab: Sets up a custom workspace.
Why It Matters: Understanding the layout prevents frustration.
Expected Deliverable: Comfort with the UI.
1.2 The Assembly Edit
Taught: Using In/Out points (I/O) and inserting clips to the timeline.
Learner Lab: Assembles a 1-minute interview.
Why It Matters: The fastest way to build a story.
Expected Deliverable: A basic sequence on the timeline.
1.3 Trimming and Refining
Taught: Ripple edit, rolling edit, and the razor tool.
Learner Lab: Removes pauses and mistakes from the interview.
Why It Matters: Tightens the pacing of the video.
Expected Deliverable: A tightened rough cut.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Assemble and trim a short interview.
📝 Practical Assignment: Deliverable: 1-minute rough cut.
💼 Commercial Application: Basic corporate talking-head videos.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Ability to navigate and perform basic cuts.
Module 02 Polish and Export 3 Lessons ⌄
Module Focus: Add professional polish to your cut with B-roll, color, audio, and graphics.
  • Layering B-roll
  • Basic Lumetri color correction
  • Audio mixing with Essential Sound
  • Exporting via Media Encoder
Key Concepts: Essential GraphicsLumetri ColorExport Settings
2.1 B-Roll and Text Graphics
Taught: Adding cutaways to V2 and using the Essential Graphics panel for titles.
Learner Lab: Adds B-roll and lower thirds to their interview.
Why It Matters: Keeps the viewer engaged visually.
Expected Deliverable: A visually dynamic edit.
2.2 Color Correction Basics
Taught: Using the Lumetri Color panel to adjust exposure, contrast, and white balance.
Learner Lab: Fixes underexposed and incorrectly white-balanced clips.
Why It Matters: Makes footage look professional and consistent.
Expected Deliverable: Properly exposed footage.
2.3 Audio Mixing and Exporting
Taught: Adjusting track volume, auto-ducking music, and export settings (H.264).
Learner Lab: Mixes dialogue with music and exports an MP4.
Why It Matters: Ensures the video sounds good and plays back smoothly on YouTube.
Expected Deliverable: A finalized, exported video file.
🛠️ Hands-on Lab: Finish the interview edit with color, graphics, audio, and export it.
📝 Practical Assignment: Deliverable: Final MP4 corporate promo.
💼 Commercial Application: Delivering finalized marketing assets.
🎯 Expected Outcome: Ability to completely finish and export a video.
