Help & Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, factual answers regarding our learning locations, course delivery, and admissions.

Can I join Nova Skills courses online from my city?

Yes. Students from all 36 listed cities can enroll in live interactive online batches. All sessions include live two-way mentor interactions, hands-on project reviews, recorded lecture archives, and weekend doubt-clearing sessions.

Does Nova Skills have physical campuses in all 36 listed cities?

No. Nova Skills' physical classroom campus is located in Siddharth Nagar, Uttar Pradesh. The 36 cities listed in this directory represent our live online learning service areas, where students participate through high-definition live interactive virtual classrooms.

Which professional courses can I explore?

You can explore 109+ practical courses across our 12 specialized Academies, including AI Digital Marketing, Graphic Design, Professional Video Editing, AI for Business, Full Stack Web Development, Python, and UI/UX Design.

How do the live interactive online classes work?

Classes are conducted live by experienced industry practitioners. Learners build real-world client portfolios, submit regular code or design assignments for personalized 1-on-1 feedback, and interact with peers through our dedicated learning community.

Can beginners join these programs?

Yes. Our foundation and professional programs are designed with a zero-to-advanced learning curve. Foundational modules build fundamental concepts before advancing to real-world capstone projects.

How do I book the 2-Day Free Demo?

You can click any 'Book Free Demo' button on this page to select your preferred career track and reserve your seat in an upcoming live demonstration batch with zero upfront commitment.

Are all courses available in live online mode?

Yes, our flagship professional programs and master diplomas are fully optimized for live interactive online delivery, featuring screen sharing, cloud lab access, and digital portfolio reviews.

Where can I check course fees and duration?

Transparent fees and durations are published on our individual course detail pages and Academy catalogues. Professional programs range from 3 to 6 months with verified launch fees starting from ₹19,900. You can visit our All Courses catalogue for a full breakdown.

Can I contact an admissions counsellor before enrolling?

Absolutely. You can schedule a Free Consultation with an academic advisor who will review your career goals, evaluate suitable learning paths, and explain batch schedules.

Where is the actual Nova Skills classroom campus?

Our physical classroom campus and administrative headquarters are located in Siddharth Nagar, Uttar Pradesh. Detailed campus directions and contact info are available on our official Contact page.
