## 1. Executive Context & Core Development Principles

This specification establishes provisional, non-overlapping educational boundaries and commercial positioning for four existing Digital Marketing programs in the Nova Skills portfolio.

### Approved Development Principles:
1. **Preserve and Differentiate All Four Courses:** The active working direction is to maintain and distinctly position all four course IDs. Course consolidation or merger is **not** authorized.
2. **Educational Completeness:** Every course must remain fully complete, self-contained, and educationally viable for its promised career outcome. Essential foundational concepts are **never** artificially removed or weakened simply to encourage upgrades.
3. **Ecosystem & Satellite Course Integrity:** Standalone specializations in the Nova Skills catalog (e.g., `google-ads`, `meta-ads`, `seo-geo`, `google-analytics-ga4`, `email-marketing`) retain independent educational and commercial value. Flagship and professional programs do not duplicate or render obsolete these specialized tracks.
4. **Strict Commercial Baseline:** All existing published course names, course IDs, URLs, catalog fees, and advertised durations remain strictly unchanged pending explicit business owner approval. Existing data inconsistencies are documented as unresolved issues rather than silently modified.

* **Course ID:** `digital-marketing-pro`
* **Current Published Name:** Digital Marketing Professional *(Unchanged)*
* **Working Position:** Digital Marketing Standard / Foundation

## 5. Distinct Practical Portfolio Outcomes

Each course is assigned distinct, verifiable portfolio deliverables corresponding to its intended outcome:

### 1. `digital-marketing-pro` Deliverables
- **Local Business Digital Starter Kit:** 30-day social content calendar, 6 customized Canva promotional assets, and basic community management guidelines.
- **Guided Lead Generation Campaign Setup:** A live or simulated Meta/Google Search campaign setup demonstrating correct objective selection and budget pacing.
- **Basic Traffic Acquisition Scorecard:** Standard GA4 traffic acquisition report with documented custom UTM campaign tags.

### 2. `dm-professional` Deliverables
- **AI-Powered Multi-Channel Marketing Plan:** Target persona dossiers, competitor search analysis, and a structured full-funnel marketing strategy document.
- **Live Search & Paid Social Campaign Build:** Production Google Ads Search campaign (ad groups, responsive search ads, negative lists) and Meta Ads Manager lead generation campaign.
- **On-Page & Local SEO Implementation:** Keyword-to-page mapping document, optimized title/meta descriptions, and a Google Business Profile optimization checklist.
- **Client Performance Presentation:** GA4 conversion event verification and a 10-slide executive marketing performance review deck.

### 3. `perf-marketing-pro` Deliverables
- **Tracking & Event Infrastructure Blueprint:** Configured Google Tag Manager web container with Data Layer event specifications for custom conversions.
- **Algorithmic Paid Search Scaling Case Study:** Google Ads Performance Max campaign architecture with segmented asset groups, audience signals, and value-based bidding.
- **Creative Testing & Optimization Framework:** Documented creative testing matrix, fatigue rotation schedule, and ROAS scaling protocol.
- **Unified Looker Studio Performance Dashboard:** Production dashboard blending Google Ads spend, Meta Ads spend, and GA4 transaction revenue to report CAC and MER.

### 4. `dm-mastery` Deliverables
- **360° Omnichannel Growth Playbook:** Complete acquisition, conversion, and retention architecture for a commercial venture (D2C or B2B).
- **Integrated Search & Content Strategy:** Technical search audit framework and Generative Search visibility plan coordinated with paid advertising channels.
- **Marketing Operations Integration Blueprint:** Documented automation architecture connecting lead capture workflows to CRM and email nurture sequences.
- **Agency Pitch & Executive Governance Suite:** Professional agency proposal deck, statement of work (SOW), client retainer agreement, and multi-channel Looker Studio master dashboard.
