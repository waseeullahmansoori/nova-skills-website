## 1. Executive Summary & Program Positioning

### 1.1 Course Positioning & Commercial Value
In the contemporary 2026 tech ecosystem, digital products define modern business value. From early-stage startup MVPs to enterprise SaaS platforms, consumer mobile applications, and high-growth e-commerce ventures, organizations demand digital product designers who operate with exceptional visual refinement, rigorous human-centered research methodologies, scalable design system architecture, and real-world implementation capabilities.

The UI/UX design hiring landscape in India and globally has matured significantly:
1. **End of "Dribbble-Only" UI Design:** Hiring teams and product leads reject superficial mockups that ignore real-world UX constraints, accessibility standards (WCAG 2.2), information architecture, edge cases, error states, and responsive behavior.
2. **Figma Mastery as the Production Standard:** Modern product designers must master advanced Figma features: Auto Layout v5, Component Properties, Nested Instances, Multi-Dimensional Variables (Color, Spacing, Booleans, Strings), Variable Modes (Light/Dark themes, Density), and Dev Mode handoff workflows.
3. **The Rise of Designer-Led Publishing in Framer:** Product designers are increasingly expected to bridge the gap between static design files and live interactive web products. Framer has emerged as the premier industry tool for publishing production-ready responsive websites, landing pages, interactive portfolios, and marketing sites with native CMS and motion.
4. **Disciplined Generative AI Integration:** Generative AI tools (Figma AI, FigJam AI, Framer AI/Agents, ChatGPT, Claude) have transformed product workflows. High-performing designers leverage AI to accelerate research synthesis, persona drafting, copy ideation, and wireframe scaffolding, while applying critical human judgment to maintain structural integrity and genuine user empathy.

**UI/UX Design Professional** is Nova Skills' flagship 5-month, 200-guided-hour intensive diploma. It is meticulously engineered to take learners from visual and research foundations to high-fidelity Figma product design systems, live Framer website engineering, and an agency-grade, interview-tested design portfolio.

### 3.3 Critical Technical Distinction: Responsive Design in Figma vs Framer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Essential Technical Reality for UI/UX Educators:**
> - **Figma Design Auto Layout:** Auto Layout v5 enables flexible, adaptive component resizing (`Fill container`, `Hug contents`, `Fixed width/height`, `Min/Max dimensions`, and `Wrap`). However, resizing an Auto Layout frame in Figma Design **does not magically swap a desktop horizontal navbar into a mobile hamburger menu drawer** within the same component instance. To achieve multi-device responsiveness in Figma, designers must create **breakpoint-specific component variants** and manually compose dedicated screen artboards (e.g., Desktop 1440px, Tablet 768px, Mobile 390px).
> - **Figma Sites (Emerging):** Figma's website publishing features support responsive breakpoint configurations, but remain distinct from the core Figma Design canvas.
> - **Framer (Production Reality):** Framer is a true code-backed visual web environment. A single Framer page natively binds Desktop (1200px+), Tablet (810px), and Mobile (390px) breakpoints with visual CSS media query inheritance. Framer enables **genuine viewport-based navigation switching**, allowing mobile hamburger navigation drawers to trigger automatically on smaller screens without duplicating canvas artboards.

### 3.4 The Generative AI Principle in Modern Product Design
1. **AI as an Accelerator, Not a Substitute:** AI tools may synthesize secondary research, generate draft personas, brainstorm user interview questions, generate microcopy variants, and suggest initial wireframe layouts.
2. **Zero Fictitious Research Evidence:** Students are strictly prohibited from using AI to simulate or fabricate user interview responses or usability testing outcomes. All UX findings must derive from real human interactions.
3. **Mandatory Human Authorship & Audit:** All AI-suggested UI components must be manually inspected, refactored into clean Auto Layout frames, bound to certified design tokens, and audited for WCAG 2.2 contrast compliance.

### MODULE 01 — UX RESEARCH, PROBLEM FRAMING & PRODUCT STRATEGY
* **Guided Hours:** 18 Hours (Weeks 01–02)
* **Primary Tools:** FigJam, Notion, Google Forms, ChatGPT / Claude (for interview prompt generation)
* **Module Purpose:** Ground learners in evidence-based human-centered design principles, teaching them how to deconstruct ambiguous business challenges, identify genuine user pain points, formulate research hypotheses, and conduct rigorous qualitative user discovery.

#### Detailed Subtopics:
1. **Introduction to Product Design & Double Diamond Methodology:** Discover, Define, Develop, Deliver; UX vs UI vs Product Management; business objectives vs user goals; stakeholder alignment.
2. **Problem Framing & Opportunity Mapping:** Authoring Problem Statements using the Point of View (POV) framework; "How Might We" (HMW) question formulation; Lean UX canvas.
3. **Qualitative Research Methodologies:** 1-on-1 user interview protocol design; open-ended vs leading questions; observational research; contextual inquiry; recruitment screener surveys.
4. **Research Synthesis & Sense-Making:** Affinity mapping in FigJam; qualitative quote clustering; extracting behavioral insights; empathy mapping (Says, Thinks, Does, Feels).
5. **Data-Driven User Personas & Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD):** Proto-personas vs validated personas; the Jobs-to-be-Done framework (When... I want to... So I can...); user mental models.
6. **Customer Journey Mapping:** Mapping touchpoints, emotions, pain points, drop-off moments, and service gaps across end-to-end user journeys.

#### Pedagogical Lessons Breakdown:
- **Lesson 1.1: Double Diamond Framework & Problem Framing (4.5 Hours):** Deconstructing client briefs, mapping business viability against user desirability, and drafting actionable HMW statements.
- **Lesson 1.2: User Interview Protocol & Field Research (4.5 Hours):** Structuring unbiased qualitative interview discussion guides, recruitment screeners, and conducting live mock interview roleplays.
- **Lesson 1.3: Qualitative Synthesis & Affinity Mapping in FigJam (4.5 Hours):** Ingesting raw transcript notes into FigJam, grouping data points by cognitive themes, and identifying core pain points.
- **Lesson 1.4: Validated Personas & Customer Journey Maps (4.5 Hours):** Translating synthesis into an authoritative User Persona and an emotional Journey Map detailing critical friction points.

#### Studio Lab & Graded Assignment:
- **Classroom Studio Lab:** Live in-class user interview simulation: students partner to conduct 15-minute interviews on a simulated FinTech onboarding brief, live-note insights in FigJam, and build an affinity map.
- **Graded Assignment:** Complete a comprehensive UX Discovery Report for a real-world problem space (HealthTech, FinTech, or EdTech) containing research goals, screener, 3 interview transcripts, affinity diagram, 1 primary persona, and 1 complete journey map. *(Weight: 10% of modular grade)*.
- **Tool Access & Fallback:** FigJam (Free Education account); fallback to Google Docs + Miro if awaiting educational verification.

### MODULE 02 — INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE, USER FLOWS & WIREFRAMING
* **Guided Hours:** 20 Hours (Weeks 03–04)
* **Primary Tools:** FigJam, Figma Design
* **Module Purpose:** Transform synthesized user insights into robust structural architectures, user navigation mental models, comprehensive task flows, and low-to-mid-fidelity wireframe layouts before introducing visual styling.

#### Detailed Subtopics:
1. **Information Architecture (IA) Fundamentals:** Cognitive psychology in IA; Miller’s Law (Chunking 7±2); Hick’s Law (Decision time vs choices); organizing digital information hierarchies.
2. **Card Sorting & Navigation Architecture:** Open vs closed card sorting; top-level navigation, secondary hierarchies, utilities, and footer architectures; authoring comprehensive sitemaps.
3. **User Flows & Task Flows:** Differentiating screen flows, task flows, and user flows; standard flowchart symbology (decision nodes, input screens, terminal actions); happy path vs edge cases and error states.
4. **Low-Fidelity Sketching & Divergent Ideation:** Crazy Eights rapid sketching; paper prototyping; divergent concept exploration; selecting viable wireframe layouts.
5. **Mid-Fidelity Digital Wireframing in Figma:** Grayscale layout fundamentals; visual hierarchy without color; typographic scale in wireframes; structural content containers.
6. **UX Writing & Microcopy Foundations:** Clear, concise interface language; functional button CTAs; form labels; contextual inline help; humanized error messages.

#### Pedagogical Lessons Breakdown:
- **Lesson 2.1: Information Architecture & Card Sorting (5.0 Hours):** Conducting card-sorting exercises in FigJam, evaluating navigation tree depth vs breadth, and drawing hierarchical product sitemaps.
- **Lesson 2.2: User Flows & Edge Case Engineering (5.0 Hours):** Mapping complex user task flows (e.g., user registration with email verification, forgot password, and KYC rejection branches).
- **Lesson 2.3: Rapid Divergent Sketching & Crazy Eights (5.0 Hours):** Translating user flows into paper sketch variations; evaluating layout viability against user task speed and cognitive load.
- **Lesson 2.4: Mid-Fidelity Wireframing in Figma (5.0 Hours):** Constructing structural 8-column and 12-column grayscale wireframes in Figma with established text hierarchies and realistic UI copy.

#### Studio Lab & Graded Assignment:
- **Classroom Studio Lab:** In-class architecture sprint: restructure a cluttered, confusing enterprise dashboard navigation tree into a clean 3-level sitemap with verified card-sorting data.
- **Graded Assignment:** Produce a complete Information Architecture & Wireframing Package for a complex multi-step digital workflow (e.g., medical appointment booking or mutual fund investment) featuring sitemap, complete branching user flow, and 8 mid-fidelity wireframe screens. *(Weight: 10%)*.

### MODULE 03 — UI VISUAL DESIGN & FIGMA FUNDAMENTALS
* **Guided Hours:** 24 Hours (Weeks 05–07)
* **Primary Tools:** Figma Design
* **Module Purpose:** Master the visual, aesthetic, and compositional principles of user interface design, developing fluency with Figma's vector engine, typographic hierarchies, color psychology, and interface layout grids.

#### Detailed Subtopics:
1. **Figma Canvas Architecture & Workspace Mastery:** Pages, frames, sections, canvas navigation, vector networks, Pen and Bend tools, Boolean operations, masks, and export configurations.
2. **Visual Hierarchy & Gestalt Principles in UI:** Proximity, similarity, continuity, closure, focal points; the F-pattern and Z-pattern reading behaviors in digital layouts.
3. **Interface Typography Architecture:** Modern UI font classification; serif vs sans-serif vs monospaced; modular type scales (Major Third 1.250, Perfect Fourth 1.333); line-height, letter-spacing, and paragraph spacing rules for digital screens.
4. **UI Color Theory & Semantic Palettes:** 60-30-10 color rule; primary, secondary, neutral, and semantic functional colors (Success, Warning, Error, Info); creating accessible tinted surfaces; dark mode color contrast laws.
5. **Layout Grids & Spatial Cadence:** The 8pt spatial grid system (4pt micro-grid for typography/icons); layout margins, gutters, and columns for mobile (4-column), tablet (8-column), and desktop (12-column).
6. **Iconography, Elevation & Visual Affordance:** Crafting pixel-perfect 24x24px vector icons on a strict bounding box; drop shadows, elevation levels (1dp to 24dp), blur effects, borders, and modern glassmorphism.

#### Pedagogical Lessons Breakdown:
- **Lesson 3.1: Figma Interface & Vector Network Engine (6.0 Hours):** Navigating the Figma canvas, manipulating vector nodes with Bend tools, building custom vector glyphs, and organizing multi-page project files.
- **Lesson 3.2: Spatial Systems & The 8pt Grid Architecture (6.0 Hours):** Implementing mathematically sound 8pt spacing rules across UI cards, modals, and container paddings with zero arbitrary pixel spacing.
- **Lesson 3.3: Typography Scales & Modular Text Hierarchy (6.0 Hours):** Constructing a production-ready typographic stylesheet in Figma from Display down to Micro-labels with optical line-height adjustments.
- **Lesson 3.4: Color Palettes, Semantic Logic & Elevation Depth (6.0 Hours):** Generating accessible, functional UI color palettes with HSL color adjustments, semantic surface tokens, and layered realistic drop shadows.

#### Studio Lab & Graded Assignment:
- **Classroom Studio Lab:** High-fidelity UI redesign: take an ugly, misaligned legacy banking interface and rebuild it into a modern, visually stunning screen utilizing the 8pt spatial grid and a modular type scale.
- **Graded Assignment:** Design a high-fidelity visual UI suite for a consumer mobile application (3 core screens: Feed, Product Detail, and Checkout) applying established typography, semantic color, and 8pt grid systems. *(Weight: 10%)*.

### MODULE 04 — ADVANCED FIGMA, AUTO LAYOUT & RESPONSIVE COMPONENTS
* **Guided Hours:** 28 Hours (Weeks 08–10)
* **Primary Tools:** Figma Design
* **Module Purpose:** Elevate Figma proficiency to elite engineering standards, mastering Auto Layout v5, complex component architecture, multi-axis variants, interactive component properties, and scalable responsive structures.

#### Detailed Subtopics:
1. **Auto Layout v5 In-Depth:** Direction (Horizontal/Vertical), Hug vs Fill vs Fixed; alignment, individual padding, gap spacing; Auto Layout wrapping (`Wrap`); Min/Max width and height constraints.
2. **Absolute Positioning & Negative Space in Auto Layout:** Layering floating badges, notification pips, and overlapping avatar stacks within rigid Auto Layout containers.
3. **Master Components & Component Properties:** Main components vs instances; Boolean properties, text swap properties, instance swap properties, and variant properties.
4. **Variants & Component Sets:** Matrix-based variant architecture; stateful components (Default, Hover, Active, Focused, Disabled, Error, Loading); nesting atomic components into complex molecules.
5. **Responsive Screen Composition in Figma:** Auto Layout screen assembly; adapting layouts across fluid parent containers; understanding the reality and limitations of Auto Layout resizing.

## 9. Comprehensive Institutional FAQs (20 Items)

1. **Can I enroll in UI/UX Design Professional as a complete beginner with no prior design experience?**
   Yes. The program is structured from first principles. Module 01 introduces human-centered design thinking, user discovery, and problem framing before touching software. No prior design degree, coding background, or drawing talent is required.
2. **What is the duration and weekly time commitment of this program?**
   The program spans 5 Months (20 calendar weeks) and includes exactly 200 guided instructional hours (~10 hours per week of live mentored instruction) plus 60–80 hours of recommended independent studio lab practice.
3. **How does UI/UX Design Professional differ from Creative Design Master?**
   Creative Design Master is an 8-month multidisciplinary program covering graphic design, advertising campaigns, packaging dielines in InDesign, brand motion in After Effects, and foundational UI. UI/UX Design Professional is a specialized 5-month program focused 100% on digital product design, advanced Figma variables, design systems, usability testing, and live web publishing in Framer.
4. **How does this course differ from Graphic Design Professional?**
   Graphic Design Professional (4 months, 160h) focuses on 2D visual communication, logos, branding, marketing creatives, social ads, and commercial print/prepress (CMYK, bleeds, dielines). UI/UX Design Professional focuses entirely on interactive digital products (mobile apps, SaaS platforms, web interfaces) with zero print design.
5. **Why is Adobe XD not taught in this program?**
   Adobe officially discontinued Adobe XD following its strategic focus on modern web ecosystems. Modern product design teams globally have standardized on Figma for interface design and Framer for live visual web publishing. Teaching obsolete software would compromise graduate employability.
6. **Why is Framer taught alongside Figma?**
   Figma is the world’s leading environment for interface design and design systems, but it cannot publish live, functional websites. Framer allows product designers to take their Figma designs and deploy live, responsive websites with real CMS data, scroll interactions, and SEO without writing code.
7. **Does this course teach frontend coding (HTML, CSS, JavaScript)?**
   No. This is a design and visual web engineering program. Instead of hand-coding syntax, students learn web layout principles (the box model, flexbox/stacks, responsive breakpoints) visually in Framer and master developer handoff using Figma Dev Mode.
8. **What is the reality of responsive design in Figma vs Framer?**
   Figma Auto Layout allows frames to stretch and adapt, but it cannot automatically transform a horizontal desktop navbar into an animated mobile hamburger drawer. In Figma, designers engineer breakpoint-specific component variants. In Framer, true responsive media queries are native, enabling real viewport-based navigation switching on a live website.
9. **How is Generative AI integrated into the curriculum?**
   Generative AI is taught in Module 08 as a professional productivity accelerator. You learn structured prompt engineering in ChatGPT/Claude for research synthesis and microcopy, alongside Figma AI and Framer AI for initial layout scaffolding. Crucially, human critical judgment and manual craftsmanship are enforced on all projects.
10. **Are software licenses included in the tuition fee?**
    Students access Figma Design and FigJam for free through the official Figma for Education program using their Nova Skills enrollment verification. Framer is fully functional on its free tier for all required coursework and live `.framer.media` publishing.
11. **Do I need an expensive Mac to participate in this course?**
    No. Figma and Framer are modern, cloud-native web applications that run smoothly in Google Chrome on both Windows and macOS laptops. A computer with an Intel Core i5 / AMD Ryzen 5 (or Apple M1/M2/M3) and 8GB–16GB of RAM is recommended.
12. **Are expensive AI subscriptions required?**
    No. Free tiers of OpenAI ChatGPT, Anthropic Claude, and free monthly credits within Figma and Framer are completely sufficient for all required coursework. Zero paid AI subscriptions are mandatory.
13. **How many portfolio case studies will I complete?**
    You build exactly 3 major modular assessed case studies plus 1 comprehensive Flagship Capstone: (1) Consumer Mobile App UX/UI, (2) Enterprise B2B SaaS Design System with Light/Dark modes, (3) Live Responsive Marketing Website in Framer with CMS, and (4) Integrated Commercial Product Launch Capstone.
14. **How are student projects evaluated?**
    Evaluation is continuous and competency-based: 25% from modular studio labs, 50% across the three core portfolio case studies, and 25% from the final integrated capstone and live oral viva voce defense.
15. **What digital accessibility standards are taught?**
    Students are trained on international WCAG 2.2 AA standards, learning contrast ratio calculation (4.5:1), touch target sizing (48x48px), focus indicators, keyboard navigation, and color-blindness simulation using the Stark plugin.
16. **What is the commercial tuition fee and tax treatment?**
    The tuition fee is flat INR 44,900. Statutory 18% GST (INR 8,082) is itemized on all commercial invoices, bringing the total payable fee to INR 52,982. There are zero hidden administrative charges.
17. **Are flexible No-Cost EMI payment options available?**
    Yes. Flexible No-Cost EMI plans are available through partner banks: INR 4,415/month for 12 months, INR 6,623/month for 8 months, or INR 8,830/month for 6 months.
18. **What career and placement support does Nova Skills provide?**
    Nova Skills provides 1-on-1 portfolio case study reviews, 1-page ATS product designer resume optimization, mock technical design defense interviews, live whiteboarding challenges, and introductions to hiring partner product teams and agencies.
19. **What job titles am I qualified for upon graduation?**
    Graduates are qualified for entry-level and junior-to-mid roles including Junior UI/UX Designer, Associate Product Designer, Junior Interaction Designer, Junior UX Researcher, and Framer Web Designer.
20. **Will I own the editable source files and live websites I create?**
    Yes. 100% of all Figma design files, FigJam research boards, Framer project links, and live published URLs remain your intellectual property for commercial portfolio and client presentation purposes.
