## 1. Executive Summary & Program Positioning

### 1.1 Course Positioning & Commercial Value
In the contemporary 2026 digital and commercial landscape in India, visual content is the primary driver of customer engagement, brand recall, and digital commerce. Startups, e-commerce brands, marketing agencies, corporate communications teams, and small-to-medium enterprises (SMEs) require a continuous stream of high-quality visual creative: corporate brand identities, social media campaigns, performance marketing ads, product catalogs, packaging labels, and print marketing collateral.

However, the modern job market has dramatically evolved:
1. **Tool Fluency Alone Is Insufficient:** Employers no longer hire button-pushers who merely know software menus. They seek designers grounded in core visual principles—typography, layout grids, color psychology, and composition—who understand *why* a design communicates effectively.
2. **Hybrid Digital & Print Reality:** While digital advertising and social content dominate daily workflows, commercial graphic designers in India must still master prepress fundamentals, CMYK color spaces, bleeds, paper stocks, and print shop handoff specifications (including legacy CorelDRAW ecosystem compatibility).
3. **Generative AI as an Acceleration Engine:** Generative AI tools (Adobe Firefly, Midjourney, Canva Magic Studio) have become standard studio accelerators. Hiring managers prioritize designers who leverage AI responsibly for rapid brainstorming, moodboarding, generative fill, and asset recoloring without losing artistic direction, vector precision, or brand consistency.

**Graphic Design Professional** is Nova Skills' intensive 4-month (160 guided instructional hours) career-focused program. It is designed to train aspiring designers, career switchers, and marketing professionals to become commercially viable, highly disciplined graphic designers capable of producing agency-grade visual creative from week one.

## 4. Comprehensive 8-Module Syllabus Architecture (160 Guided Hours)

```
================================================================================
MODULE 01 — GRAPHIC DESIGN FOUNDATIONS, COLOR & TYPOGRAPHY
Duration: 16 Guided Instructional Hours | Focus: Visual Grammar, Layout Principles & Type
================================================================================
```

### Module Overview
Master the universal laws of visual communication, human perception, and typographic hierarchy. Students learn to deconstruct commercial design briefs, formulate creative concepts, apply Gestalt perceptual psychology, and engineer mathematical layout grids.

### Topics & Detailed Subtopics
1. **Visual Communication & Design Thinking**
   - The commercial purpose of graphic design: problem solving vs artistic expression.
   - Anatomy of a commercial design brief: client goals, target demographic, key message, deliverables.
   - Divergent brainstorming techniques, mind mapping, and thumbnail sketching.
   - Visual research, curation, and moodboard construction.
2. **Gestalt Principles & Compositional Mechanics**
   - Gestalt laws in advertising: Proximity, Similarity, Continuity, Closure, Figure/Ground.
   - Focal hierarchy: establishing primary, secondary, and tertiary visual focal points.
   - Optical balance vs mathematical balance: symmetrical, asymmetrical, and radial balance.
   - Purposeful negative space (white space) as a structural design element.
   - The Rule of Thirds, Golden Ratio, and visual flow (Z-pattern and F-pattern scanning).
3. **Typography Science & Microtypography**
   - Anatomy of type: baseline, x-height, ascender, descender, counter, serif, stress.
   - Type classifications: Old Style, Transitional, Modern Serif, Geometric Sans, Humanist Sans, Slab Serif, Script, Display.
   - Microtypography mechanics: kerning, tracking, leading, measure (line length), and paragraph rags.
   - Building modular type scales (Minor Third, Major Third, Perfect Fourth) for consistent digital and print hierarchy.
   - Font pairing rules: contrast vs harmony, pairing serifs with sans-serifs, avoiding conflicting typefaces.
   - Licensing awareness: open-source Google Fonts vs commercial font foundry licensing.
4. **Color Theory & Technical Reproduction**
   - The psychological and emotional impact of color across commercial industries.
   - Color harmonies: monochromatic, analogous, complementary, split-complementary, triadic.
   - Technical color spaces: Additive RGB (screens) vs Subtractive CMYK (print).
   - HEX codes, web-safe palettes, and color contrast accessibility (WCAG AA standards).
   - Spot colors and introduction to the Pantone Matching System (PMS).
5. **Grid Systems & Layout Architecture**
   - Column grids, modular grids, baseline grids, and hierarchical grids.
   - Margin and gutter mathematical relationships.
   - Breaking the grid intentionally to generate commercial visual tension.

### Pedagogical Lessons Breakdown
* **Lesson 1.1: Design Brief Deconstruction & Thumbnail Sketching** (4 Hours)
  - *What is taught:* How to read client briefs, identify business objectives, research competitors, and execute 30 rapid thumbnail sketches.
  - *Why it matters:* Designers who start in software without thumbnail sketches produce generic, poorly structured layouts.
  - *What learner does:* Analyze a brief for an organic food brand and draw 20 thumbnail compositions on paper.
  - *Expected outcome:* A structured design brief breakdown and an analog thumbnail sketchbook sheet.
  - *Example:* Translating a client request for "fresh and trustworthy" into concrete visual choices: open whitespace, earthy green tones, and humanistic typography.
* **Lesson 1.2: Compositional Structure & Gestalt in Action** (4 Hours)
  - *What is taught:* Applying Gestalt laws to organize complex visual elements into intuitive, instantly digestible compositions.
  - *Why it matters:* Visual clutter confuses consumers; Gestalt principles ensure immediate visual comprehension.
  - *What learner does:* Create 4 digital layout studies using strictly black-and-white geometric shapes to demonstrate proximity, closure, and visual hierarchy.
  - *Expected outcome:* Four high-contrast composition boards demonstrating mastery of visual weight and balance.
  - *Example:* Using negative space closure in a poster composition to form an implicit secondary shape that directs the eye to the call-to-action.
* **Lesson 1.3: Typographic Systems & Font Pairing Mastery** (4 Hours)
  - *What is taught:* Font anatomy, modular type scaling, manual optical kerning, and establishing robust typographic hierarchies.
  - *Why it matters:* Over 90% of commercial design communication is textual. Poor typography immediately signals amateur execution.
  - *What learner does:* Typeset a multi-level promotional poster excerpt, manually adjust problematic kerning pairs, and configure a 4-level type scale.
  - *Expected outcome:* A complete typographic specimen sheet exhibiting professional tracking, leading, and hierarchy.
  - *Example:* Correcting awkward kerning in high-visibility headline letter pairs such as "AV", "To", and "Wa".
* **Lesson 1.4: Color Palettes & Technical Reproduction Standards** (4 Hours)
  - *What is taught:* Formulating 5-swatch brand color harmonies, mapping RGB to CMYK, and verifying WCAG contrast compliance.
  - *Why it matters:* Mismatched color profiles cause muddy, unacceptable print outputs and poor digital legibility.
  - *What learner does:* Construct a certified 5-color corporate brand palette specifying exact RGB, CMYK, and HEX values with contrast ratios verified.
  - *Expected outcome:* A production-ready color specification sheet with accessibility ratings.
  - *Example:* Converting a vibrant RGB electric blue into its closest achievable CMYK formula to prevent dull purple shifting during commercial offset printing.

### Classroom Studio Lab
Construct an analog and digital concept board: sketch 20 thumbnail ideas, configure a 12-column modular grid, select a two-font pairing system, and engineer a certified 5-color harmony palette.

### Graded Assignment
Design a conceptual typographic poster for a cultural exhibition or commercial conference, demonstrating strict adherence to modular grid alignment, 3-tier hierarchy, optical kerning, and WCAG-compliant color contrast.

### Module Overview
Master Adobe Illustrator as the primary commercial engine for vector artwork, logos, iconography, and scalable marketing illustrations. Develop flawless Bezier curve control, mathematical precision using geometric grids, and production-ready vector export standards.

### Topics & Detailed Subtopics
1. **Illustrator Workspace & Vector Mechanics**
   - Mathematical nature of vector graphics (resolution independence vs raster pixels).
   - Canvas, artboards, bleed settings, pixel grids, and coordinate geometry.
   - Master navigation, custom workspaces, guides, smart guides, and ruler origins.
2. **Precision Path Construction & The Pen Tool**
   - Flawless pen tool mechanics: anchor points, directional handles, smooth points, corner points.
   - Minimizing anchor points for smooth, production-grade vector curves.
   - Curvature Tool, Pencil Tool, and path smoothing techniques.
   - Path operations: Join, Split, Average, Simplify, and Outline Stroke.
3. **Shape Construction & Geometric Geometry**
   - Primitives and geometric transformations.
   - Shape Builder Tool vs Pathfinder Panel (Unite, Minus Front, Intersect, Exclude, Divide).
   - Building complex vectors from overlapping geometric primitives.
   - Golden Ratio circular construction grids and isometric grid setups.
4. **Typography Engineering in Illustrator**
   - Point type vs Area type; threading text across multi-column containers.
   - Type on a path, envelope distort, and warp effects.
   - Converting live typography to outlines (`Create Outlines`) and vector glyph editing.
5. **Color, Gradients & Vector Stylization**
   - Global swatches, spot swatches, and color groups.
   - Linear, radial, and freeform gradients.
   - Appearance panel: stacking multiple strokes and fills on a single vector object.
   - Vector patterns, seamless pattern tiles, and custom brush creation (Art, Scatter, Pattern).
6. **Vector Assets & Illustration Production**
   - Flat vector illustration, isometric iconography, and badge creation.
   - Image Trace panel: converting clean raster scans into editable vector paths, tracing parameters, cleanup.
   - Asset Export panel: exporting multi-artboard SVG, EPS, PDF, and high-resolution PNG @1x/@2x/@3x.

### Pedagogical Lessons Breakdown
* **Lesson 2.1: Pen Tool Precision & Anchor Point Minimization** (7 Hours)
  - *What is taught:* Advanced Bezier curve manipulation, handle symmetry, placing anchor points strictly at curve extrema (highest/lowest/outermost points).
  - *Why it matters:* Excess anchor points result in bumpy, amateur vector curves that fail during large-format vinyl cutting or embroidery.
  - *What learner does:* Vectorize 5 complex hand-drawn organic curves and badges using the minimum number of anchor points.
  - *Expected outcome:* Clean vector line art with zero redundant points and perfect curve symmetry.
  - *Example:* Vectorizing an organic botanical leaf using exactly 4 extrema anchor points instead of 18 jagged points.
* **Lesson 2.2: Shape Builder, Pathfinder & Geometric Construction** (7 Hours)
  - *What is taught:* Geometric construction mechanics using the Shape Builder and Pathfinder to construct balanced symbols.
  - *Why it matters:* Professional commercial brand marks are built from geometric primitives, ensuring mathematical perfection at all scales.
  - *What learner does:* Construct a stylized animal brand symbol from intersecting circles and rectangles using the Shape Builder tool.
  - *Expected outcome:* A mathematically precise, unified vector symbol with clean, closed vector paths.
  - *Example:* Building the iconic Twitter or Apple logo geometry using strictly intersecting circles and pathfinder subtraction.
* **Lesson 2.3: Commercial Iconography & Isometric Vector Art** (7 Hours)
  - *What is taught:* Establishing an isometric grid (30-degree angles), constructing a cohesive 6-piece icon set with uniform stroke weights and corner radiuses.
  - *Why it matters:* Consistency in stroke weight and visual metaphor across an icon set is a primary hiring test for digital marketing designers.
  - *What learner does:* Design a 6-piece commercial e-commerce icon suite (cart, delivery, security, payment, support, return) on a 64x64px grid.
  - *Expected outcome:* A pixel-aligned 6-piece vector icon set exported in SVG and PNG formats.
  - *Example:* Applying uniform 2px strokes and 4px rounded corner radiuses across every icon in a corporate suite.
* **Lesson 2.4: Advanced Gradients, Patterns & Multi-Asset Export** (7 Hours)
  - *What is taught:* Freeform gradient meshes, creating seamless repeating background patterns, and batch asset export workflows.
  - *Why it matters:* Agencies require designers who can package hundreds of vector assets rapidly for development and marketing teams.
  - *What learner does:* Create a vibrant seamless geometric pattern for brand packaging, configure global swatches, and batch export 10 artboards via Asset Export.
  - *Expected outcome:* A seamless vector pattern swatch and an organized folder of production-ready vector assets.
  - *Example:* Naming and exporting 10 social media icons in SVG 1.1 and PNG @2x with standardized file naming conventions.

### Classroom Studio Lab
Execute a vector illustration challenge: build a set of 3 custom promotional badges featuring geometric construction, custom warped typography, freeform gradients, and seamless pattern backgrounds.

### Graded Assignment
Deliver an original 6-piece commercial vector icon suite and accompanying promotional illustration banner for a tech or consumer brand, complete with master `.AI` source files, expanded strokes, and organized layers.

### Module Overview
Master Adobe Photoshop as the industry benchmark for raster image editing, commercial photo retouching, non-destructive compositing, and advertising visual production. Students learn professional masking, frequency separation, lighting harmonization, and color grading.

### Topics & Detailed Subtopics
1. **Photoshop Workspace & Non-Destructive Foundations**
   - Raster imaging mechanics: pixels, PPI, resolution vs print DPI, color bit depth (8-bit vs 16-bit).
   - Non-destructive workflow philosophy: adjustment layers, clipping masks, and Smart Objects.
   - Layer organization, color-coding, naming protocols, and group hierarchies.
2. **Precision Selections & Advanced Masking**
   - Selection tools: Object Selection, Quick Selection, Pen Tool (paths to selections).
   - Perfecting complex selections: Select and Mask workspace, Global Refinements, Radius, Feather, Contrast, Shift Edge.
   - Masking complex organic hair, fur, transparent glass, and semi-translucent smoke.
   - Channel-based selections: creating ultra-sharp high-contrast masks from the RGB channels.
3. **Commercial Retouching & Frequency Separation**
   - Healing toolchain: Spot Healing Brush, Healing Brush, Patch Tool, Content-Aware Move, Clone Stamp.
   - Frequency Separation: splitting an image into high-frequency (texture) and low-frequency (tone/color) layers.
   - Non-destructive skin retouching: smoothing blemishes while preserving 100% realistic skin pores and texture.
   - Dodge and Burn techniques using 50% neutral gray overlay layers for commercial facial contouring.
4. **Commercial Product Cleanup & Mockup Engineering**
   - E-commerce product photo cleanup: removing dust, scratches, reflections, and manufacturing defects.
   - Pen tool clipping paths for razor-sharp product isolation.
   - Recreating photorealistic drop shadows and contact shadows using directional blur and brush density.
   - Building custom reusable product mockup PSDs using Smart Object displacement and perspective warping.
5. **Photographic Compositing & Visual Harmonization**
   - Multi-source image compositing: blending disparate background, subject, and foreground photographic elements.
   - Perspective matching: horizon line alignment and vanishing point grids.
   - Lighting matching: directional highlights, ambient bounce light, and rim lighting.
   - Color harmonization: using Curves, Selective Color, Gradient Maps, and match color adjustments.
   - Atmosphere and depth: depth of field, Gaussian blur, atmospheric haze, dust particles, and lens flare integration.
6. **Commercial Export & Asset Optimization**
   - Color profiles: sRGB IEC61966-2.1 for web vs Adobe RGB 1998 for photography.
   - Export As panel: WebP, progressive JPEG, 24-bit PNG with alpha transparency.
   - Sharpening for web display: High Pass filter and Unsharp Mask workflows.

### Pedagogical Lessons Breakdown
* **Lesson 3.1: Non-Destructive Masking & Hair Extraction** (7 Hours)
  - *What is taught:* Using the Pen tool for hard-edge objects and Select & Mask / Channel calculations for fine flyaway hair and translucent elements.
  - *Why it matters:* Jagged, poorly isolated product edges or haloed hair cutouts immediately ruin commercial advertising credibility.
  - *What learner does:* Isolate a human model with complex curly hair and place the subject seamlessly against a dark studio background.
  - *Expected outcome:* A flawlessly extracted model cut with zero fringing, green spill, or lost hair strand detail.
  - *Example:* Using the Decontaminate Colors slider and manual brush blending to eliminate color cast on edge hair strands.
* **Lesson 3.2: Commercial Frequency Separation & Skin Retouching** (7 Hours)
  - *What is taught:* Mathematical frequency separation (16-bit Apply Image calculations), texture cloning, and 50% gray dodge/burn contouring.
  - *Why it matters:* Over-blurred, plastic-looking skin from amateur blur filters is rejected by commercial clients. Professional retouching preserves true texture.
  - *What learner does:* Retouch a raw beauty portrait, eliminate acne blemishes on the high frequency, and balance skin tone blotchiness on the low frequency.
  - *Expected outcome:* A high-end commercial beauty portrait that looks clean, healthy, and natural without looking blurred.
  - *Example:* Removing a forehead blemish by cloning texture from an adjacent skin area while leaving the underlying shadow contour unchanged.
* **Lesson 3.3: Product Cleanup & Photorealistic Shadow Creation** (7 Hours)
  - *What is taught:* Cleaning e-commerce cosmetic/tech product photography, removing reflections, and hand-crafting 3-tier realistic shadows (contact, ambient, cast).
  - *Why it matters:* Over 80% of freelance Photoshop work in India involves e-commerce catalog image enhancement and Amazon/Flipkart listing visuals.

## 9. Comprehensive Institutional FAQs (18 Items)

1. **Can I enroll in Graphic Design Professional as a complete beginner?**
   Yes. The curriculum begins with foundational design principles, visual observation, and sketching in Module 01 before introducing software. Zero prior graphic design or art school experience is required.
2. **What is the duration and weekly time commitment of this program?**
   The program runs for 4 Months (16 calendar weeks), with 160 guided instructional hours (~10 hours/week) and 40–60 hours of recommended independent studio lab practice.
3. **How does this course differ from the 8-month Creative Design Master?**
   Graphic Design Professional is a focused 4-month program specializing in commercial 2D graphic design, branding, social ads, and print collateral. Creative Design Master is an 8-month multidisciplinary flagship covering UI/UX in Figma, motion graphics in After Effects, advanced packaging engineering, and an agency capstone.
4. **Why are both Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop taught?**
   Illustrator creates vector graphics (logos, icons, typography, dielines) that scale infinitely without pixelation. Photoshop edits raster images (photos, retouching, compositing) with pixel-level control. Both are mandatory for commercial design.
5. **Why is Adobe InDesign included in a graphic design course?**
   InDesign is the global publishing benchmark for multi-page documents (brochures, catalogs, corporate annual reports). Designing multi-page publications in Photoshop or Illustrator is considered an amateur practice that agencies reject.
6. **Why is Canva Pro taught alongside professional Adobe software?**
   Over 65% of marketing agencies, startups, and clients in India require Canva templates for fast daily social posting. Learning to build locked Brand Kits in Canva Pro allows graduates to serve freelance clients efficiently.
7. **Does this course teach CorelDRAW?**
   The course teaches the CorelDRAW prepress bridge. While primary vector design is executed in modern Adobe Illustrator, students learn the export settings, font outlining, and file formats (.CDR, EPS, PDF/X) required by thousands of local printing and packaging presses across India.
8. **Does this course cover UI/UX design or mobile app design in Figma?**
   No. To preserve strict curricular excellence, UI/UX design, user research, wireframing, and Figma prototyping belong strictly to our UI/UX Design Professional and Creative Design Master programs.
9. **Does this course teach video editing or After Effects motion graphics?**
   No. Motion graphics and video editing belong to the Video Production Academy and Creative Design Master. This course focuses on static 2D visuals, carousels, and basic Canva animated social assets.
10. **How is Generative AI integrated into the curriculum?**
    Generative AI is taught in Module 07 as a practical productivity engine. Students learn Adobe Firefly Generative Fill/Expand, text-to-vector, inpainting, and prompt engineering, while strictly retaining human authorship over design geometry and typography.
11. **Do I need an expensive computer to run this software?**
    A computer with an Intel Core i5 or AMD Ryzen 5 processor (or Apple M1/M2/M3), 16GB of RAM, and 50GB of free SSD storage is strongly recommended to run Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, and InDesign smoothly.
12. **Are software licenses included in the tuition fee?**
    No. In accordance with standard educational practices, students obtain their own Adobe Creative Cloud subscription (discounted student plans are available from Adobe India for approx. ₹1,500/month).
13. **Are expensive AI subscriptions required?**
    No. Adobe Firefly includes free monthly generative credits with Creative Cloud plans. Free and open-source AI tools are utilized alongside Midjourney demonstration labs; zero paid Midjourney subscriptions are required.
14. **Will I work on real or simulated client briefs?**
    Students work on commercially realistic client briefs modeled after actual agency requirements from Indian startups, e-commerce brands, and consumer product companies.
15. **Will I own the editable source files for my portfolio projects?**
    Yes. 100% of all `.AI`, `.PSD`, `.INDD`, and exported assets created during the course belong to you for your commercial portfolio and client presentations.
16. **What is the commercial tuition fee and tax treatment?**
    The tuition fee is flat INR 29,900. Statutory 18% GST (INR 5,382) is itemized on the tax invoice, bringing the total payable fee to INR 35,282. There are zero hidden fees.
17. **Are No-Cost EMI options available?**
    Yes. Flexible No-Cost EMI plans are available: INR 2,940/month for 12 months, INR 4,410/month for 8 months, or INR 5,880/month for 6 months through approved partner banks.
18. **What placement and career support does Nova Skills provide?**
    Nova Skills provides portfolio reviews, Behance case study optimization, 1-page ATS resume typesetting, mock interview defense sessions, and access to the Nova Skills placement network for junior graphic design roles.
