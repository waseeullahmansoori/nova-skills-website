## 1. Product Positioning & Target Job Roles

### 1.1 Program Strategic Positioning
In the 2026–2027 commercial landscape, e-commerce has matured beyond simple digital storefronts into complex, multi-touchpoint transactional operations. In India and worldwide, Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) brands, omnichannel retail businesses, B2B wholesalers, and digital product merchants rely heavily on agile, no-code commerce platforms to launch, iterate, and scale without incurring the prohibitive costs and prolonged release cycles of custom headless software engineering.

The contemporary e-commerce job market requires a hybrid professional: an individual who possesses sophisticated visual design sensibilities (UI/UX, mobile responsiveness, micro-interactions), operational fluency (catalog taxonomy, variant logic, inventory routing, SKU architecture), technical integration competence (payment gateways, GST invoicing, automated shipping, COD fraud prevention), and data-driven optimization skills (conversion rate optimization, GA4 e-commerce tracking, automated email/WhatsApp CRM).

**E-Commerce Website Mastery** is Nova Skills' flagship 6-month, 240-guided-hour professional immersion. It is engineered from first principles to train beginners, career switchers, aspiring freelance store builders, and e-commerce entrepreneurs into high-performing store implementation specialists capable of building, configuring, securing, and maintaining commercial-grade online stores on **Shopify** and **WooCommerce**.

### 1.3 Strict Academic Course Boundaries
To maintain rigorous pedagogical focus and respect adjacent programs within Nova Skills, `ecommerce-mastery` enforces explicit boundary constraints:

- **NO Advanced Custom Programming:** The course strictly excludes raw PHP software development, custom WordPress plugin development from scratch, React/Next.js frontend development, Node.js backend engineering, or Shopify CLI custom Remix app development. (Learners seeking full-stack software development are directed to `fullstack-foundation` or `ai-assisted-full-stack-web-development` in the Programming Academy).
- **NO Headless Commerce Architecture:** Headless setups involving decoupled GraphQL APIs, Next.js Commerce, headless Medusa, or Commercelayer are excluded to avoid code overwhelm.
- **NO Paid Media Ad Buying Execution:** While the course covers product feeds (Google Merchant Center, Meta Catalog) and tracking pixels (GA4, CAPI), it does NOT teach paid media media buying, Meta Ads campaign scaling, or Google Ads bidding strategies. (Learners seeking media buying are directed to `performance-marketing-specialist` or `ecommerce-growth-marketing-specialist` in the Digital Marketing Academy).
- **IN-SCOPE CORE COMPETENCIES:** Complete end-to-end visual store architecture, product taxonomy, Shopify theme customizer mastery, WooCommerce ecosystem, Indian & international payment gateways, statutory GST tax settings, shipping & logistics automation, mobile CRO, automated CRM retention funnels, generative AI creative production, store security, performance tuning, and client handoff.

## 4. Full 12-Module Detailed Curriculum

### Module 01: E-Commerce Strategy, Business Models & Store Architecture
- **Guided Instructional Hours:** 12 Hours
- **Prerequisite Dependencies:** Basic computer literacy and web browsing familiarity.
- **Main Topics:**
  - Modern E-Commerce Landscape (D2C, B2C, B2B, Marketplace vs Independent Store).
  - Business Models: Inventory-Led vs Dropshipping vs Print-on-Demand vs Digital Goods.
  - Platform Architecture Evaluation: Shopify vs WooCommerce vs Magento vs Custom Code.
  - Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Modeling: Subscriptions, hosting, apps, transaction fees, payment processing.
  - Store Hierarchy & User Journey Mapping: From homepage landing to checkout confirmation.
  - Domain Strategy, DNS Routing, SSL Certificates, and Professional Business Email Setup.
- **Detailed Subtopics:**
  - Analyzing real-world Indian D2C success case studies (packaging, branding, unit economics).
  - Merchant unit economics: Gross Margin, Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), Return to Origin (RTO), and Average Order Value (AOV).
  - Mapping platform selection criteria to merchant constraints: budget, technical comfort, customization needs, and SKU volume.
  - Domain configuration: A records, CNAME records, MX records, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC verification for transactional deliverability.
- **Practical Hands-on Lab Exercises:**
  - **Lab 1.1:** Build an E-Commerce Business Feasibility Model and TCO Calculator for a D2C client in Google Sheets.
  - **Lab 1.2:** Configure custom domain DNS records, SSL encryption, and connect a domain to a staging commerce environment.
- **Tools & Software:** Google Sheets (TCO Model), Namecheap/Cloudflare DNS, Shopify Partner Admin.
- **Learning Outcomes:** Students can evaluate client business requirements, recommend the optimal commerce platform with financial justification, and configure domain infrastructure securely.
- **Assessment Criteria:** Complete TCO comparison spreadsheet and DNS diagnostic audit with 100% routing accuracy.

### Module 02: Product Catalogs, Information Architecture & Inventory Operations
- **Guided Instructional Hours:** 20 Hours
- **Prerequisite Dependencies:** Module 01 (Store Architecture).
- **Main Topics:**
  - Product Catalog Information Architecture: Taxonomies, Categories, Tags, and Collections.
  - SKU (Stock Keeping Unit) Naming Conventions & Barcode Standards (EAN/UPC).
  - Simple vs Complex Products: Options, Variations, Multi-Dimensional Attributes (Size, Color, Material).
  - Digital vs Physical Products: Downloadable limits, file delivery protocols, license keys.
  - Inventory Operations: Safety stock, stock thresholds, backorder policies, and SKU tracking.
  - Bulk Product Data Migration: Formatting, cleaning, and importing/exporting CSV and Excel catalog feeds.
- **Detailed Subtopics:**
  - Multi-level category trees: Breadcrumbs, nested collections, and faceted navigation logic.
  - Designing scalable variant matrices without breaking platform limits (Shopify 100-variant limit vs WooCommerce attribute combinations).
  - Inventory tracking across multiple fulfillment locations (Warehouse vs Retail Store).
  - Bulk image URL hosting and automated image linking during CSV catalog imports.
  - Product status lifecycles: Draft, Active, Archived, Out of Stock, Pre-Order.
- **Practical Hands-on Lab Exercises:**
  - **Lab 2.1:** Design a standardized 50-SKU master product catalog in Excel with attributes, tags, HSN codes, and image URLs.
  - **Lab 2.2:** Execute a zero-error bulk CSV catalog import into a clean store database with multi-variant options and collections.
- **Tools & Software:** Microsoft Excel / Google Sheets, CSV Validators, ImgBB/Cloudinary (asset hosting).
- **Learning Outcomes:** Students master catalog data structures, build structured variant matrices, and execute clean enterprise-grade catalog migrations.
- **Assessment Criteria:** Flawless import of a 50-product multi-variant catalog with verified pricing, stock levels, and collection sorting.
