## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Positioning

### 1.1 Course Mission
The **Career Launch & Placement Readiness Professional** program is an intensive 3-month (120 guided hours) career acceleration and employment readiness specialization. It bridges the critical divide between technical competence and corporate employability.

Modern technical graduates and career changers possess foundational domain tools (e.g. Figma, React, Premiere Pro, Python, or SEO software), yet frequently struggle to secure employment due to:
1. **Generic, Unoptimized Resumes:** Submitting text-heavy, keyword-deficient CVs that fail Applicant Tracking System (ATS) parsing and recruiter screening scans.
2. **Invisible or Passive LinkedIn Presence:** Lacking search-optimized profiles, professional positioning, and structured networking strategies.
3. **Weak, Context-Free Portfolios:** Presenting isolated graphics, code snippets, or video clips rather than commercial Problem-Action-Result (PAR) case studies that demonstrate measurable business impact.
4. **Behavioral & Communication Deficiencies:** Struggling with structured behavioral interview frameworks (e.g., STAR methodology), cross-cultural corporate communication, and technical portfolio defense.
5. **Inability to Pass Employer Screening Assessments:** Stumbling on pre-interview screening tasks, creative tests, code challenges, or aptitude rounds under timed corporate hiring conditions.

This program provides structured, faculty-guided training that equips learners with the tools, operational workflows, and verified artifacts required to navigate competitive hiring pipelines across startups, agencies, enterprise corporations, and remote employers.

The **Career & Freelancing Academy** (`career`) contains two distinct flagship offerings:

1. **Career Launch & Placement Readiness Professional (`career-launch-placement-readiness-professional`):**
   - **Focus:** Employment readiness, corporate/startup/agency recruitment, resume engineering, LinkedIn branding, technical hiring assessments, behavioral interviews, salary negotiation, and workplace readiness.

## 4. 2026–2027 Industry and Platform Research Benchmark

### 4.1 Entry-Level Hiring Ecosystem
Modern corporate and startup recruitment across India and international remote employers operates across four distinct organizational tiers:

1. **High-Growth Startups & Scaleups:**
   - Prioritize velocity, autonomous execution, and multi-disciplinary versatility.
   - Hiring process: Initial screening -> Practical take-home technical challenge -> Founder/Lead culture fit interview.
   - Decision factor: Tangible proof of work (live GitHub repos, Figma prototypes, published Reels/videos) over formal credentials.

2. **Creative & Digital Agencies:**
   - Value fast turnaround, client empathy, and adherence to brand design guidelines.
   - Hiring process: Creative portfolio review -> Timed design/video/ad test -> Cross-functional interview.
   - Decision factor: Visual craft excellence, speed under deadline pressure, and presentation confidence.

3. **Corporate Enterprises & MNCs:**
   - Rely heavily on structured recruitment funnels, automated ATS screening, and strict competency benchmarks.
   - Hiring process: ATS resume scan -> Online aptitude/screening test -> Technical panel interview -> HR behavioral evaluation.
   - Decision factor: Quantifiable problem-solving methodology, structural communication (STAR format), and cultural reliability.

4. **Remote & Global Distributed Teams:**
   - Demand exceptional asynchronous communication, self-management, and proactive documentation.
   - Hiring process: Asynchronous video intro (Loom) -> Paid trial project -> Video interview.
   - Decision factor: Written clarity, time-zone overlap management, and independent problem-solving.

### 4.2 Role-Specific Technical Hiring Benchmarks
Module 09 introduces 8 specialized technical evaluation tracks aligned with Nova Skills academies:

1. **Graphic Design:** Branding guidelines execution, vector typography, multi-asset marketing collateral packaging.
2. **UI/UX Design:** User flow mapping, high-fidelity interactive prototyping in Figma, component design system teardowns.
3. **Video Editing & Motion Graphics:** Narrative pacing, commercial reel pacing, audio sweetening, kinetic typography in Premiere/After Effects.
4. **Generative AI Video Production:** Prompt synthesis, cinematic coherence, multi-shot commercial continuity, post-generation finishing.
5. **Digital Marketing:** Full-funnel campaign architecture, Meta/Google ad account structure, ROAS/CAC analysis, technical SEO audit.
6. **Web Development:** Modern responsive front-end application delivery (HTML5, modern CSS, JavaScript/React), API integration, clean code standards.
7. **Python Development:** Data structure manipulation, REST API endpoint construction, script automation, algorithmic problem-solving.
8. **AI Automation:** Webhook data routing (Make / n8n), CRM payload mapping, automated lead triage with failure fallback logging.

### 4.3 Modern Recruitment Platforms & ATS Truths
- **LinkedIn Jobs & LinkedIn Job Tracker:** Utilizing boolean search operators (`AND`, `OR`, `NOT`, title filters), tracking active application states, and engaging with recruiters via mutual second-degree connections.
- **Naukri & Indeed India:** Keyword frequency optimization for domestic recruitment search algorithms, headline clarity, and fresh profile update routines.
- **National Career Service (NCS) & Enterprise Portals:** Navigating formal public-sector and corporate career portals (Workday, SuccessFactors, Taleo).
- **The ATS Reality Check:**
  > [!NOTE]
  > **ATS Educational Fact:** An ATS (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever) is a database management tool, not an artificial intelligence that arbitrarily rejects applicants. Resumes fail parsing due to complex multi-column tables, graphics, non-standard headers, and missing job-description keywords. **A high ATS score does not guarantee an interview**, but standard, clean formatting ensures human recruiters actually see your qualifications.

### 4.4 AI Career Acceleration Tools
- **ChatGPT, Claude & Gemini:** Supervised prompt engineering for resume bullet point refinement (Google X-Y-Z formula: *"Accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]"*), mock behavioral question simulation, and company pre-interview intelligence research.
- **Loom:** Recording 60-to-90 second asynchronous video introductions that accompany cold outreach and portfolio submissions.
- **Google Meet & Zoom:** Replicating corporate video interview environments with audio checks, lighting, and screen-share presentation protocols.

### MONTH 1: FOUNDATIONS & PROFESSIONAL IDENTITY (40 HOURS)

#### MODULE 01: CAREER DISCOVERY & JOB-MARKET INTELLIGENCE

- **Tools:** LinkedIn Jobs, Naukri, Indeed, Google Sheets, Industry Research Reports.
- **Main Topics & Detailed Subtopics:**
  1. *The 2026–2027 Employment Landscape:* Tech, creative, and digital marketing hiring trends; startup vs agency vs corporate vs remote employer profiles; contract vs full-time career trajectories.
  2. *Job-Market Research Methodologies:* Deconstructing live job descriptions; extracting primary technical competencies and secondary soft skills; tracking hiring seasonality.
  3. *Target Employer Ecosystem Mapping:* Tier 1 (Dream corporate/enterprise), Tier 2 (High-growth scaleups/agencies), and Tier 3 (Entry-level stepping stone) categorization.
  4. *Salary & Compensation Benchmarking:* Researching realistic entry-level and junior compensation bands across Indian metro regions and global remote markets; understanding CTC components (Basic, HRA, Allowances, ESOPs).
- **Instructor Demonstration:** Live deconstruction of three disparate job descriptions (Startup, Agency, Enterprise MNC); compiling a market competency demand matrix in Google Sheets.
- **Classroom Practical:** Students extract competencies from 10 active job postings in their specialization and construct an Employer Target List of 20 companies.
- **Independent Exercise:** Complete an in-depth Compensation & Industry Demand Report for the learner's chosen career discipline.
- **Assessable Deliverables:** Target Employer Ecosystem Matrix and Job-Market Competency Dossier.
- **Assessment Rubrics:** Evaluated on research empirical rigor (40%), role competency extraction accuracy (35%), and employer categorization realism (25%).
- **Skill Prerequisites:** Basic computer literacy and web research proficiency.
- **Privacy & Ethics:** Use public job boards ethically; zero scraping of private employee records.

#### MODULE 02: SKILLS-GAP ASSESSMENT & INDIVIDUAL CAREER ROADMAP

- **Tools:** Google Sheets, Notion, Competency Diagnostic Rubrics.
- **Main Topics & Detailed Subtopics:**
  1. *Objective Self-Assessment:* Auditing hard technical tools, project complexity, and production speed against junior/mid-level industry benchmarks.
  2. *The Skills-Gap Matrix:* Identifying critical technical deficiencies, missing portfolio project types, and professional communication gaps.
  3. *Individual Career Roadmap Construction:* Establishing 30-, 60-, and 90-day learning and application milestones with quantitative KPIs.
  4. *Career Track Confirmation:* Finalizing the student's primary target role (e.g. Junior UI/UX Designer, Front-End Developer, Motion Graphic Artist) and secondary backup specialization.
- **Instructor Demonstration:** Conducting a live diagnostic skills-gap teardown of a sample candidate profile, highlighting blind spots and structuring a remedial 90-day action plan.
- **Classroom Practical:** Students complete their diagnostic self-evaluation rubric and plot their personal Skills-Gap Analysis Matrix.
- **Independent Exercise:** Draft the Individual Career Development Plan detailing weekly hours, skill-building milestones, and target application dates.
- **Assessable Deliverables:** Completed Skills-Gap Analysis Matrix and Individual Career Development Plan.
- **Assessment Rubrics:** Evaluated on diagnostic honesty and depth (40%), clarity of gap identification (35%), and action plan feasibility (25%).
- **Skill Prerequisites:** Knowledge of student's own technical work and projects.
- **Privacy & Ethics:** Honest appraisal of skill levels without unearned self-inflation.

- **Assessable Deliverables:** Master Resume, Two Tailored 1-Page Resumes (PDF & Word), and Two Cover Letter Templates.
- **Assessment Rubrics:** Evaluated on ATS format compliance (30%), quantifiable impact phrasing (35%), tailoring relevance (20%), and language polish (15%).
- **Skill Prerequisites:** Document editing skills in Google Docs or Word.
- **Privacy & Ethics:** 100% truthfulness in education, employment dates, and skill claims; zero fabricated credentials.
