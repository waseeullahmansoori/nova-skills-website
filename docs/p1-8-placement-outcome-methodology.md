## 1. Objective & Ethical Principles

This methodology governs how student career transitions, employment outcomes, and alumni achievements are verified, tracked, calculated, and reported across all Nova Skills publications.

Under Section 4 and Section 10 of the P1-8 mandate:
- **No Conflation:** A course completion is not an interview; an interview is not an offer; a freelance project is not a salaried employment placement.
- **Auditable Denominators:** Any public placement statistic or percentage must be calculated using a defined denominator, reporting cohort, and verification date.
- **Salary Integrity:** Irrelevant freelance earnings, one-off contest prizes, or hypothetical target CTCs must never be represented as guaranteed or average placement salaries.
- **Privacy & Consent:** Student data is protected in full compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

## 2. The 12-Stage Student Career Progression Funnel

Every student trajectory is tracked through distinct, sequential milestones. Stages cannot be combined or assumed:

```
[Stage 1: Enrolled in Program]
         │
[Stage 2: Completed Curriculum Modules]
         │
[Stage 3: Completed Mandatory Capstone Projects]
         │
[Stage 4: Satisfied Eligibility & Verified Placement-Eligible] ─── (CRITICAL GATE)
         │
[Stage 5: Resume & Portfolio Formatted & Approved]
         │
[Stage 6: Mock Technical & HR Interview Completed]
         │
[Stage 7: Candidate Profile Forwarded to Employer Opening]
         │
[Stage 8: Employer Shortlist & Interview Scheduled]
         │
[Stage 9: Interview Attended by Candidate]
         │
[Stage 10: Formal Employment Offer Received]
         │
[Stage 11: Employment Offer Formally Accepted]
         │
[Stage 12: Employment Commenced (Verification of Joining)]
```

## 3. Strict Operational Data Definitions

### A. Students Trained
- **Definition:** Learners who have attended at least 80% of scheduled class sessions and successfully completed the academic modules of their enrolled course.
- **Excluded:** Individuals who submitted inquiries, attended free demos, or dropped out prior to course midpoint.

### B. Placement-Eligible Students (The Denominator)
- **Definition:** Course completers enrolled in a Tier A or Tier B program who have satisfied **all six published placement eligibility conditions** (100% module completion, approved capstones, passing assessment grade, approved ATS resume, approved portfolio, and mock interview completion).
- **Rule:** This group constitutes the **sole legitimate denominator** for any future published placement rate calculations.

### C. Students Referred
- **Definition:** Placement-eligible students whose profiles, resumes, and portfolios were formally forwarded to an employer with an active vacancy (with explicit student consent).
- **Rule:** A referral is NOT a placement.

### D. Students Interviewed
- **Definition:** Students who participated in a confirmed technical or HR interview with an employer.
- **Rule:** An interview opportunity is NOT a placement.

### E. Students Placed
- **Definition:** Students who have formally received, accepted, and commenced employment in a verifiable role (documented via offer letter, employment agreement, or joining confirmation).

## 4. Placement Statistics & Rate Calculation Rules

### Formula for Published Placement Rate
If Nova Skills publishes a formal placement rate for an academic year or cohort, it must strictly adhere to the following formula:

$$\text{Placement Rate (\%)} = \frac{\text{Number of Verified Placed Students in Cohort}}{\text{Total Placement-Eligible Students in Cohort}} \times 100$$

### Rules Governing Denominators:
1. **No Inquiry Inflation:** Never calculate placement percentage against total leads, inquiries, or demo attendees.
2. **Defined Timeframe:** The reporting window must be clearly stated (e.g., *"For the 2025–2026 Academic Cohort, measured 180 days post-course completion"*).
3. **Cohort Isolation:** Rates must not aggregate short certification students who never requested or qualified for placement support.
4. **Current Status:** If an audited, statistically significant cohort calculation is pending or unverified, **Nova Skills will suppress numerical percentage claims** and publish verified qualitative metrics instead.

## 5. Outcome Classification (Zero Conflation Standard)

Student career transitions must be categorized into one of six distinct buckets:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        CAREER OUTCOME TAXONOMY                         │
├────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Full-Time Salaried Role  │ Verifiable employment offer with annual   │
│                            │ CTC at an external company.               │
├────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. Paid Internship         │ Time-bound paid training or apprenticeship│
│                            │ with stipend (Must NOT be called full CTC)│
├────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. Freelancing & Contracts │ Independent client projects or gig work   │
│                            │ (Reported as client billings, NOT salary) │
├────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. Entrepreneurship        │ Launching an agency, e-commerce store,    │
│                            │ or business (Reported as founder launch)  │
├────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 5. Career Advancement      │ Existing working professionals promoted   │
│                            │ or receiving appraisal in current firm    │
├────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 6. Further Studies         │ Students pursuing degree or higher certs  │
└────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Prohibited Conflations:
- **Prohibition 1:** Never count a freelancing client contract (e.g. ₹15,000 logo design project) as a salaried job placement.
- **Prohibition 2:** Never count a 2-month internship stipend (e.g. ₹10,000/mo) as an annualized full-time CTC of ₹1.2 LPA.
- **Prohibition 3:** Never count a student launching their own startup or e-commerce store as a placement by Nova Skills.

## 6. Compensation & Salary Reporting Standards

If salary figures are referenced in career guidance materials:
1. **Distinguish Industry Norms from Guarantees:** Salary ranges must be clearly identified as *"Typical industry entry-level benchmarks"* rather than guaranteed student outcomes.
2. **Compensation Breakdown:** Clearly specify whether figures represent Fixed Base Salary, Annual CTC, or Hourly Freelance Billings.
3. **Median vs. Average:** Where cohort data is reported, both Median and Mean CTC must be disclosed to prevent skew from outlier offers.
4. **Scrubbing Hardcoded Fallbacks:** Eliminate unverified fallback values (such as `'₹4.5 – ₹9.0 LPA'`) in website widgets and AI algorithms.

---

## 7. Privacy, Consent & DPDP Act 2023 Compliance

1. **Offer Letter Redaction:** Actual employer offer letters, compensation slips, and employee identification numbers are confidential business records and will never be published publicly on the website.
2. **Student Identity Consent:** Any public alumni attribution (such as testimonial or placement case study) must have an active signed consent record (`namePublicationApproved`, `quotePublicationApproved`, `rolePublicationApproved`).
3. **Non-Attributed Case Studies:** Where a student requests confidentiality, outcomes may be published as anonymized composites (e.g., *"Graduate from Batch 2025-Q3, Placed as Junior Full-Stack Developer at a Mumbai SaaS Startup"*).
4. **Minor Protection:** Minors (Kids Tech Academy) are completely excluded from placement tracking, resume dissemination, or employer referral.
