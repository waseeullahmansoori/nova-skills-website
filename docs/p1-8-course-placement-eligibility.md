## 1. Objective

This framework establishes clear, program-specific eligibility rules and service boundaries for placement support at Nova Skills Education Institute.

Nova Skills offers 109 distinct courses across 12 academies, spanning durations from 3 weeks to 9 months. It is operationally and ethically misleading to advertise identical "placement assistance" across a 3-week introductory software module and an 8-month intensive engineering career program.

This document codifies:
1. The 4-Tier Program Classification across all 109 courses.
2. The exact Career Services included, conditional, or unavailable for each tier.
3. The mandatory Student Eligibility Requirements that must be satisfied before placement referral activation.
4. The statutory No-Job-Guarantee legal protections.

**Duration:** 3 to 8 Weeks  
**Tuition Range:** ₹2,900 to ₹9,900  
**Target:** Skill-Specific Upskillers, Students Adding a Single Tool or Workflow  
**Placement Level:** **Practical Portfolio & Capstone Support** (No corporate placement referrals)

### Scope & Description
- This tier encompasses all modular 3-to-8 week certifications (e.g., `adobe-photoshop`, `react-cert`, `meta-ads`, `google-ads`, `prompt-engineering`, `blender-cert`, `notion-cert`, `microsoft-excel`, etc.).
- **What is Provided:**
  - Verified Course Completion Certificate with QR verification.
  - Real project briefs completed for student portfolio usage.
  - Mentor feedback on capstone assignments.
  - General career guidance on how to position this skill on a CV/LinkedIn.
- **What is EXCLUDED (Unavailable):**
  - No dedicated placement cell representation.
  - No individual recruiter forwarding or direct interview scheduling.
  - No ongoing job search monitoring.

**Duration:** 4 Weeks to 8 Months

- All Kids Certifications (`scratch-programming`, `python-for-kids`, `ai-for-kids`, `web-design-for-kids`, `robotics-basics`).

### Policy & Minor Protection:
- Under Indian labor laws and Nova Skills' Child Safety Policy, minor learners are never subject to employment preparation, placement marketing, or corporate referral.
- Instead, students receive:
  - Junior Project Showcase (interactive games, Scratch animations, Python mini-apps).
  - Young Innovator Certificate of Completion.
  - Creative problem-solving and computational literacy milestones.

## 7. Mandatory Student Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for active placement referral drives under Tier A and Tier B, students must satisfy all six mandatory criteria:

1. **Curriculum Completion & Attendance:**
   - 100% completion of all core curriculum modules.
   - Minimum 80% attendance in live lectures and mentor labs (or verifiable completion of recorded modules with code submissions).
2. **Capstone & Portfolio Verification:**
   - Satisfactory submission of all mandatory capstone projects.
   - All projects must be hosted live with clean source code (GitHub) or high-resolution design case studies (Behance/portfolio site). Plagiarized or AI-generated projects without demonstrated student understanding are rejected.
3. **Academic Assessment & Viva Defense:**
   - Achieving a score of $\ge 70\%$ on the end-of-course practical assessment.
   - Successful defense in a mentor viva voce covering technical choices, business logic, and code/design architecture.
4. **Recruiter-Ready Career Assets:**
   - Completion and mentor approval of an ATS-compliant one-page resume.
   - Active, professional LinkedIn profile incorporating verified Nova Skills coursework and project links.
5. **Interview Readiness:**
   - Attendance and satisfactory completion of at least one formal 1-on-1 mock interview.
6. **Professional Conduct:**
   - Adherence to professional communication standards, prompt response to scheduled interviews, and truthful representation of academic and professional background.

## 8. Non-Guarantee Legal Protections

Every course page, placement brochure, and admissions interaction must clearly disclose:
1. **No Job Guarantee:** Enrollment in any Nova Skills course does not guarantee employment, a job offer, an interview call, or a specific salary package.
2. **Selection Autonomy:** All hiring, shortlisting, interviewing, and remuneration decisions rest solely with independent employers.
3. **Outcome Dependency:** Career outcomes depend on individual student competence, communication skills, portfolio quality, market demand, and employer criteria.
