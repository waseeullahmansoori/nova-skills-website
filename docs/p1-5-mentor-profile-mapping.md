## 1. Overview & Discipline-Based Faculty Structure

Nova Skills organizes faculty and mentorship through specialized academic discipline teams. Instead of inventing fictional individual names, degrees, or AI-generated portraits, Nova Skills provides transparent domain leads and practical project critique teams for each of its 12 academies.

## 3. Secondary Project Mentorship

In addition to the Academy Domain Lead, every course features a dedicated **Hands-On Lab & Portfolio Review Mentor** tailored to the discipline:
- **Digital Marketing:** Campaign Lab & Ad Budget Reviewer
- **AI & Automation:** AI Engineering & Workflow Lab Mentor
- **Design:** Design Critique & Asset Review Mentor
- **Programming:** Full Stack Code Review & Architecture Mentor
- **No-Code Web:** No-Code E-Commerce & CMS Reviewer
- **Video & Motion:** Video Editing & Timeline Critique Mentor
- **3D:** 3D Render & Asset Topology Reviewer
- **Career & Freelancing:** Proposal & Client Pitch Reviewer
- **Communication:** Executive Voice & Speech Critique Coach
- **Kids Tech:** Youth Computing & Gamified Lab Mentor
- **Creator:** Channel Analytics & Thumbnail Reviewer
- **Office Productivity:** Spreadsheet Model & Dashboard Reviewer

This replaces the previous repetitive, uninformative "Senior Project Trainer" (PT) with specific, credible, and context-appropriate laboratory mentorship across all 109 courses.
