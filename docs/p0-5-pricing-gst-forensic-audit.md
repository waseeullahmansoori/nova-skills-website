## 1. Executive Summary & Root Cause Analysis

### Forensic Finding
Prior to this P0-5 audit, the Nova Skills website contained a critical sitewide contradiction between course-detail hero pricing components and FAQ fee answers:
- **Course-Detail Hero Sidebar (all 109 courses):** Displayed `💳 Flexible monthly installment options available · 18% GST included`.
- **Course-Detail FAQs (69 published courses):** Described `statutory 18% GST extra` (or `plus applicable GST`) and performed mathematical additions calculating additional tax on top of the advertised launch fee.

For example, for `ai-mastery`, the hero advertised a launch fee of ₹64,900 with "18% GST included", while the FAQ calculated:
$$\text{Regular Tuition: } ₹89,900 \quad | \quad \text{Launch Tuition: } ₹64,900 \quad | \quad \text{18% GST: } ₹11,682 \quad | \quad \text{Total Payable: } ₹76,582$$

This created conflicting expectations about whether students must pay ₹64,900 or ₹76,582.

### Root Cause Analysis
1. **Absence of Unified Commercial Source:** A historical marketing push in Phase 4 hardcoded `18% GST included` into `course-detail.html:136`, `course-detail.js:375`, and `scripts/build-course-pages.js:378` as an aspirational conversion badge.
2. **Disjoint Curriculum Generation:** Simultaneously, academic curriculum templates generated FAQ blocks with explicit tax additions stating `(statutory 18% GST extra)` and computing `launch fee + 18% GST = total payable`.
3. **Absence of Online Payment Gateway:** A repository-level search confirmed that Nova Skills does **NOT** operate an automated online payment gateway (no live Razorpay, Stripe, or Cashfree credentials). All candidate admissions are counsellor-facilitated via lead inquiry modals.
4. **Institutional Tax Policy Awaiting Signoff:** Because no signed finance fee master or statutory billing invoice rules exist in the repository, neither "inclusive" nor "exclusive" could be legally or commercially verified as the permanent corporate policy.

### Authoritative Policy & Interim Solution
Per P0-5 specification Section 4:
> *"If the approved tax treatment cannot be established, do not invent it. For any unresolved course, do not independently decide inclusive versus exclusive, do not retain contradictory definitive statements, do not display an unsupported numerical GST amount or total payable, preserve the existing advertised course and launch prices, and replace conflicting tax statements with a neutral disclosure:*
> ***'The final payable amount, including any applicable taxes, will be clearly displayed before payment.'***
